Each entry is one release, live for every workspace. Subscribe to the [RSS feed](/changelog/rss.xml) to get new entries as they ship.

Entries are grouped by area: **Resolve** (incidents), **Review** (code review), **Cyber** (security testing), **Optimize** (cloud cost), and **Approvals** (which agent writes run). Role names follow [Roles](/guide/security/roles), and the [Glossary](/guide/glossary) defines every other term.

<Update label="October 20, 2026" description="v0.24.0" tags={["Approvals", "Chat", "Memory", "Review", "Cyber", "Optimize", "Resolve", "Connections", "Audit logs"]}>
  <Note>
  **Check after this release.** Only organization Owners and Admins can read [audit logs](/guide/security/audit-logs) now. Automations, webhooks, and incident investigations can now wait for approval, so make sure your [approvers](/guide/approvals/approvers) watch the notification bell.
  </Note>

  **Rules decide agent writes**

  Rules now decide each [write](/guide/glossary#w), meaning any agent action that changes your systems through a connection. When no Rule covers a write, your [Approval mode](/guide/approvals/approval-mode) decides: Manual waits for a person, and only Auto with Rules + AI lets AI judgment allow, ask, or refuse. [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview), formerly Auto Mode, now has its own page in the sidebar where you pick the mode.

  - **Approvals:** Agents propose Rules, and **Find rules** drafts them for your workspace. Set [approver](/guide/approvals/approvers) groups with a two-person rule, give time-boxed grants, and dry-run [Rules](/guide/approvals/rules) against past history.
  - **Chat:** [Side chat](/guide/commands) (`/btw`) asks a quick side question in a fork next to a running chat. Shared chats show who is present and typing. **Stop** halts the running reply at once, and long chats load older messages about 3 times faster.
  - **Memory:** A nightly [Dreaming](/guide/memory-dreaming) run tidies workspace [memory](/guide/memory) and custom skills. Workspace Admins can run Dreaming, review its work, and edit items in workspace **Settings → Memory**.
  - **Review:** A review posts a few focused findings that stay stable between pushes. A push or deploy no longer stops a running review, and the Review page now opens on the revamped Overview.
  - **Cyber:** Resume an interrupted [pentest](/guide/security/pentest). A cloud pentest waits in the queue instead of failing.
  - **Optimize:** Developers and above can edit [findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations) and see each change in Activity. Workspace Admins get one [monthly digest](/guide/cost-optimization/anomalies) instead of an email after every scan.
  - **Resolve:** A flapping alarm that a person closes as a false alarm stops looping. [Root cause analysis](/guide/incident/root-cause-analysis) reports say how to check each claim again.
  - **Connections:** A failed connection explains the problem in plain words and gives a next step. [Drata](/guide/connections/drata) is a new connection.

  **Fixes and improvements**

  - Optimize shows savings per month again, not a value about 12 times too high.
  - Scheduled tasks, webhooks, and incident investigations wait for your approval instead of declining the action, when their Approval mode asks a person. The request shows in the [notification](/guide/notifications) bell.
  - Triage in a workspace with no credits waits and sends admins one notice a day.
  - Only organization Owners and Admins can read audit logs, and share links check permission.
  - A turn that pauses for approval is charged once.
  - [PostgreSQL](/guide/connections/postgresql) connections start again.
</Update>

<Update label="October 11, 2026" description="v0.23.0" tags={["MCP", "Resolve", "Review", "Cyber", "Chat", "Google Chat", "Automations", "Optimize", "Connections"]}>
  **Connect Claude to your workspace**

  Connect Claude (claude.ai, the Claude desktop app, or Claude Code) to CloudThinker in one consent step through the [Claude Connector](/guide/mcp/overview). Claude can read your incidents, Optimize, Cyber, Review, and automations, and can start agent runs that follow your Approval mode. Revoke a connection in **Account settings → Connected apps**.

  - **Resolve:** The triage agent decides which incident an alert joins and whether to page. An unconfirmed signal opens a quiet investigation first, and an alert storm feeds one triage run. Download the [root cause analysis](/guide/incident/root-cause-analysis) as a PDF.
  - **Review:** Thread closes, scorecards, and notes survive a Git provider outage. A [Layers](/guide/code-review/review-workspace#read-a-review) tab walks through a merge request in named steps, and a shorter scorecard decides the merge.
  - **Cyber:** [Pentest](/guide/security/pentest) creates its own test accounts and signs in with TOTP. [Findings](/guide/security/cyber-findings) open on **To do**, **In progress**, **Fixed**, and **All** tabs, sync to your issue tracker, and export as a report.
  - **Chat:** Steer, which sends your queued message into a running reply, takes effect the moment you press it. Replies arrive one finished block at a time. Search conversations with filters, and message a running subagent from Agents.
  - **Google Chat:** [Google Chat](/guide/google-chat-integration) adds a guided install, per-space health, link previews, approve and reject buttons, and live progress.
  - **Automations:** A probe trigger runs a cheap check and starts a run only when the check fires. An [automation](/guide/automation/automations) can continue one conversation.
  - **Optimize:** [Cost Health](/guide/cost-optimization/overview#key-concepts) leads with what raises your score. Cost Health and Findings download as PDF, and Azure shows the full Cost Health view.
  - **Connections:** [Wazuh](/guide/connections/wazuh) Cloud and self-hosted share one connection and support several instances. [FPT Cloud](/guide/connections/fptcloud) agents manage volumes, snapshots, and power schedules.

  **Fixes and improvements**

  - [Pulse](/guide/pulse/overview) keeps unrelated alerts apart, and forwarded log lines no longer open incidents on their own.
  - Webhook owners can set their own rate limit. A burst of alerts gets a clear retry answer instead of an error.
  - The Auto [Approval mode](/guide/approvals/approval-mode) asks for approval less often. Approving a command after an investigation continues the chat.
  - Scheduled [Keeper](/guide/glossary#former-names) runs start again, and older automations can be edited again.
  - [Elasticsearch](/guide/connections/elasticsearch) connections accept a username and password, and self-signed HTTPS.
</Update>

<Update label="September 30, 2026" description="v0.22.0" tags={["Sandboxes", "Optimize", "Review", "Cyber", "Resolve", "Chat", "Notifications", "Desktop", "Connections"]}>
  **Manage your workspace sandboxes**

  Open a sandbox from the workspace header to see its activity and settings. A crashed or stuck sandbox is replaced automatically, so your workspace keeps working.

  - **Optimize:** Cost Health now includes Reserved Instance and Savings Plan commitments. The weekly digest leads with spend, savings, and the top action. Search and filter [Optimize findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations).
  - **Review:** Merge an MR or PR from the Review page as yourself. Each MR row shows its diff size.
  - **Cyber:** Reverify a [pentest](/guide/security/pentest) finding in one click. The pentest agent can log in to your app with saved test credentials.
  - **Resolve:** The [Incident Room](/guide/incident/overview) opens with a short brief. The root cause analysis is shorter and states the cause once.
  - **Chat:** Agents can read your earlier conversations and uploaded files. The PDF preview adds zoom, page jump, and search.
  - **Notifications:** The bell updates in about one second and shows approvals as they arrive. The inbox has filters and day sections. Automation owners hear about failed runs on every channel. See [notifications](/guide/notifications).
  - **Desktop:** The Desktop app updates itself and ships as a Linux `.deb` package.
  - **Connections:** [FPT Cloud](/guide/connections/fptcloud) answers questions across your whole tenant without a VPC. [GitLab](/guide/connections/gitlab) token errors explain the cause.

  **Fixes and improvements**

  - [Pulse](/guide/pulse/overview) Memory saves cluster memory again, and clusters no longer show as unavailable after a Pulse run.
  - [Keepers](/guide/glossary#former-names) pause in a workspace with no credits and resume when credits return.
  - The Azure idle load balancer, idle VPN gateway, empty Application Gateway, and GCP Artifact Registry checks work again.
  - When a log doesn't record the field you ask about, the agent says it can't tell from that source instead of answering "none".
  - Long background commands run to their deadline instead of stopping after 2 minutes.
</Update>

<Update label="September 27, 2026" description="v0.21.0" tags={["Workers", "Chat", "BYOK", "Review", "Resolve", "Rooms", "Optimize", "Cyber", "Guardrails", "Notifications", "Connections"]}>
  **Run agents on your own machine**

  Run a chat on a machine you control with a [Worker](/guide/workers), a self-hosted sandbox. The agent works in the one folder you name and reads its `AGENTS.md` from the first turn. Only you can use a personal Worker, while a shared Worker is open to everyone in the workspace. Workers are in beta.

  - **Chat:** Switching chats takes under 1 second instead of 2.4 seconds, and streamed replies no longer stutter. Charts zoom, expand, and export.
  - **BYOK:** The organization Owner can pick the model for each part of CloudThinker on your own Amazon Bedrock or Anthropic key. An expired key falls back to the platform model instead of ending the reply. See [BYOK](/guide/byok).
  - **Review:** Each MR gets one notification row. A [Pipelines](/guide/code-review/pipelines) page lists your pipeline runs. Findings on unchanged GitLab lines now post.
  - **Resolve:** Ask CloudThinker in the Incident Room. Slack and the Room share one root cause analysis. Incident updates on Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Google Chat say what happened, what was found, and what to do next.
  - **Rooms:** Rooms add pins, visible edits, and a jump to any date.
  - **Optimize:** Ask CloudThinker from Cost Explorer, with **Ask why** on each service. Run a finished full-account scan again from the Overview. Scans now check every billed service by its cost driver.
  - **Cyber:** A pentest finding shows the exploited API request and response, in the app and in the PDF report.
  - **Guardrails:** One page holds ten editable [data classes](/guide/security/custom-guardrails#what-agents-see) with an organization baseline. A [custom pattern](/guide/security/custom-guardrails) takes three fields, and fewer IDs and checksums get masked by mistake.
  - **Notifications:** The bell shows notifications from every workspace and organization.
  - **Connections:** [FPT Cloud](/guide/connections/fptcloud) is a new connection. A failed write says whether a retry is safe.

  **Fixes and improvements**

  - A command that asks for several cloud connections runs with the healthy ones and reports the rest, instead of failing.
  - Azure DevOps reviews follow the pull request's latest iteration, and Bitbucket and Azure DevOps review threads resolve correctly.
  - Optimize opens an Azure account in seconds instead of up to three minutes.
  - A long conversation summarizes its earlier messages instead of growing until it hits the model limit.
</Update>

<Update label="September 21, 2026" description="v0.20.0" tags={["Rooms", "Review", "Chat", "Optimize", "Cyber", "Resolve", "Connections"]}>
  **Rooms become your team's shared space**

  The Rooms rail shows your mentions, your Rooms, and the threads you joined. One search field covers every Room, and reactions appear live for everyone.

  - **Review:** [Advanced Review](/guide/code-review/setup#choose-a-review-mode) keeps its findings when a run crashes. A workspace can reorder, turn off, and write its own review summary sections.
  - **Chat:** A new conversation is now the home page, and a conversation reopens on the model it last used. The `/` picker is one ranked list that starts with the commands you run most.
  - **Optimize:** Cost Explorer groups spend by service, region, or account. The chart, breakdown, and CSV export follow your choice.
  - **Cyber:** Cyber follows your uploaded PDF or DOCX template when it writes a [report](/guide/security/cyber-reports).
  - **Resolve:** Connect an incident platform in one step from the [Integrations](/guide/incident/integrations) page.
  - **Connections:** Two new connections: Kong Konnect and Railway. An agent can use several [AWS](/guide/connections/aws) and [GCP](/guide/connections/gcp) connections in one command.

  **Fixes and improvements**

  No notable fixes in this release.
</Update>
