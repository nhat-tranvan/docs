Prove It is a three-day, hands-on workshop series that CloudThinker runs with FCAJ. You give an AI agent safe, read-only access to a cloud environment, use it to investigate what is going on, and then check every claim it makes before you put your name on a report. This page tells you what happens each day, what you need, and what you hand in.

<Note>
**The September 2026 series has ended.** The Day 01 and Day 02 labs are self-serve: see [Do the labs on your own](#do-the-labs-on-your-own). The schedule, rules and registration links below are kept as a record of the event.
</Note>

<Info>
**Use this page with CloudThinker.** Paste the URL of any workshop page into a CloudThinker chat and ask it to explain a section, quiz you, or outline your report. For example: "Quiz me on the rules on this page, then tell me which one my team is most likely to break."
</Info>

## Rules for every day

| Rule | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Read-only** | The agent can look. It can't change anything. Every fix stays a recommendation until a person approves it. |
| **Credentials stay with the owner** | You connect your own account or cluster with a read-only role, and the owner connects it on their own screen. Nobody pastes keys into a chat, a slide or a shared document. |
| **Evidence over lists** | A short report with strong evidence beats a long list of best practices. |
| **Redact before you share** | Remove account IDs, ARNs, endpoints, hostnames and customer data before a report leaves your team. |
| **Own account, extra credit (Day 01)** | At the event, a Day 01 team that ran the task on its own AWS account, under the same read-only rules, got extra credit when the organizers picked teams to present. Day 02 wasn't scored, and Day 03 ran only on the environments provided. |

## Before you arrive

Ten minutes of setup saves the first block of the lab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a free CloudThinker account">
    Sign up at [app.cloudthinker.io](https://app.cloudthinker.io). One person per team is enough to connect the team's account.

    **Success state:** you land on the CloudThinker home screen with a chat box and a left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring your teammates into one workspace">
    An organization Owner or Admin invites each teammate to the organization (**Admin Settings → Organization → Invite Members**), then adds them to the workspace from **Members**. Steps: [Invite your team](/learn/workshops/prove-it/day-01-govern#invite-your-team).

    **Success state:** every teammate appears in the workspace members table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the account you'll connect">
    Day 01 needs an AWS account and Day 02 a Kubernetes cluster. A staging or sandbox environment is best. Adding a connection needs the workspace **Admin** role, so the person who owns the account should be a workspace Admin, or ask one to change their role. See [Workspace roles](/guide/workspace-users#workspace-roles).

    **Success state:** you know which account and cluster you'll use, and the person connecting them can open **More → Workspace settings → Connections**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the day's page">
    Skim [Day 01](/learn/workshops/prove-it/day-01-govern) and [Day 02](/learn/workshops/prove-it/day-02-investigate) before you start. Each page has the exact connect steps, the prompts to start with, and what the report must contain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Bring a laptop and your team. No installs beyond what your own account needs (AWS CloudShell for Day 01, `kubectl` for Day 02).

## Do the labs on your own

You can repeat the Day 01 and Day 02 labs any time, alone or with a team, on your own read-only AWS account and Kubernetes cluster. Follow the **Practice** section of each day's page: the Day 01 lab is planned as 95 minutes and the Day 02 lab as 30. At the event, the labs ran on a demo cloud that CloudThinker operated. That demo is no longer available, so your findings will differ from what teams found on the day.

Each connection stays in your workspace until you remove it. To remove it, open **More → Workspace settings → Connections**, select the service, and click **Disconnect**. That needs the workspace Admin role.
