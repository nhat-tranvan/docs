Prove It is a three-day, hands-on workshop series that CloudThinker runs with FCAJ. You give an AI agent safe, read-only access to a cloud environment, use it to investigate what is going on, and then check every claim it makes before you put your name on a report. This page tells you what happens each day, what you need, and what you hand in.

<Info>
**Use this page with CloudThinker.** Paste the URL of any workshop page into a CloudThinker chat and ask it to explain a section, quiz you, or outline your report. For example: "Quiz me on the rules on this page, then tell me which one my team is most likely to break."
</Info>

## Schedule

| Day | Date | What you do | What you hand in |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Day 01: Govern](/learn/workshops/prove-it/day-01-govern) | Sep 5, 2026 | Decide what an agent may do before it touches a cloud account, then run a read-only review of an AWS demo account. | One optimization report per team |
| [Day 02: Investigate](/learn/workshops/prove-it/day-02-investigate) | Sep 12, 2026 | Investigate a live incident on a Kubernetes demo cluster, test rival explanations, and separate the root cause from what only looks like one. | One incident report per team |
| [Day 03: Prove It Arena](/learn/workshops/prove-it/day-03-prove-it-arena) | Sep 19, 2026 | Investigate an unfamiliar environment and an unexpected incident, then defend your conclusions in front of judges. | Revealed on the day |

Each session runs 2.5 hours in three blocks: **Learn** (the ideas), **Practice** (the lab) and **Share** (teams present). The split changes by day.

| Day | Learn | Practice | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 01 | 35 min | 95 min, with a 10-minute break | 20 min |
| Day 02 | 35 min, plus a 25-minute keynote | 30 min, no break | 20 min |
| Day 03 | Revealed on the day | Revealed on the day | Revealed on the day |

## Location and teams

- **Ho Chi Minh City** runs in person. **Hanoi and Da Nang** run online.
- **Teams have 1 to 4 members.** No team yet? Register on your own and the organizers will match you with one.
- **To enter the Prove It Arena** you must attend at least one of the first two days.

## Rules for every day

| Rule | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Read-only** | The agent can look. It can't change anything. Every fix stays a recommendation until a person approves it. |
| **No credentials** | The lab runs on a demo cloud that CloudThinker operates. You click **Use demo** and there is nothing to fill in. You don't need an AWS account or keys. |
| **Evidence over lists** | A short report with strong evidence beats a long list of best practices. |
| **Redact before you share** | Remove account IDs, ARNs and any customer data before a report leaves your team. |
| **Own account, extra credit** | A team may run the same task on its own cloud account, under the same read-only rules. A real account scores higher than the demo. |

## Before you arrive

Ten minutes of setup saves the first block of the lab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a free CloudThinker account">
    Sign up at [app.cloudthinker.io](https://app.cloudthinker.io). One person per team is enough to connect the demo; everyone else joins that person's workspace.

    **Success state:** you land on the CloudThinker home screen with a chat box and a left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that you can start a demo">
    In the left sidebar, find the **Try a demo environment** card ("A read-only cloud we run"). If the card says you need connector edit permission, ask your workspace Admin to start the demo or to change your role. See [Workspace Members](/guide/workspace-users).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the day's page">
    Skim [Day 01](/learn/workshops/prove-it/day-01-govern) before Sep 5 and [Day 02](/learn/workshops/prove-it/day-02-investigate) before Sep 12. Each page has the exact connect steps, the prompts to start with, and what the report must contain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Bring a laptop and your team. No installs.

## Do the labs on your own

The demo environment ships in the product, so you can repeat the Day 01 and Day 02 labs any time. Follow the **Practice** section of each day's page. The demo data may have changed since the event, so your findings may not match what teams found on the day.
