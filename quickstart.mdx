This quickstart takes you from sign-in to your first AI investigation: CloudThinker works out a root cause and proposes a fix. Allow **about 10 minutes** — and no cloud credentials if you start with the demo.

<Info>
**What you'll need:** a Google or GitHub account, or any work email. Your own AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, or Kubernetes access is optional — the read-only demo works without it.
</Info>

## The 30-second syntax

<PromptSyntaxBasics />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [app.cloudthinker.io](https://app.cloudthinker.io) and sign in with Google, GitHub, or SSO. To use email instead, enter your address, click **Continue with email**, and type the 6-digit code CloudThinker sends you.

    The first time you sign in, two short questions follow: **Tell us about you** (your role) and, for the organization owner, **How can I help you?** Answer them or click **Skip** — choose **Just exploring** to follow this guide.

    **Success state:** you land on Home, with a chat box and the **Needs you**, **Modules**, and **Automation today** cards. The sidebar shows **New chat**, **Artifacts**, **Automations**, and **Investigation**.

    <Tip>
    A new organization starts on a 14-day Team trial with no card required. See [Pricing](/guide/billing/pricing) for plan limits.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect the demo or your own cloud">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Demo (no credentials)">
        1. Click **Try a demo environment** in the sidebar, or go to **Settings → Connections**.
        2. Pick a service marked **Demo available** — AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, or Kubernetes, whichever is offered.
        3. Click **Connect**, then **Use demo**. Setup takes about a minute.

        The [demo environment](/guide/connections/overview#try-the-demo-environment) is read-only and shared with other people trying it. Demo chats use [credits](/guide/billing/usage) like any other chat.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Your own cloud">
        1. Go to **Settings → Connections** and pick your provider.
        2. Click **Connect** and follow the provider's form. For AWS, choose **Role-Based** (recommended) or **Access Keys**.
        3. Keep the starter permissions read-only. Agents ask for your [approval](/guide/approval) before any change.

        Full walkthroughs: [AWS](/guide/connections/aws), [Azure](/guide/connections/azure), [Google Cloud](/guide/connections/gcp), and [Kubernetes](/guide/connections/kubernetes).
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    **Success state:** the connection shows **Connected**. If it shows **Connection error**, see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    With the demo, CloudThinker opens a chat with an editable starter prompt, such as *Give me an overview of this AWS account.* Send it as is.

    With your own cloud, click **New chat** and send:

    ```text
    Give me an overview of this AWS account: resources by service and region, and anything that looks idle
    ```

    Swap in your provider or cluster name.

    **Success state:** CloudThinker answers with services, regions, and resources you recognize from the account — not generic best practices.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run your first investigation">
    1. Click **Investigation** in the sidebar. If Resolve asks you to connect your first alert source, click **Skip for now** — the next step covers it.
    2. Click **Log Incident**.
    3. In **What's happening?**, describe a real problem — for example, something the previous answer flagged. Or pick a **Quick Start Template** such as **Service Degradation** to pre-fill the form.
    4. Choose a **Severity**, add any detail under **Context**, and click **Log Incident**.

    CloudThinker starts root-cause analysis right away. A manually logged incident needs no alert source.

    **Success state:** the incident opens in the Investigation view under **AI is handling**. When the investigation finishes, it shows a root cause with evidence, a timeline, and remediation suggestions for you to approve — see [Investigation and RCA](/guide/incident/root-cause-analysis) for how to read it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Investigate every incident automatically (optional)">
    Go to **Resolve** and click **Connect a source**. Choose a webhook (Datadog, PagerDuty, Grafana, Opsgenie, or custom alerts), cloud polling (AWS, Azure Monitor, Google Cloud Monitoring), or Slack or Microsoft Teams.

    **On Duty** — automatic investigation — is on by default, so every incident [Pulse](/guide/pulse/overview) routes from that source gets investigated without anyone clicking **Investigate**. The full setup is in [Connect Pulse sources](/guide/pulse/setup).

    **Success state:** an event sent from your tool appears in Pulse as a Signal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're done when…

- [x] A connection — demo or your own — shows **Connected**
- [x] An agent answered with resources you recognize from that environment
- [x] Your logged incident shows its investigation result, and you can trace it back through the evidence

The **Getting started checklist** tracks these first steps and adds credits as you complete them. From your next sign-in, [Home](/guide/activity) is your starting point: **Needs you** lists anything waiting on your approval, and a banner appears for any live incident.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The sign-in code didn't arrive" icon="envelope">
    **Check:** your spam or promotions folder, and that the address on the code screen is spelled correctly.

    **Fix:** click **Resend code**, or sign in with Google or GitHub instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No service shows 'Demo available'" icon="flask">
    **Likely cause:** a demo is offered only while its demo target is available.

    **Fix:** click **Show all connections** and check the other providers, try again later, or connect your own cloud instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The connection shows 'Connection error'" icon="circle-exclamation">
    **Likely cause:** the credential is wrong or lacks permissions — for AWS, usually an External ID mismatch in the role's trust policy.

    **Fix:** correct the credential in your cloud provider, then click **Retry** on the connection. Provider-specific checks are in each connection guide, such as [AWS troubleshooting](/guide/connections/aws#troubleshooting).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The agent says it has no access, or answers generically" icon="user-slash">
    **Likely cause:** the connection lives in a different [workspace](/guide/workspaces) from the chat you're in.

    **Fix:** check the workspace selector at the top of the sidebar, switch to the workspace where you added the connection, and ask again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The investigation ended with 'Not found'" icon="magnifying-glass">
    **Likely cause:** the available evidence didn't support a root cause — common for an incident that never happened in the connected environment.

    **Fix:** log an incident about something real in that environment, add detail under **Context**, or connect the cloud or monitoring tool that holds the evidence.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
