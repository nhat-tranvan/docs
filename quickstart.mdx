title: 'Quickstart'
description: "Connect one system read-only and have CloudThinker investigate an issue happening now, or an incident from last week, in about 10 minutes."
icon: bolt
---

Bring a real problem: something wrong right now, or an incident from last week. In about 10 minutes CloudThinker investigates it in your own environment and shows you the timeline, the root cause with evidence, and a proposed fix you decide on.

<Info>
**You'll need:** a Google, GitHub or work email account, and read-only access to where the issue happened: an AWS, Google Cloud or Azure account, or a Kubernetes cluster.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [app.cloudthinker.io](https://app.cloudthinker.io) and sign in with Google, GitHub, SSO or your email. This creates your organization and first workspace.

    **Success state:** you land on Home with a chat box.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect where the issue happened">
    Open **More → Workspace settings → Connections**, pick your provider, and connect it with a read-only role:

    | Provider | Setup guide |
    |---|---|
    | AWS | [Connect AWS](/guide/connections/aws) (a CloudShell script creates a `ReadOnlyAccess` role) |
    | Kubernetes | [Connect Kubernetes](/guide/connections/kubernetes) (a kubeconfig that can only `get`, `list`, `watch`) |
    | Google Cloud or Azure | [Connect Google Cloud](/guide/connections/gcp) or [Connect Azure](/guide/connections/azure) |

    If your metrics and logs live in another tool, such as [Datadog](/guide/connections/datadog) or [Grafana](/guide/connections/grafana), connect it too.

    **Success state:** the connection shows **Connected**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask CloudThinker to investigate">
    Click **New chat**, fill in the brackets, and send.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Happening now">
        ```text wrap
        Investigate [checkout-api] in [production]: [5xx errors started about 30 minutes ago]. Find the root cause and show the evidence.
        ```
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Last week's incident">
        ```text wrap
        Investigate the incident on [Tuesday 14:00 to 15:30 UTC]: [orders failed to process]. Build a timeline, find the root cause, and show the evidence.
        ```
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Not sure yet">
        ```text wrap
        What's unhealthy in this account right now? Investigate the worst issue.
        ```
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    **Success state:** you get a **timeline** with times from your environment, the **root cause** with the metric, log or change that proves it, and a **proposed fix**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Decide on the fix">
    New workspaces start on **Auto · Rules + AI · Ask me**, where AI judgment can run a safe write without asking. To see the approval card for this chat, click the approval control next to the send button and choose **Manual**.

    Then ask *Apply the fix you proposed*. CloudThinker shows an approval card with the exact command (or tells you writes are off for this connection, if you turned off **Allow writes**). Click **Decline** for now. With a read-only role, your provider would reject the change anyway.

    **Success state:** nothing in your environment changed. Set the default for your workspace in [Approvals](/guide/approvals/approval-mode).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Stuck? The sign-in code, connection errors and approval controls are covered in [Troubleshooting](/guide/troubleshooting).
</Tip>
