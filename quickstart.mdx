By the end of this page you'll have a workspace with one read-only connection, an answer about your own environment, and a declined approval so you know how changes are gated. Allow **about 10 minutes**.

<Info>
**What you'll need:** a Google or GitHub account, or any work email. Cloud credentials are optional: the shared demo environment works without them.
</Info>

## Get your first answer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and land in your workspace">
    Go to [app.cloudthinker.io](https://app.cloudthinker.io) and sign in with Google, GitHub, or SSO. To use email, enter your address, click **Continue with email**, and type the code CloudThinker sends you.

    Signing up creates your [organization](/guide/organization) (billing and members) and a first [workspace](/guide/workspaces) (the boundary that holds your connections, chats, and data). Two short questions follow: **Tell us about you** asks your role, and for the organization owner, **How can I help you?** asks your goal. Pick **Just exploring** to follow this guide, or click **Skip**.

    **Success state:** you land on Home, with a chat box and the **Needs you**, **Products**, and **Automation today** sections.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect one system read-only">
    A [connection](/guide/connections/overview) is a credential for one service. Agents can only see what your connections allow.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Demo (no credentials)">
        1. Click **Try a demo environment** in the sidebar. Workspace settings open on **Connections**, filtered to services with a demo.
        2. Pick a service that shows **Demo available**, such as Kubernetes or AWS.
        3. Click **Use demo**. Setup takes about a minute.

        The demo is a read-only environment CloudThinker runs and shares with other people trying it. Agents can look but can't change it.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Your own cloud">
        1. In the sidebar, click **More → Workspace settings → Connections**, then pick your provider.
        2. Follow the provider's form. For AWS, choose **Role-Based** (recommended) or **Access Keys**.
        3. Grant read-only permissions for now. You can widen them later.

        Full walkthroughs: [AWS](/guide/connections/aws), [Google Cloud](/guide/connections/gcp), [Azure](/guide/connections/azure), and [Kubernetes](/guide/connections/kubernetes).
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    The status moves through **Connecting** and **Verifying** while CloudThinker tests the credential.

    **Success state:** the connection shows **Connected**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    With the demo, CloudThinker opens a chat with a starter prompt already in the box, such as *Give me an overview of this Kubernetes cluster.* Send it as is.

    With your own cloud, click **New chat** and send:

    ```text
    Give me an overview of this AWS account: resources by service and region, and anything that looks idle
    ```

    Swap in your provider, project, or cluster name. CloudThinker runs read-only commands against the connection and shows its work as it goes.

    **Success state:** the answer names services, regions, and resources you recognize from that environment, not generic best practices.
  </Step>

  <Step title="See an approval, then decline it">
    Every write an agent wants to make passes through [approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) first. New workspaces use **Auto · Rules + AI · Ask me**, which can let a safe write run without asking. To see the approval card every time, switch this chat to Manual:

    1. In the prompt box, click the approval control. It shows the current mode, such as **Auto · Rules + AI · Ask me**.
    2. Under **Approval for this chat**, choose **Manual**. This applies to this chat only.
    3. Ask for a small, harmless change:

    ```text
    Add the tag owner=quickstart to one of the resources you found
    ```

    The agent pauses and shows an approval card with the exact command it wants to run, plus **Approve** and **Decline** buttons. Click **Decline**. Nothing changes, and the agent carries on without that action.

    **Success state:** the card closes as declined and the resource has no new tag. On the demo, a write can't succeed either way, because the environment is read-only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're done when…

- [x] A connection, demo or your own, shows **Connected**
- [x] CloudThinker answered with resources you recognize from that environment
- [x] You saw an approval card and declined it

From your next sign-in, [Home](/guide/activity) is your starting point: **Needs you** lists anything waiting on your approval. The **Getting started checklist** tracks your first steps.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The sign-in code didn't arrive" icon="envelope">
    **Check:** your spam or promotions folder, and the spelling of the address on the code screen.

    **Fix:** click **Resend code**, or sign in with Google or GitHub instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="No service shows 'Demo available'" icon="flask">
    **Likely cause:** a demo is offered only while its target is up.

    **Fix:** click **Show all connections** to check other providers, try again later, or connect your own cloud.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The connection shows an error" icon="circle-exclamation">
    **Likely cause:** the credential is wrong or lacks permissions. For AWS, this is usually an External ID mismatch in the role's trust policy.

    **Fix:** correct the credential with your provider, then click **Retry** on the connection. See [AWS troubleshooting](/guide/connections/aws#troubleshooting) for provider checks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The agent says it has no access, or answers generically" icon="user-slash">
    **Likely cause:** the connection lives in a different workspace from the chat you're in.

    **Fix:** check the workspace selector at the top of the sidebar, switch to the workspace that holds the connection, and ask again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't change the approval control in the chat" icon="lock">
    **Likely cause:** your workspace role is view-only for approvals. The control says **View only. Ask a workspace admin.**

    **Fix:** ask a workspace admin to change your role, or to try the approval step themselves. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
