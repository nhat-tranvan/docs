By the end of this page you'll have a workspace with one read-only connection, an answer about your own environment, and a declined approval so you know how changes are gated. Allow **about 10 minutes**.

<Info>
**What you'll need:** a Google or GitHub account, or any work email, and read-only access to one system you want to ask about: an AWS, Google Cloud or Azure account, or a Kubernetes cluster. A staging or sandbox account is a good place to start.
</Info>

<Tip>
**Here for pull request review?** Do the [sign-in step](#get-your-first-answer), then go to [Set up Review](/guide/code-review/setup). You don't need a cloud connection.
</Tip>

## Get your first answer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and land in your workspace">
    Go to [app.cloudthinker.io](https://app.cloudthinker.io) and sign in with Google, GitHub, or SSO. To use email, enter your address, click **Continue with email**, and type the code CloudThinker sends you. There's no password.

    Signing in with GitHub doesn't give Review access to your repositories. Review setup installs the GitHub App for that.

    Signing up creates your [organization](/guide/organization) (billing and members) and a first [workspace](/guide/workspaces) (the boundary that holds your connections, chats, and data). **Tell us about you** asks your role. Answer it or click **Skip**. If you created the organization, **How can I help you?** follows: each goal opens the matching product or a chat with a starter prompt. Pick **Just exploring** to stay on Home and follow this guide.

    Want to keep your evaluation apart from real work? Rename the first workspace in workspace settings under **General**, or create a second one. See [Workspaces](/guide/workspaces).

    **Success state:** you land on Home, with a chat box and the **Needs you**, **Products**, and **Automation today** sections.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect one system read-only">
    A [connection](/guide/connections/overview) is a credential for one service. Agents can only see what your connections allow.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="AWS">
        For AWS, you need rights to create an IAM role in the target account. Setup runs a script in AWS CloudShell that creates a role with `ReadOnlyAccess`, then you paste the role ARN back. The script already carries your workspace's External ID, so copy it from the dialog and don't edit it. Follow the [AWS Role-Based steps](/guide/connections/aws). They take a few minutes.

        With a read-only role, AWS rejects every write, whatever your approval mode says. To also make CloudThinker refuse writes during an evaluation, turn off **Allow writes** in the connection's detail panel.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Kubernetes">
        Use a kubeconfig whose service account can only read (`get`, `list`, `watch`). Follow the [Kubernetes steps](/guide/connections/kubernetes), starting in **More → Workspace settings → Connections**.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Google Cloud or Azure">
        Follow the [Google Cloud](/guide/connections/gcp) or [Azure](/guide/connections/azure) steps with a read-only role, starting in **More → Workspace settings → Connections**.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    The status moves through **Connecting** and **Verifying** while CloudThinker tests the credential. If it ends in an error, see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).

    **Success state:** the connection shows **Connected**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    Click **New chat** and send:

    ```text
    Give me an overview of this AWS account: resources by service and region, and anything that looks idle
    ```

    Swap in your provider, project, or cluster name. With several accounts, type `#` and pick the connection, such as `#production`, so CloudThinker looks in the right one. CloudThinker runs read-only commands against the connection and shows its work as it goes.

    **Success state:** the answer names services, regions, and resources you recognize from that environment, not generic best practices.

    For an SRE-style second question on AWS, try the prompt below. On Kubernetes, ask *Which pods are in CrashLoopBackOff right now?* instead.

    ```text
    Which EC2 or RDS instances have CloudWatch alarms in ALARM right now?
    ```

    Every chat message spends [credits](/guide/billing/usage). A sweep across every region takes longer and spends more than a question about one region or service, so scope your first questions. [What makes a run cost more](/guide/billing/usage#what-makes-a-run-cost-more) explains the drivers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="See an approval, then decline it">
    Every write an agent wants to make passes through [approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) first. New workspaces use **Auto · Rules + AI · Ask me**, which can let a safe write run without asking. To see the approval card every time, switch this chat to Manual:

    1. In the bottom row of the prompt box, click the approval control. It's the pill just left of the send button, next to the model picker, and shows the current mode, such as **Auto · Rules + AI · Ask me**.
    2. Under **Approval for this chat**, choose **Manual**. This applies to this chat only.
    3. Ask for a small, harmless change:

    ```text
    Add the tag owner=quickstart to one of the resources you found
    ```

    The agent pauses and shows an approval card with the exact command it wants to run, plus **Approve** and **Decline** buttons. Click **Decline**. Nothing changes, and the agent carries on without that action.

    **Success state:** the card closes as declined and the resource has no new tag.

    **If you turned off Allow writes:** no approval card appears. CloudThinker tells you it can't change that connection instead, which also counts for this step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're done when…

- [x] Your connection shows **Connected**
- [x] CloudThinker answered with resources you recognize from that environment
- [x] You declined an approval card, or CloudThinker told you writes are off for that connection

From your next sign-in, [Home](/guide/activity) is your starting point: **Needs you** lists anything waiting on your approval. The **Getting started checklist** tracks your first steps: open it from the round launcher at the bottom-right of Home.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The sign-in code didn't arrive" icon="envelope">
    **Check:** your spam or promotions folder, and the spelling of the address on the code screen.

    **Fix:** click **Resend code**, or sign in with Google or GitHub instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The connection shows an error" icon="circle-exclamation">
    **Likely cause:** the credential is wrong or lacks permissions. For AWS, this is usually an External ID mismatch in the role's trust policy.

    **Fix:** correct the credential with your provider, then click **Retry** on the connection. See [AWS troubleshooting](/guide/connections/aws#troubleshooting) for provider checks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The agent says it has no access, or answers generically" icon="user-slash">
    **Likely cause:** the connection lives in a different workspace from the chat you're in.

    **Fix:** check the workspace selector at the top of the sidebar, switch to the workspace that holds the connection, and ask again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't change the approval control in the chat" icon="lock">
    **Likely cause:** your workspace role is view-only for approvals. The control says **View only. Ask a workspace admin.**

    **Fix:** ask a workspace admin to change your role, or to try the approval step themselves. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
