## How CloudThinker works

Every product runs the same agentic loop: **Detect → Analyze → Resolve → Validate**.

<img src="/images/platform/agentic-loop-light.svg" alt="Agentic loop: Detect, Analyze, Resolve, and Validate in a cycle, with engineers at the center setting intent and approving" className="block dark:hidden" style={{width: '100%', maxWidth: '720px', margin: '0 auto'}} />
<img src="/images/platform/agentic-loop-dark.svg" alt="Agentic loop: Detect, Analyze, Resolve, and Validate in a cycle, with engineers at the center setting intent and approving" className="hidden dark:block" style={{width: '100%', maxWidth: '720px', margin: '0 auto'}} />

| Stage | What happens | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Detect** | A signal arrives from one of your [connections](/guide/connections/overview). | A Datadog alert, a new pull request, a cost spike |
| **Analyze** | The agent gathers evidence from your real systems, plus [memory](/guide/memory) of past work and your [knowledge](/guide/knowledge). | Checks the recent deploy, pod events, and database metrics |
| **Resolve** | The agent proposes or runs the fix. Every write passes your [approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) first. | Proposes a rollback and waits for your **Approve** |
| **Validate** | The agent checks the outcome and saves what it learned. | Confirms error rates recovered, saves the lesson for next time |

Reads run on their own. Changes to your systems follow your Approval mode: **Manual** asks a person for every write, and **Auto** lets your Rules (and optionally AI judgment) decide first. Your engineers set the goal and approve the calls that need judgment. The [field guide](/learn/aio/introduction) covers the method behind the loop.

## Why CloudThinker

Most of an engineer's time on an incident goes to investigation: pulling context from monitoring, cloud consoles, and Git before anyone can decide on a fix. CloudThinker's agents do that work on every issue and hand your team a root cause, the evidence behind it, and a proposed fix. Every change runs under your approval policy and leaves an audit trail. Every run also feeds shared memory, so the next investigation starts with what the last one learned. Start with Resolve, then bring the same agents to pull requests, app security, and cloud spend.
