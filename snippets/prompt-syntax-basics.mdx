Talk to agents in plain language. The full pattern is:

```text
@agent #connection your request
```

- **`@agent`** *(optional)* — leave it out and CloudThinker answers, or mention one of your workspace's [custom agents](/guide/agents/overview#custom-agents).
- **`#connection`** *(optional)* — the connection to use, such as your AWS account or a Kubernetes cluster.
- **`/`** *(optional)* — run a skill or a command.
- **Your request** — what you need, in your own words. Add any context inline.

See [Commands](/guide/commands) for the full list.
