| Mode | What happens to a write that no Rule decides |
|------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Manual** | A person approves every write before it runs, also one that an Allow Rule covers. |
| **Auto** | Your Rules decide first. AI judgment can allow a safe write that no Rule covers, if it is on. The rest wait for a person (**Ask me**) or are refused (**Refuse**). |

Rules decide only once CloudThinker turns on Rule enforcement for your workspace; until then they record the verdict they would give and the Approval mode alone decides. See [Rules in preview](/guide/approvals/rules#troubleshooting).
