Tony is CloudThinker's Database Engineer. Tony tunes SQL and NoSQL schemas, queries, backups, and availability.

## What Tony does best

- Find the slowest queries and read their execution plans.
- Recommend indexes and query rewrites, with the tradeoffs.
- Tune configuration and connection pooling.
- Check backups, replication, and recovery readiness.

Changes to your environment follow your workspace's autonomy mode, Manual or Auto. See [Auto Mode](/guide/auto-mode) and [Approval](/guide/approval).

## Example prompts

Mention an agent with `@`, a connection with `#`, and a Skill or command with `/`. Plain language works.

```text
@tony show the 20 slowest queries on production PostgreSQL with their plans
@tony which indexes would help most, and what do they cost on writes?
@tony check backup status and replication lag for production
```

## Connections

Tony uses your database connections, such as PostgreSQL.
