Kai is CloudThinker's Kubernetes Engineer. Kai runs secure and reliable Kubernetes on EKS, GKE, AKS, and self-managed clusters.

## What Kai does best

- Troubleshoot crash loops, OOMKills, and scheduling failures.
- Right-size resource requests and limits from real usage.
- Tune autoscaling and plan rollouts and rollbacks.
- Review RBAC and network policies.

Changes to your environment follow your workspace's autonomy mode, Manual or Auto. See [Auto Mode](/guide/auto-mode) and [Approval](/guide/approval).

## Example prompts

Mention an agent with `@`, a connection with `#`, and a Skill or command with `/`. Plain language works.

```text
@kai why is the checkout pod crash-looping?
@kai recommend requests and limits from the last 30 days of usage
@kai audit RBAC for least privilege
```
