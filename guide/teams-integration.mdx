Connect Microsoft Teams once and anyone in a mapped team can mention `@CloudThinker` in a channel to ask about your cloud, then keep talking in the thread. The same connection delivers [notifications](/guide/notifications), such as alerts and code review summaries, as Adaptive Cards in the channels you choose.

## Prerequisites

- A CloudThinker organization **Owner** or **Admin**. Other roles can view the Teams page but can't connect or change it.
- A Team plan or above. On lower plans the Microsoft Teams card shows **Requires Team plan or above** and an **Upgrade Plan** button. See [pricing](/guide/billing/pricing).
- A Microsoft 365 administrator who can grant admin consent in Microsoft Entra ID. CloudThinker publishes its bot to your organization's Teams app catalog, which needs admin consent. One person can hold both roles, or two people can work through the setup together.
- CloudThinker hosted at app.cloudthinker.io. A self-hosted install shows **Not available on self-host** on the card.

Each Microsoft tenant can connect to one CloudThinker organization.

## Connect Microsoft Teams

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Teams integration">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Integrations**, find the **Microsoft Teams** card, and click **Go to configure**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect with Microsoft">
    Sign in with the Microsoft admin account. On the permissions screen, check **Consent on behalf of your organization**, then click **Accept**.

    **Success state:** you return to the Teams page with a **Teams connected successfully** message, and **Authenticate with Microsoft** shows your tenant name and ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a team">
    Under **Install bot and configure workspaces**, click **Add team**, search for the team, and select it. CloudThinker installs the bot into that team.

    **Success state:** a **CloudThinker bot installed to team** message appears and the team shows as a row. Click **Add another team** to repeat for more teams.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the workspace for each team">
    Open **Select workspace** on the team's row and pick the CloudThinker workspace whose connections that team should use. Every channel in the team uses this workspace.

    **Success state:** a **Workspace mapping updated** message appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test message">
    In any standard channel of that team, mention the bot:

    ```text
    @CloudThinker What drove our cloud spend up last week?
    ```

    **Success state:** CloudThinker replies in the thread. Reply in that thread to follow up. You don't need to mention the bot again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Anyone who can post in a mapped team can ask CloudThinker to work with that workspace's connections. Requests from people who haven't linked a CloudThinker account run as the workspace owner. Map only teams whose members should have that access.
</Warning>

## Ask CloudThinker in Teams

Start each new request with `@CloudThinker` in a team channel. After that, you write the same requests you'd type in the web app.

<PromptSyntaxBasics />

```text
@CloudThinker Which EC2 instances ran under 10% CPU for the last 14 days?
@CloudThinker Summarize open critical security findings for production
@CloudThinker Why did checkout latency spike at 14:00 UTC today?
```

| You see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| A typing indicator | CloudThinker is working on the request |
| A reply in the thread | The answer. The full conversation also appears in the CloudThinker web app |
| A **Permission Required** card with **View in CloudThinker** | The request needs an action that your [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) setup holds for a person. Open the link and approve or reject it in the web app |
| A **Link Your CloudThinker Account** card | CloudThinker couldn't match you to a user. Click **Link Account** and sign in to CloudThinker |

CloudThinker works in team channels only. In a personal chat or a group chat it doesn't run requests, and it replies **I can only respond in team channels** when it can't find a team.

## Send notifications to Teams

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open notification settings">
    On the Teams page, under **Configure notifications**, click **Go to notification settings**. You can also open **Notifications** from the app sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a default channel for the workspace">
    Under **Teams Notification Channel**, search for and select a channel. Only standard channels are listed. Private and shared channels can't receive bot notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Override per notification type (optional)">
    Pick a different channel or severity threshold for a specific type, such as alerts. See [Notifications](/guide/notifications) for the full list of types.

    **Success state:** the next matching event posts as a card in the chosen channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Code review notifications

When [Review](/guide/code-review/overview) finishes a pull request or merge request on a connected repository, CloudThinker can post an Adaptive Card to Teams:

- A table of findings by severity
- Up to 5 unresolved findings
- How many earlier findings are now resolved
- A **View Review** button that opens the review in CloudThinker
- **No issues found** when the review is clean

Choose the channel and severity threshold for reviews in [Notifications](/guide/notifications).

## Manage the connection

| Task | How |
| --- | --- |
| Move a team to another workspace | Change **Select workspace** on its row |
| Stop using CloudThinker in a team | Remove the team's row on the Teams page |
| Disconnect | Click **Disconnect from Teams** at the bottom of the Teams page |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Teams authorization denied">
    The Microsoft sign-in was denied or cancelled. Click **Connect with Microsoft** again and accept the permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Missing required permissions when adding a team">
    The Microsoft account didn't grant admin consent. Sign in again as a Microsoft 365 administrator and check **Consent on behalf of your organization**, or grant admin consent for CloudThinker in Microsoft Entra ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="This tenant is already connected">
    Your Microsoft tenant is already connected to another CloudThinker organization. Disconnect it there first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="This team isn't linked to any workspace">
    The team has no workspace mapping. On the Teams page, set **Select workspace** on the team's row, or click **Add team** if the team isn't listed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The bot doesn't reply">
    Check that you posted in a standard channel of a team you added, and that you mentioned `@CloudThinker` in a new message. A message without the mention gets a reply only inside a thread CloudThinker already started.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A channel doesn't appear in the notification picker">
    Only standard channels are listed. Pick a standard channel in the same team, or check that the team appears on the Teams page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
