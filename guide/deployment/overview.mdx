Most CloudThinker customers run on the shared, CloudThinker-managed cloud service (multi-tenant SaaS) at [app.cloudthinker.io](https://app.cloudthinker.io). For environments with strict data residency, compliance, or network-isolation requirements, CloudThinker also offers dedicated and self-hosted deployments through the design-partner program.

## Deployment models

| Model | Description |
| --- | --- |
| SaaS (multi-tenant) | Default — managed by CloudThinker. Fastest path to value, no operational burden |
| Dedicated cloud | Single-tenant instance hosted by CloudThinker in your preferred region and cloud |
| Self-hosted | Run CloudThinker inside your own environment — your data never leaves your perimeter |
| Air-gapped | Offline install for classified or otherwise isolated environments |

Self-hosted and air-gapped deployments run inside your own security boundary — review the fit with your security and compliance teams as part of onboarding.

## Get access

System requirements, supported topologies, and upgrade paths depend on your environment and are confirmed during design-partner onboarding rather than published here. [Contact us](https://cloudthinker.io/contact) to discuss whether a dedicated, self-hosted, or air-gapped deployment fits your requirements.
