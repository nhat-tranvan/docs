You buy CloudThinker Enterprise as an AWS Marketplace contract, billed through your AWS invoice. The AWS resources the stack creates are billed to your account separately; see the [cost estimate](/guide/deployment/aws-marketplace/overview#monthly-aws-cost-estimate).

## Monthly credits

Your contract includes a number of credits each month. The install's license page shows the credits included and used this month.

The first and last months are prorated by days, counted in UTC: a month the subscription starts or ends partway through gets credits in proportion to the days it covers.

When the month's credits run out and pay-as-you-go is off, runs stop until the next month's credits arrive.

## When the subscription ends

| Time after the subscription ends | What the install does |
|---|---|
| Days 1 to 30 | Keeps running normally during a 30-day grace period. Administrators see a notice with the date the grace period ends. |
| After 30 days | Stops starting new runs. Your data stays in your account. |

Renew in AWS Marketplace at any point to keep the install running. To remove it instead, see [Uninstall](/guide/deployment/aws-marketplace/uninstall).

## If the install cannot reach CloudThinker billing

<Note>
**Subject to change.** These windows are under review and may be extended for Enterprise installs.
</Note>

The install syncs its license with the CloudThinker billing endpoint about every 15 minutes. If it cannot reach it:

| Time without a good license | What the install does |
|---|---|
| Up to 24 hours | Keeps running normally |
| After 24 hours | Pauses scheduled and alert runs. Manual investigations still work. |
| After 14 days | Stops all runs. An administrator sees the reason on the license page. |

Runs resume on their own once the install reaches billing again. Keep outbound HTTPS to the billing endpoint open, as in [Network access](/guide/deployment/aws-marketplace/network-access#outbound-https-the-install-needs).
