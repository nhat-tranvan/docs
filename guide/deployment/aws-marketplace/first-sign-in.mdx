The first administrator is the email you entered on the setup page. That person signs in first and invites everyone else.

<Warning>
**Applies from the next release. Until then:** the administrator signs in at `<your-install-address>` with a code sent by email, so [email](/guide/deployment/aws-marketplace/email) must work first. The sender must differ from the administrator's email, and while Amazon SES is in sandbox mode the administrator's address must be verified in SES or the account moved to production access. Steps and labels below can change before release.
</Warning>

## Sign in as the administrator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the one-time setup link">
    In the CloudFormation console, open the stack's **Outputs** tab. The **AdminSetup** output names a secret: open it in AWS Secrets Manager and choose **Retrieve secret value** to copy the one-time administrator setup link. It works once, within 24 hours of the install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a local password">
    Open the link, confirm the administrator's email, and choose a password. This local sign-in stays available as a break-glass account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open `<your-install-address>` and sign in with the email and password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the link expired or was used

Make a new link: in CloudFormation, update the nested Enterprise stack (the one whose name ends in `Enterprise`), keep every other value, and set **AdminSetupReissue** to any new value. When the update completes, retrieve the secret again. The install restarts the app once, which takes a few minutes.

## Turn on single sign-on

After the first sign-in, connect your identity provider so your team signs in with their company accounts. CloudThinker supports SAML 2.0 and OIDC. See [Single sign-on](/guide/security/sso).

Keep at least one administrator who can sign in with a local password, in case the identity provider is unavailable.

## Invite your team

Invitations go out by email, so finish [Email](/guide/deployment/aws-marketplace/email) first. With Amazon SES still in sandbox mode, only verified addresses receive mail.
