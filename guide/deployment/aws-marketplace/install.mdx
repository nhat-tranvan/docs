Installing takes three parts: subscribe on AWS Marketplace, fill in the CloudThinker setup page, then launch the stack from AWS. Plan about 25 minutes from launch to a working app.

## Prerequisites

- The quotas and deployment permissions in [Before you start](/guide/deployment/aws-marketplace/before-you-start).
- A Region from the [supported list](/guide/deployment/aws-marketplace/overview#supported-regions).

## Subscribe and set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subscribe on AWS Marketplace">
    Open the CloudThinker Enterprise listing, choose **View purchase options**, review the contract, and choose **Subscribe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the setup page">
    When the subscription is active, choose **Set up your account**. AWS opens the CloudThinker setup page in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the setup page">
    Enter the administrator's email, choose how the app sends email, and choose who can reach the app. Private network access is the default. For internet access, enter the IP ranges to allow, such as `203.0.113.0/24`. Save the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to AWS">
    Choose the button that takes you back to AWS Marketplace **Configure and launch**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Success state:** the setup page shows your install and its managed address, `<your-install-address>`.

## Launch the stack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable integration">
    On **Configure and launch**, choose **Enable integration** if AWS asks. You do this once per account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Launch template and your Region">
    Choose **Launch template**. AWS preselects US East (N. Virginia). Change the Region to the one you planned before you continue, since the stack is built where you launch it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Quick create form">
    CloudFormation opens with the deployment settings filled in from the setup page. Leave the prefilled references unchanged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acknowledge and create">
    Tick the boxes that acknowledge IAM resources and nested stacks, then choose **Create stack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the stack">
    Watch **Events**. The stack takes about 17 minutes to reach `CREATE_COMPLETE`. The app is usually ready about 6 minutes later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Stack outputs

The launch creates a top-level stack, the one you named, and a nested stack whose name contains `-Enterprise-`. When the top-level stack completes, open its **Outputs** tab.

| Output | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| `CloudThinkerUrl` | The address to open: `<your-install-address>` |
| `EmailSetup` | What email delivery still needs, such as DNS records to add |
| `ClusterName` | The EKS cluster that runs CloudThinker |
| `EgressIp` | The one public address every outbound call leaves from. Allow it on firewalls and IP-filtered tokens. |

The nested stack's **Outputs** tab adds the database, cache, bucket, and certificate, and `KeptResources`: what stays in your account if you delete the install. Read the nested stack, but never update or delete it directly. Manage the install through the top-level stack.

If `<your-install-address>` does not open yet, wait a few minutes: the name can take a short time to resolve after the stack completes.
