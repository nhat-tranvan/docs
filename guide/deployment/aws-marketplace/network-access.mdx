You choose who can reach the app when you fill in the setup page. The install also makes a small set of outbound HTTPS calls that your network must allow.

## Who can reach the app

| Option | How it works | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Private network** (default) | The app sits on an internal Network Load Balancer, reachable only from networks that route to the VPC: VPC peering, AWS Transit Gateway, Site-to-Site VPN, or AWS Direct Connect. | Your users and connected systems are on your corporate network. |
| **Internet, limited to your IP ranges** | The app sits on an internet-facing Network Load Balancer that admits only the IPv4 ranges you list (up to 20), such as your office and VPN egress, for example `203.0.113.0/24`. | Users work from known public addresses and you have no private route to the VPC yet. |

Both options serve the app over HTTPS only, with a certificate for `<your-install-address>`. Inbound webhooks from outside services are off by default.

To change the access option or the allowed ranges after launch, [contact CloudThinker](https://cloudthinker.io/contact).

## Outbound HTTPS the install needs

Every outbound call leaves from one public address, shown as the `EgressIp` stack output. If your network inspects or filters egress, allow HTTPS (TCP 443) from that address to:

| Destination | Why |
|---|---|
| The CloudThinker billing endpoint | Keeps the license current and reports usage for your contract. Ask your CloudThinker contact for the exact address to allow. |
| AWS service APIs | Amazon ECR in US East (N. Virginia), where the software is pulled from for every Region; and in your Region, Amazon Bedrock, Amazon S3, AWS KMS, AWS Secrets Manager, Amazon SES, and AWS STS. |
| The services you connect | The cloud accounts, tools, and repositories your team adds as [connections](/guide/connections/overview). |

<Warning>
If the install cannot reach the CloudThinker billing endpoint, scheduled and alert runs pause after 24 hours and all runs stop after 14 days. Test egress changes before you apply them. See [Contract and billing](/guide/deployment/aws-marketplace/contract-and-billing#if-the-install-cannot-reach-cloudthinker-billing).
</Warning>

## What the sandboxes can reach

Agent-generated code runs in sandboxes on their own nodes. A sandbox can reach the internet through the same egress address. It can never reach the install's database, cache, or instance metadata. It reaches the cluster API only if you set `SandboxClusterApi` to `on`.

Your private IP ranges are blocked by default. To let agents reach internal services over peering, Transit Gateway, or VPN, [contact CloudThinker](https://cloudthinker.io/contact) with the private ranges to allow.
