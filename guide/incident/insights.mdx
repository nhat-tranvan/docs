**Resolve → Insights** shows how Resolve, CloudThinker's incident response module, performed over a time window you pick, and whether you can trust its diagnoses. Use it to decide what to fix next: a noisy alert source, a service with slow recovery, or an incident type with no runbook.

## Prerequisites

- A workspace with [Resolve](/guide/incident/overview) receiving alerts. Insights reads past incidents, so it stays empty until some exist.
- Permission to view incidents.
- For the accuracy numbers: people on your team marking diagnoses right or wrong (see [Mark a diagnosis](#mark-a-diagnosis-right-or-wrong)).

## Open Insights and pick a window

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    In the sidebar, open **Resolve → Insights** (under **Analytics**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a time window">
    Use the selector in the page header. Options run from **Last 1 hour** to **Last 30 days**, and the default is **Last 7 days**. The window stays the same when you switch tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    Choose **Overview**, **Accuracy & trust**, **Pulse analysis**, or **Runbook analytics**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Success state:** the tab fills with cards for the window you chose. A card that says something like "No runbook executions in this period" means there's no data yet, not an error.

## Choose the right tab

| Tab | Question it answers | Start here when |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | How fast does Resolve get from alert to fix, and which services hurt most? | Your on-call lead asks why last week felt slow. |
| **Accuracy & trust** | How often did people confirm Resolve's root cause was correct? | You're deciding how much to rely on automatic investigation. |
| **Pulse analysis** | How much alert noise does Pulse absorb, and which sources make the most? | Responders complain about alert volume. |
| **Runbook analytics** | Which runbooks run, succeed, and which services have none? | You're planning which runbook to write next. |

## Read the Overview tab

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Mean time to resolve** | Average time to resolve, across resolved incidents. |
| **Alert → root cause** | Median time from the incident starting to a root cause being found. It covers detection, correlation, and analysis together, not only how long the agent ran. |
| **Auto-resolved** | Share of resolved incidents CloudThinker closed with no human touch. |
| **Noise reduced** | How raw events collapse into signals, clusters, and incidents. Hover the help icon for the full funnel. |
| **Where the time goes** | Time spent in each stage: **Detect**, **Correlate**, **Root cause**, **Fix staged**, **Validate**. |
| **MTTR over time** | Mean time to resolve (MTTR), plotted across the window. |
| **Recurrence trend** | How often the same problems come back. |
| **Reliability by service** | Per service: **Incidents**, **MTTR**, **Auto**, and **Manual takeover**. |

When total response time goes up, check **Where the time goes** first. A slower **Detect** stage points at your alert sources, not at root cause analysis (RCA).

## Read the Accuracy & trust tab

Every number on this tab comes from people on your team marking a diagnosis as right or wrong. Nothing comes from the agent scoring itself.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Root-cause accuracy** | Diagnoses confirmed correct, out of those reviewed. |
| **Fix acceptance** | Staged fixes approved, out of those offered. |
| **False-positive rate** | Incidents marked as noise, not a real issue. |
| **Your review coverage** | Resolved incidents someone reviewed, out of all resolved incidents. |
| **When Resolve was off, why?** | The reasons people gave when they marked a diagnosis wrong. |
| **Review more incidents** | How many resolved incidents are still unmarked. |
| **Accuracy by service** | **Reviewed**, **Accurate**, and **Rate** per service. Services come from each incident's free-text tags. |

Use the reasons to decide what to change:

| Reason | What it usually points at |
|---|---|
| **Wrong root cause** | Missing evidence. Connect the logs, metrics, or traces the investigation needed. |
| **Wrong service** | Missing context about how your services depend on each other. |
| **Fix wouldn't work** | Remediation quality. Add or update a [runbook](/guide/incident/runbooks). |
| **False positive** | A noisy alert. Tune it at the source or in [Pulse](/guide/pulse/clusters). |
| **Something else** | Read the comments on those incidents. |

<Warning>
Check **Your review coverage** before you act on **Root-cause accuracy**. With only a handful of reviewed incidents, one verdict swings the rate a lot. Read the underlying incidents before you change a connection, runbook, or process.
</Warning>

### Mark a diagnosis right or wrong

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a resolved incident">
    Go to **Resolve → Investigation** and open the incident.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the question">
    Under **Was Resolve's diagnosis right?**, mark it accurate or inaccurate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what was wrong">
    If you mark it inaccurate, pick the closest reason under **What was wrong?** and add an optional note.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Success state:** the incident shows **Marked accurate** or **Marked not accurate**, and the verdict counts on the **Accuracy & trust** tab. Marks are per incident and stay until someone changes them.

## Read the Pulse analysis tab

[Pulse](/guide/pulse/overview) is the part of Resolve that drops alert noise and groups related alerts into clusters before anything becomes an incident.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Correlation yield** | Share of signals whose cluster was routed to an incident or resolved. |
| **Cluster MTTR** | Median time to resolve a cluster. |
| **Signals ingested** | Raw events received in the window. |
| **Noise suppressed** | Suppressed signals out of all signals seen. |
| **Top noisy sources** | Per source: **Signals**, **Suppressed**, and **Clusters routed**. |

**Noise suppressed** here and **Noise reduced** on Overview use different denominators. Noise suppressed counts signals Pulse dropped. Noise reduced counts raw events that collapsed into incidents. For more detail, see [Pulse analytics](/guide/pulse/analytics).

## Read the Runbook analytics tab

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Runbook coverage** | Closed incidents that used a runbook, out of all closed incidents. |
| **Executions** | Runbook runs in the window. |
| **Completion rate** | Runs that finished successfully, out of runs that started. It doesn't tell you whether the fix held later. |
| **Runbook effectiveness** | Per runbook: **Mode**, **Runs**, **Executed**, and **Success**. |
| **Coverage gaps** | Services whose incidents closed without any runbook running. |

**Coverage gaps** is your runbook to-do list: the service at the top is where a new runbook helps most. The full list of individual runs stays on **Resolve → Runbooks**.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Every tab is empty">
    **Cause:** no incidents fall inside the window. **Check:** widen the selector to **Last 30 days**. **Fix:** if it's still empty, confirm alerts are arriving on **Resolve → Pulse**, then follow [Pulse setup](/guide/pulse/setup).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Accuracy shows no percentage">
    **Cause:** nobody has marked a diagnosis in this window. **Fix:** mark a few resolved incidents under **Was Resolve's diagnosis right?**. The **Review more incidents** card shows how many are waiting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A service is missing from Accuracy by service">
    **Cause:** services on this table come from each incident's free-text tags. **Fix:** make sure incidents for that service carry a consistent tag.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A tab shows a load error">
    Click **Retry**. If it keeps failing, see [Resolve troubleshooting](/guide/incident/troubleshooting).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
