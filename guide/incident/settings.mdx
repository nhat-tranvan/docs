Resolve settings decide whether CloudThinker investigates incidents on its own and where it reports progress. The two workspace-wide switches are **On duty**, in the header of **Resolve → Investigation**, and **Live progress updates**, on **Resolve → Settings**.

## Prerequisites

- The workspace **Admin** role to change any setting. Developers and Viewers can see the values but can't change them. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles).
- Resolve set up with at least one alert source. See [Connect Pulse sources](/guide/pulse/setup).

## Where each setting lives

Resolve spreads its controls across a few pages. Use this table to find the one you need.

| Setting | Where | Default | What it controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| **On duty** | **Resolve → Investigation**, page header | On | Whether CloudThinker starts investigations automatically |
| **Live progress updates** | **Resolve → Settings → Behaviour** | On | Whether progress and SITREP updates post to the incident's channels |
| **Auto-investigate** | Each webhook, under **Resolve → Integrations** | On | Whether alerts from that source can start an investigation. See [Webhook options](/guide/pulse/setup#webhook-options-that-change-behavior). |
| **Only from a minimum severity** | Each webhook | Off | The lowest severity that starts an investigation |
| **Send directly to CloudThinker** | Each webhook | Off | Whether alerts skip Pulse filtering and grouping. Use only for low-volume sources where every event matters. |
| **Enable On-call** | **Resolve → On-call** | Off | Whether a qualifying incident opens an Incident Room with your responders. See [On-call](/guide/incident/oncall). |
| **Policies** | **Resolve → Settings**, only where enabled | Not shown | The effect of each command from an uploaded runbook. See [Runbooks](/guide/incident/runbooks#control-command-effects). |

If you don't see **Policies**, it's off for your deployment. Email [support@cloudthinker.io](mailto:support@cloudthinker.io) to ask about it. Without it, workspace [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) decide.

Every switch saves as soon as you flip it. There's no **Save** button.

## Pause automatic investigation

**On duty** is CloudThinker's autonomy switch. It isn't your team's on-call rotation.

| State | Header shows | What happens to a new incident |
|---|---|---|
| On | **On duty** | Pulse detects it, and CloudThinker investigates on its own when the source has **Auto-investigate** on and the severity is at or above its minimum |
| Off | **Paused** | Pulse still detects and groups alerts and files the incident as Open. It waits in **Not started** until someone clicks **Investigate**. |

Pause it during a planned maintenance window, or while you tune a noisy source.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the investigation queue">
    Go to **Resolve → Investigation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flip the switch">
    In the page header, turn off the **On duty** switch.

    **Success state:** the label next to the switch reads **Paused**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investigate by hand while paused">
    Select any incident in **Not started** and click **Investigate** (Admins only). Manual investigations work the same whether CloudThinker is on duty or paused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it back on">
    Flip the switch back. The label reads **On duty** again, and new qualifying incidents are investigated automatically. Incidents filed while paused stay in **Not started**. They're listed under the **Not started** group in the **Resolve → Investigation** sidebar, each row with its severity. There's no bulk action, so click **Investigate** on each one, starting with the highest severity. Skip any whose alert cleared during the maintenance window and needs no investigation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
There's no timer or auto-resume. **On duty** stays paused until someone turns it back on, so check the header after every maintenance window. The header doesn't show who paused it or when. Each change is recorded in [Audit Logs](/guide/security/audit-logs): filter **Category** to **Incident** and look for the raw action `incidents.settings_updated`. The entry shows who made the change, when, and which setting changed with its new value (not the old one). The same event covers **Live progress updates** changes.
</Tip>

## Choose where progress appears

While CloudThinker investigates, it writes progress notes and SITREPs (short situation reports: what's known, what's being checked, what's next).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open settings">
    Go to **Resolve → Settings**. The **Behaviour** section is at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Live progress updates">
    Leave **Live progress updates** on to post each update to the incident's channels as it's written. Turn it off to keep updates on the incident's **Investigation timeline** only.

    **Success state:** the change applies right away, including to investigations already running: their next update posts (or stops posting) to their channels. To check it, watch the next update on an incident that already has an active war room or Incident Room.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The channels are the ones attached to the incident's response: its Slack war room, its Incident Room, and any Teams incident channel or Google Chat incident space. Updates post only while the incident has an active response. [On-call](/guide/incident/oncall) explains how a war room and Incident Room are attached.

Turn it off when a busy incident floods a shared channel. Nothing is lost: every update stays on the incident timeline.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The switches are greyed out">
    **Cause:** you don't have the workspace Admin role. The settings page says "You can view these settings, but only editors can change them." There, "editors" means workspace Admins. **Fix:** ask a workspace Admin to change the setting or your role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="On duty is on, but an incident wasn't investigated">
    **Cause:** that webhook has **Auto-investigate** off, or **Only from a minimum severity** is set above the alert's severity. **Check:** open the webhook under **Resolve → Integrations**. **Fix:** adjust the option, then click **Investigate** on the waiting incident. More causes are in [Resolve troubleshooting](/guide/incident/troubleshooting).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
