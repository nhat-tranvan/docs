**Every incident gets a room.**

On-call decides who joins an incident when it needs people, so engineers and agents work it together. When an incident qualifies, Resolve opens an **Incident Room**, a chat inside CloudThinker where your responders and CloudThinker work the incident together, and can open a matching Slack war room.

<OnCallScene />

## How Resolve decides an incident needs people

<Note>
On-call isn't a rotation or a phone pager. It doesn't call or text anyone. Responders get an in-app notification and, if you set it up, a Slack channel invite. For off-hours response, keep your external pager in the loop.
</Note>

Every incident Resolve opens carries a response level. CloudThinker's triage sets it from the evidence it gathered, and a person can raise it later.

| Response level | When triage picks it | What opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Page responders** (the page level) | Users or revenue are affected now or within the hour | Incident Room (if On-call is on) and Slack war room (if set up and the severity clears its floor) |
| **Track only** | The incident is real but not urgent | Nothing. The incident stays in **Resolve → Investigation** |

The **On duty** switch on **Resolve → Investigation** also matters here. When it's **Paused**, CloudThinker can't page responders on its own unless a person started that investigation. You can always page by hand.

## Prerequisites

- The workspace **Admin** role. Developers and Viewers see the On-call page read-only, and the **Page responders** button doesn't show for them. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles).
- Responders must be members of the workspace, because the Incident Room lives there.
- For a Slack war room: Slack connected to Resolve under **Resolve → Integrations**. See [Integrations](/guide/incident/integrations). War rooms are public channels the CloudThinker app creates, so your Slack admins must let apps create public channels.

## Turn on On-call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open On-call">
    Go to **Resolve → On-call**. Each control saves the moment you change it. There's no Save button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable On-call">
    Turn on **Enable On-call**. It's off in a new workspace.

    **Success state:** the switch stays on after you reload the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add responders">
    Click **Add responders** under **Responders** and pick people from the workspace member list. Click a name again to remove it. You can add up to 50.

    Leave the list empty and each Incident Room opens with CloudThinker alone.

    **Success state:** the field shows the first two names and a `+N` count for the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: add agents from other workspaces">
    If another workspace shares an agent with yours and allows it to join rooms, **Agents from other workspaces** lists it. Check up to 5. These agents join every Incident Room and answer read-only questions when someone mentions them. See [Sharing](/guide/sharing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

From now on, every new incident at the page level gets one Incident Room, named `inc-<short id>-<title>`.

## Add a Slack war room

A Slack war room is a dedicated Slack channel for one incident. It's separate from On-call: it opens for page-level incidents even when On-call is off, as long as its severity floor is met.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Slack integration">
    Go to **Resolve → Integrations** and select **Slack**. Connect it first if it isn't connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on war rooms">
    Under **What Resolve can do**, turn on **Create war-room channels**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the minimum severity">
    Pick which incidents get a channel:

    | Option | Opens a channel for |
    |---|---|
    | **Critical only** | Critical incidents |
    | **High & critical** | High and critical incidents (the default) |
    | **All severities** | Every page-level incident |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who's invited">
    Pick up to 20 Slack members to invite to every new war room.

    This list is separate from On-call responders, which are CloudThinker workspace members. Resolve doesn't copy one into the other, so add the same people to both if they should join both.

    **Success state:** the counter reads `N of 20 selected`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The channel is named `inc-<short id>-<title>`: the first 8 characters of the incident ID, then the title in lowercase with spaces and symbols turned into hyphens, cut at 80 characters. For example, `Checkout API 5xx rate above 5%` becomes `inc-3f9a1c2e-checkout-api-5xx-rate-above-5`. If **Post incident updates** is on for your broadcast channel, Resolve also posts a pointer there to the new war room.

## Page responders by hand

Triage may mark an incident **Track only** when you know it's urgent. Raise it yourself:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the incident">
    Go to **Resolve → Investigation** and open the incident.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page">
    Click the **Page responders** button, then confirm with **Page responders** in the dialog. This raises the incident to the page level, and Resolve notifies everyone once.

    **Success state:** the button changes to **Open Incident Room**, and the timeline shows **Responders paged**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A manual page follows the same rules as one from triage:

| Setting | Incident Room | Slack war room |
|---|---|---|
| **Enable On-call** on | Opens | Opens if war rooms are on and the severity clears the floor |
| **Enable On-call** off | Doesn't open | Opens if war rooms are on and the severity clears the floor |

The **Page responders** button only shows while the incident is in active response and has no Room yet. On an incident CloudThinker recorded after the impact ended, you see **Start response** instead. It restarts response on the same investigation and asks you why it needs one (at least 10 characters).

## Work the incident together

Click **Open Incident Room** on the incident to join. CloudThinker posts the first message: the title, severity, impact, a link back to the incident, and a note that it's investigating.

| Who | What they do |
|---|---|
| CloudThinker | Keeps running the root-cause analysis (RCA) and answers in the Room from that same investigation, so you don't repeat context |
| Responders | Ask questions, share what they see, and decide on fixes |
| Shared agents | Answer read-only questions about their own workspace's data when mentioned |
| Slack war room | Holds one summary card (incident, state, severity, impact, source) that's edited in place as things change, plus lifecycle updates such as RCA completing |

Mention CloudThinker in the Room to ask about the incident.

```text
@CloudThinker what changed on checkout-api in the hour before the first alert?
@CloudThinker which pods behind checkout-api are failing health checks right now?
@CloudThinker what's the safest rollback, and what does it touch?
```

Fixes still go through [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview), so talking in the Room doesn't skip your policy.

When the incident resolves, the Slack summary card updates to show it. Resolve doesn't archive the Incident Room or the Slack channel, so archive the channel in Slack when your team is done with it. For the full analysis, open the incident and read the [root cause](/guide/incident/root-cause-analysis).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An incident opened but no Incident Room">
    Check three things. **Enable On-call** must be on in **Resolve → On-call**. The incident must be at the page level: if triage chose **Track only**, click the **Page responders** button on the incident. And while **On duty** is **Paused**, CloudThinker can't page on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A responder isn't in the Room">
    The Room only adds people who can still open the workspace. Someone removed from the workspace is skipped. Add them back to the workspace, and the next Incident Room includes them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No Slack war room">
    Confirm Slack is connected in **Resolve → Integrations**, **Create war-room channels** is on, and the incident's severity clears the floor you set. A **High** incident doesn't open a channel under **Critical only**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Agents from other workspaces doesn't show">
    The section only appears when agent sharing is on and at least one shared agent is allowed to join rooms. Ask the owning workspace to allow it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
