Admin Settings hold everything that applies to your whole [organization](/guide/organization) rather than one workspace. Open them from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Admin settings**. Only organization Owners and Admins can open them, and some sections are Owner-only; see [Roles](/guide/security/roles).

## Every section

| Section | What you set there | Learn more |
|---|---|---|
| **Organization** | The organization name, members and their organization role | [Organization](/guide/organization) |
| **Workspaces** | Create, rename and delete workspaces, and choose who can open each one | [Workspaces](/guide/workspaces) |
| **Billing** | Your plan, seats, payment method and invoices | [Subscription](/guide/billing/subscription) |
| **Credit limits** | A monthly credit limit per workspace and what happens when it's reached | [Credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits) |
| **Usage** | Credits spent, by workspace, product and person | [Usage and credits](/guide/billing/usage) |
| **BYOK** | Your own LLM credentials for every workspace | [BYOK](/guide/byok) |
| **Capabilities** | Which capabilities, such as Advisor, Blind Spot and Conversation recaps, run in your workspaces | [Capabilities](/guide/capabilities) |
| **Identity and access** | Domain verification, SSO and SCIM provisioning | [SSO](/guide/security/sso), [SCIM](/guide/security/scim) |
| **Guardrails** | Patterns that hide secrets and personal data from agents | [Custom guardrails](/guide/security/custom-guardrails) |
| **Integrations** | Slack, Microsoft Teams and Google Chat for the organization | [Slack](/guide/slack-integration), [Teams](/guide/teams-integration), [Google Chat](/guide/google-chat-integration) |
| **Audit Logs** | Who did what, and when, across the organization | [Audit logs](/guide/security/audit-logs) |
| **Sandboxes** | Workers: machines you control where agents run commands | [Workers](/guide/workers) |

Settings that apply to one workspace, such as connections and approvals, live in [Workspace settings](/guide/workspace-settings).

## Set up a new organization

Owners usually do these once, in this order:

1. **Identity and access:** verify your email domain, then set up SSO if you use one.
2. **Workspaces:** create a workspace per team or environment.
3. **Organization:** invite members and give each the right role.
4. **Credit limits:** set a monthly limit so no workspace spends more than you expect.
