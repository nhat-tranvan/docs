Organization settings hold everything that applies to your whole [organization](/guide/organization) rather than one workspace. Only organization Owners and Admins can open them, and some pages are Owner-only; see [Roles](/guide/security/roles).

## Open organization settings

Click the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**, or press ⌘, (Ctrl+, on Windows and Linux). Then click the **Organization** tab at the top. Developers and Viewers see "Organization admin access required" there instead of the pages.

## Every page

The **Organization** tab groups its pages into three sections, in this order.

| Group | Page | What you set there | Learn more |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overview | **General** | The organization name and description, plus a summary of seats, workspaces, and credits | [Organization](/guide/organization) |
| Overview | **Workspaces** | Create, open, and delete workspaces | [Workspaces](/guide/workspaces) |
| Overview | **Members** | Who is in the organization, their organization role, and which workspaces each Developer or Viewer can open | [Organization](/guide/organization#manage-members-and-invitations) |
| Overview | **Identity** | Domain verification, SSO, and SCIM provisioning | [SSO](/guide/security/sso), [SCIM](/guide/security/scim) |
| Overview | **Audit log** | Who did what, and when, across the organization | [Audit logs](/guide/security/audit-logs) |
| Access | **Guardrails** | Patterns that hide secrets and personal data from agents | [Custom guardrails](/guide/security/custom-guardrails) |
| Access | **Policies** | Whether Advisor and Blind Spot are off, chosen per workspace, or on everywhere. Owner only | [Capabilities](/guide/capabilities) |
| Access | **BYOK** | Your own model credentials for every workspace, including OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Vertex AI, LiteLLM, and compatible endpoints | [BYOK](/guide/byok), [Endpoint providers](/guide/byok-endpoint-providers) |
| Access | **Chat integrations** | Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Google Chat for the organization | [Slack](/guide/slack-integration), [Teams](/guide/teams-integration), [Google Chat](/guide/google-chat-integration) |
| Access | **Environments** | Machines you control where agents run commands | [Environments](/guide/workers) |
| Subscription | **Billing** | Your plan, payment method, and invoices, plus the **Credit limits** section: a monthly credit limit per workspace and what happens when it's reached | [Subscription](/guide/billing/subscription), [Credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits) |
| Subscription | **Usage** | Credits spent, by workspace, product, and person. CloudThinker cloud only | [Usage and credits](/guide/billing/usage) |

Settings that apply to one workspace, such as connections and Autonomy, live on the **Workspace** tab. See [Workspace settings](/guide/workspace-settings).

## Set up a new organization

Owners usually do these once, in this order:

1. **Identity:** verify your email domain, then set up SSO if you use one.
2. **Workspaces:** create a workspace per team or environment.
3. **Members:** invite members and give each the right role.
4. **Billing → Credit limits:** set a monthly limit so no workspace spends more than you expect.
