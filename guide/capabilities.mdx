Capabilities are helpers that work alongside CloudThinker in Chat. There are two: **Advisor** and **Blind Spot**. The organization Owner decides who controls each one, and workspace Admins turn them on or off for their workspace when the Owner allows it.

When capabilities are available for your organization, both start **on** in every workspace and use that workspace's credits. To stop one, a workspace Admin turns its switch off in **Settings → Workspace → Capabilities**, or the Owner sets it to **Off** for every workspace in **Settings → Organization → Policies**.

<Note>
  If **Settings → Workspace → Capabilities** shows neither of them, they aren't available for your organization yet. Open Settings from the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar, or press ⌘⇧, (Ctrl+Shift+, on Windows and Linux).
</Note>

## What each capability does

| Capability | What it does | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| **Advisor** | A second model reviews the agent's work and suggests fixes. On a longer Chat turn, it checks the work with the same tools the agent uses and passes a short note back to the agent. Short turns get no Advisor review. | An **Advisor review** you can open on the reply. It says whether Advisor passed a note to CloudThinker or found nothing to add. You don't act on the note, the agent does. |
| **Blind Spot** | Points out what the agent may have missed. While a long Chat turn runs, it looks for one important thing you're likely to miss. | A **Heads up** card above the chat box with **Why?** (when it has an explanation), **Add to composer**, **I know this**, and **Dismiss**. **I know this** tells it not to raise that topic again. A card you ignore goes away after a couple of messages. |

Each capability makes its own model calls, so it uses credits from your workspace. When a workspace runs out of credits, the capability stops running there until credits are available again. See [usage](/guide/billing/usage).

## Who can change them

| Where | Who can change it | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| **Settings → Organization → Policies** | Organization **Owner** only. Admins don't see **Policies** in the Organization list. An Admin who opens its link sees it locked with "Only the organization owner can change capabilities". | Whether each capability is off everywhere, chosen per workspace, or on everywhere |
| **Settings → Workspace → Capabilities** | Workspace **Admin**. Organization Owners and Admins are Admin in every workspace. Developers and Viewers see the switches with "Only a workspace admin can change this." | Whether each capability runs in this workspace |

See [Roles and Permissions](/guide/security/roles) for the full permission tables.

## Set capabilities for the organization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Policies">
    Go to **Settings → Organization → Policies**. The page is titled **Capabilities**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a mode for each capability">
    Each capability has three choices:

    | Mode | Effect |
    |---|---|
    | **Off** | Off in every workspace |
    | **Workspace** | Each workspace Admin turns it on or off in workspace settings |
    | **Organization** | On in every workspace. Workspace Admins can't change it |

    **Success state:** a confirmation names the capability and its new state, for example "is now chosen by each workspace admin."
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Workspace** is the starting mode. In that mode, a capability is on in a workspace until a workspace Admin turns it off.

## Turn a capability on or off in a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Capabilities">
    Go to **Settings → Workspace → Capabilities**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the switch">
    Find the capability and turn its switch on or off. The change applies to new work, not to a turn that's already running.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the Owner has set a capability to **Off** or **Organization**, its switch is locked and shows "Your organization turned this off" or "Your organization turned this on". Ask the Owner to change it in **Settings → Organization → Policies**.

Changes at both levels are recorded in [audit logs](/guide/security/audit-logs).
