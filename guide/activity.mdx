Home is the screen you land on after sign-in: a chat box, everything waiting on you, one status line per product, and a banner for any live incident. Activity is the full, searchable inbox of everything agents have reported, and the **Runs** tab on Automations lists every automation run.

## Where things show up

| You want to | Go to |
|---|---|
| Answer an approval or decision an agent is waiting on | Home → **Needs you** |
| See which products have work queued | Home → **Products** |
| Confirm today's scheduled work ran | Home → **Automation today** |
| See live incidents | The incident banner at the top of Home |
| Find an older report, finding, or alert | **Activity** at [app.cloudthinker.io/activity](https://app.cloudthinker.io/activity) |
| See every run of every automation | **Automations → Runs** |

Each item only appears if your [role](/guide/workspace-users) can view the product it belongs to.

## Clear what needs you

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Home">
    Click **Home** in the sidebar, or sign in. The **Needs you** card shows up to four items, newest ask first. Each row names the item, the kind of ask, and how long it has waited.

    **Success state:** you see the card with rows, or the line *Nothing is waiting on you.* An empty card is the goal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an item">
    Click a row. It opens the place where you answer (see the table below). The row's action reads **Review** for approvals and decisions, **Open** for everything else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer it">
    For a chat approval, the action card shows what the agent wants to run. Click **Approve**, **Approve and draft rule** (approves now and drafts a [rule](/guide/approvals/rules) so similar actions stop asking), or **Decline**.

    **Success state:** the agent continues in the conversation, and the item leaves **Needs you**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Item | What it means | Where it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Chat approval** | An agent paused for your sign-off, per your [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) settings | The conversation |
| **Subagent waiting** | A [subagent](/guide/agents/overview#subagents) needs your input | The conversation |
| **Runbook approval** | Resolve wants to run a [runbook](/guide/incident/runbooks), a predefined fix procedure | The incident |
| **Keeper approval** | A scheduled Optimize check proposes an action | The conversation |
| **Incident decision** | Resolve finished investigating and needs your call | The incident |
| **Ready to merge** | A merge request passed [Review](/guide/code-review/overview) | Its page in Review |
| **Connection error** | A [connection](/guide/connections/overview) lost access | **Connections** |

Items older than 30 days drop off the card. The work itself stays in its product.

## Read the Products card

The **Products** card has one tile per product your role can see. Click a tile to open that product.

| Product | Status line examples | What it counts |
|---|---|---|
| [Optimize](/guide/cost-optimization/overview) | `$1.2K/mo to save`, `No savings found` | Monthly savings agents found. This is potential, not money already saved |
| [Review](/guide/code-review/overview) | `3 reviews queued`, `No reviews queued` | Merge requests waiting for review |
| [Cyber](/guide/security/cyber-overview) | `2 open criticals`, `Connect an app` | Open critical findings. **Connect an app** shows before your first app |
| [Resolve](/guide/incident/overview) | `1 decision waiting`, `No decisions waiting` | Incidents where the investigation is done and waits for you |

## Check today's automation runs

The **Automation today** card lists each [automation](/guide/automation/automations) scheduled or run today, with a count such as `5 runs · 1 failed`. Rows call out failures and runs waiting for approval. Click **Manage** to open Automations.

For anything older than today:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Runs list">
    Click **Automations** in the sidebar, then the **Runs** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a run">
    Click a run to see its chat. Statuses are **Pending**, **Running**, **Succeeded**, **Failed**, **Required Approval**, and **Cancelled**.

    **Success state:** the run's conversation opens, showing what the agent did and any action still waiting for approval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Respond to the incident banner

When incidents are live, a banner sits above everything else on Home and shows up to the three most severe. Hover over it to fan out the stack, then click an incident to open it. No live incidents means no banner. Incidents in the banner aren't repeated in **Needs you**.

## Find a past report in Activity

The bell in the top bar opens a quick panel with **Notifications** and **Alerts** tabs. For anything older or harder to find, use the full Activity page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Activity">
    Go to [app.cloudthinker.io/activity](https://app.cloudthinker.io/activity). Reports are grouped by day, newest first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Type in **Search activity**, or click the filter icon and set **Read state** (**All** or **Unread**), **Category** (a product), and **Workspace** (shown when you belong to more than one). Each active filter shows as a chip above the list. Click a chip to clear it.

    **Success state:** the list shows only matching reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open or tidy a report">
    Click a row to open the finding, review, incident, or conversation it reports on. Use the row menu to **Mark as read**, **Mark as unread**, or **Archive**, or click **Mark all read** to clear the unread count.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To choose which events reach you by email, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or Google Chat, see [Notifications](/guide/notifications).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A teammate sees an item I don't">
    Home and Activity only show what your role can view. An incident decision, for example, appears only if you can view Resolve. Ask a workspace admin to check your [role](/guide/workspace-users).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An item left Needs you without anyone acting on it">
    Items older than 30 days drop off the card automatically. Open the product it came from to find it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A card is missing from Home">
    A card hides when its data can't load, instead of showing an error. **Automation today** also needs permission to view automations. Reload the page, then ask an admin about your role.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
