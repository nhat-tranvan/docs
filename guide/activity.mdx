Home shows what's waiting on you today, and Activity is the searchable inbox of everything agents have reported.

## Where things show up

| You want to | Go to |
|---|---|
| Answer an approval or decision an agent is waiting on | Home → **Needs you** |
| See which products have work queued | Home → **Products** |
| Confirm today's scheduled work ran | Home → **Automation today** |
| See live incidents | The incident banner at the top of Home |
| Find an older report, finding, or alert | **Activity** at [app.cloudthinker.io/activity](https://app.cloudthinker.io/activity) (bookmark it: there's no sidebar link) |
| See every run of every automation | **Automations → Runs** |

Each item only appears if your [role](/guide/workspace-users) can view the product it belongs to.

<Note>
A Home card whose data can't load hides instead of showing an error. If a card is missing, see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).
</Note>

## Clear what needs you

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Home">
    Click **Home** in the sidebar, or sign in. The **Needs you** card shows up to four items, newest ask first. There's no overflow list: older asks stay in the product they came from, and each one moves up as you clear the newer ones. Each row names the item, the kind of ask, and how long it has waited.

    **Success state:** you see the card with rows, or the line *Nothing is waiting on you.* An empty card is the goal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an item">
    Click a row. It opens the place where you answer (see the table below). The row's action reads **Review** for approvals and decisions, **Open** for everything else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer it">
    For a chat approval, the action card shows what the agent wants to run. Click **Approve**, **Approve and draft rule** (approves now and drafts a [rule](/guide/approvals/rules) so similar actions stop asking), or **Decline**. For other items, follow the **What you do** column below.

    **Success state:** the agent continues in the conversation, and the item leaves **Needs you**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Item | What it means | Where it opens | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chat approval** | An agent paused for your sign-off, per your [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) settings | The conversation | Answer the action card |
| **Subagent waiting** | A [subagent](/guide/agents/overview#subagents) needs your input | The conversation | Answer it in the **Agents** tab of the side panel |
| **Runbook approval** | Resolve wants to run a [runbook](/guide/incident/runbooks), a predefined fix procedure | The incident | Check the runbook's steps, then approve or decline them |
| **Keeper approval** | A keeper (a scheduled scan that checks your cloud against detection rules) paused before an action | The conversation | Answer the action card |
| **Incident decision** | A Resolve investigation found a cause, paused for your input, or stopped, and needs your call | The incident | Review the cause and remediation suggestions, or answer what CloudThinker asked |
| **Ready to merge** | A pull request (merge request on GitLab) passed [Review](/guide/code-review/overview) with nothing left unresolved | Its page in Review | Merge it in your Git host |
| **Connection error** | A [connection](/guide/connections/overview) lost access | **Connections** | Fix the credential, then click **Retry** |

Items older than 30 days drop off the card. The work itself stays in its product.

## Read the Products card

The **Products** card has one tile per product your role can see: Optimize, Review, Cyber, and Resolve. Automation has its own card, below. Click a tile to open that product.

| Product | Status line examples | What it counts |
|---|---|---|
| [Optimize](/guide/cost-optimization/overview) | `$1.2K/mo to save`, `No savings found` | Monthly savings agents found. This is potential, not money already saved |
| [Review](/guide/code-review/overview) | `3 reviews queued`, `No reviews queued` | Open pull requests CloudThinker hasn't finished reviewing |
| [Cyber](/guide/security/cyber-overview) | `2 open criticals`, `Connect an app` | Open critical findings. **Connect an app** shows before your first app |
| [Resolve](/guide/incident/overview) | `1 decision waiting`, `No decisions waiting` | Incidents where the investigation is done and waits for you |

## Check today's automation runs

The **Automation today** card lists each [automation](/guide/automation/automations) scheduled or run today, with a count such as `5 runs · 1 failed`. Rows call out failures and runs waiting for approval. Click **Manage** to open Automations.

For anything older than today:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Runs list">
    Click **Automations** in the sidebar, then the **Runs** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a run">
    Click a run to see its chat.

    **Success state:** the run's conversation opens, showing what the agent did and any action still waiting for approval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | The run is queued and hasn't started | Nothing yet |
| **Running** | The agent is working | Nothing. Open it to follow along |
| **Required Approval** | The run paused on a write that waits for an approver | Open the run and answer the action card |
| **Succeeded** | The run finished | Read the result in its chat |
| **Failed** | The run stopped on an error | Open its chat to see where it stopped |
| **Cancelled** | The run was stopped before it finished | Nothing, or run the automation again |

## Respond to the incident banner

When incidents are live, a banner sits above everything else on Home and shows up to the three most severe. Hover over it to fan out the stack, then click an incident to open it. No live incidents means no banner. Incidents in the banner aren't repeated in **Needs you**.

## Find a past report in Activity

The bell in the top bar opens a quick panel with **Notifications** and **Alerts** tabs. For anything older or harder to find, use the full Activity page.

<Note>
Neither the bell panel nor the sidebar links to Activity. Open it by URL and bookmark it.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Activity">
    Go to [app.cloudthinker.io/activity](https://app.cloudthinker.io/activity). Reports are grouped by day, newest first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Type in **Search activity**, or click the filter icon and set **Read state** (**All** or **Unread**), **Category** (a product), and **Workspace** (shown when you belong to more than one). Each active filter shows as a chip above the list. Click a chip to clear it.

    **Success state:** the list shows only matching reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open or tidy a report">
    Click a row to open the finding, review, incident, or conversation it reports on. Use the row menu to **Mark as read**, **Mark as unread**, or **Archive**, or click **Mark all read** to clear the unread count.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To choose which events reach you by email, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or Google Chat, see [Notifications](/guide/notifications).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A teammate sees an item I don't">
    Home and Activity only show what your role can view. An incident decision, for example, appears only if you can view Resolve. Ask a workspace admin to check your [role](/guide/workspace-users).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An item left Needs you without anyone acting on it">
    It was older than 30 days. See [Clear what needs you](#clear-what-needs-you). Open the product it came from to find it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A card is missing from Home">
    A card hides when its data can't load, instead of showing an error. A working card with nothing in it never hides: **Needs you** reads *Nothing is waiting on you.* and **Automation today** reads *Nothing is scheduled for today.* **Automation today** also needs permission to view automations. Reload the page, then ask an admin about your role. If the card is still missing, email [support@cloudthinker.io](mailto:support@cloudthinker.io) with the card name and your workspace.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
