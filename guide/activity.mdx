Home shows a timeline of what happened and what's coming in your workspace, above the chat box. The **Inbox** collects everything waiting on you: decisions, approvals, notifications, and alerts.

## Where things show up

| You want to | Go to |
|---|---|
| See what changed, broke, or ran in the last hours, and what's scheduled next | Home → **Workspace activity** |
| Make a decision an app is waiting on | **Inbox → Needs you** |
| Approve or decline a change an agent wants to make | **Inbox → Approvals** |
| Find an agent report, finding, or review update | **Inbox → Notifications** |
| Acknowledge an alert | **Inbox → Alerts** |
| See every run of one automation | **Automations**, then the automation's **Past runs** |

Each item only appears if your [role](/guide/workspace-users) can view the product it belongs to.

## Read the Workspace activity chart

Click **New chat** at the top of the sidebar, or sign in, to open Home. The **Workspace activity** chart sits above the chat box.

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Lanes | **Changes**, **New issues**, **Investigations**, **Alerts**, and **Planned** work, one row each |
| **Now** line | Past events sit to its left, upcoming work to its right |
| **24h** / **3h** | The time range: 12 hours on each side of now, or 2.5 hours before and 30 minutes after |
| Legend | Critical and High events in their severity color, **Other activity**, and **Scheduled** work as an outline |

Drag across a lane to zoom into that stretch of time. Click a mark to see the events behind it and open one. Times show in your browser's time zone.

If the chart can't load, Home shows **Could not load workspace activity** with a retry button. You can still start a chat below it.

## Work through the Inbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Inbox">
    Click **Inbox** in the sidebar. The row shows a count when something needs you. The page has four tabs, each with its own count: **Needs you**, **Approvals**, **Notifications**, and **Alerts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Type in the tab's search box, or use its filters:

    | Tab | Filters |
    |---|---|
    | **Needs you** | Source app, such as Resolve, Optimize, Review, or Cyber |
    | **Notifications** | **Read state** (**All** or **Unread**), **Source**, and **Workspace** (**This workspace** or **All workspaces**) when you belong to more than one |
    | **Alerts** | **Status** (**All** or **Open**), and **Workspace** when you belong to more than one |

    **Success state:** the list shows only matching items. Click **Clear filters** to see everything again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an item">
    Click a row to open it in the preview pane next to the list. The pane shows the item's details and a button to open it where you act, such as **Open in Resolve**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on it">
    On **Approvals**, check **What will run**, then click **Approve** or **Decline**. Declining asks for an optional **Reason** that the requester sees. On **Notifications**, use **Mark read**, **Mark unread**, **Archive**, or **Mark all read**. On **Alerts**, use **Acknowledge** or **Acknowledge all**.

    **Success state:** the item leaves the tab, and its count drops. An empty tab reads, for example, **Nothing needs you**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Approvals** tab groups requests into **Waiting for you**, **You sent · waiting for approval**, **Approved · applying**, and **Decided**. Which changes wait for a person depends on your [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) setup.

To choose which events reach you by email, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or Google Chat, click the settings icon (**Notification settings**) at the top of the Inbox, or see [Notifications](/guide/notifications).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A teammate sees an item I don't">
    Home and the Inbox only show what your role can view. A Resolve decision, for example, appears only if you can view Resolve. Ask a workspace admin to check your [role](/guide/workspace-users).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A tab says Could not load">
    Nothing was changed. Click **Retry** after a moment. If a search fails, the Inbox keeps what you typed and shows **Could not search. Your search is kept.**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't find the Activity page">
    Activity is now the Inbox. Old `/activity` links open the Inbox.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
