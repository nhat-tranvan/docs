**Potential monthly savings** adds up the monthly estimates on every open finding (one savings opportunity a scan found) for one cloud account. Use it to size the work in front of you, not as a promise of a smaller bill.

## Where the number appears

Every Optimize page shows one account at a time. To switch, open the account menu next to the page title (it shows the current account's name) and pick another. Totals use the account's billing currency: USD on AWS and GCP, and the billing scope's currency on Azure.

| Location | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Optimize** (Overview), **Potential monthly savings** card | Every open, actionable finding for the selected account. The line under it shows how many open findings make up the total, and **Review opportunities** opens **Findings** for that account. |
| **Optimize → Findings**, summary cards | The same total, plus separate **Quick wins** and **Needs review** cards, each with its own count and `/mo potential` amount. |

There's no savings total across accounts. To see them side by side, generate a **Savings opportunities** report with **All accounts** scope on [Reports](/guide/cost-optimization/reports).

On **Findings**, the summary follows the **Service** filter, so picking one service shows that service's share. The **Quick wins** and **Needs review** cards, search, the effort and risk filters, and paging don't change the summary, so the header stays stable while you browse.

Each summary card on **Findings** also compares the current total with the total recorded when the last scan completed. In this line, "actions" means counted findings:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `+2 actions, +$X/mo vs last scan` | Two more counted findings, and the total went up |
| `-1 action, -$X/mo vs last scan` | One fewer counted finding, and the total went down |
| **No change since last scan** | Same count and amount |
| **No previous scan baseline** | The account has only one completed scan |

The delta moves for any reason the total moves: new findings, findings you moved or marked, edited estimates, and lapsed snoozes.

Run a new scan after your team ships changes, or after you dispute findings, since the agent re-checks disputed findings then. **Re-scan** on **Overview** and on **Findings** start the same account scan, and the comparison then measures against the total it records. See [Refresh findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations#refresh-findings).

## How estimates are calculated

During a scan, CloudThinker reads the account's billed spend for each service and inspects the resources behind it with read-only commands. The permissions it uses are listed per provider: [AWS](/guide/connections/aws#required-permissions), [GCP](/guide/connections/gcp#required-permissions), [Azure](/guide/connections/azure#required-permissions). Each estimate comes from those measurements, not from a fixed rate card, and each finding's write-up shows the arithmetic.

For example, two idle NAT gateways billed at $33/mo each give a $66/mo estimate, with the gateway IDs and the measured traffic as evidence. The figures are illustrative.

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Unit | Per month. Yearly or term savings are divided by 12 |
| Spend basis | What you're actually billed. On AWS that's net unblended cost, so discounts and existing RI or Savings Plans coverage are already reflected |
| Billing source and currency | Differ by provider. See [Provider differences](/guide/cost-optimization/overview#provider-differences) |
| Where to check the math | Open the finding. The **Overview** tab in its detail panel shows the evidence, the estimate, and the affected resource. **Open analysis** shows the read-only commands the scan ran |

Check a finding's evidence badge before you quote its number to finance. **Verified** is the one to quote; **Inferred** and **Assumed** leave something the scan didn't read, so confirm that part yourself. See [how to read a finding](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations#how-to-read-a-finding).

## Which findings count

A finding counts toward the total only when all three conditions hold.

| Condition | Counted | Not counted |
|---|---|---|
| Class | **Quick win**, **Needs review** | **No action** (the scan checked the spend and proposes no change) |
| Status | **Pending**, **In Progress** | **Implemented**, **Ignored** |
| Mark | No mark, a snooze whose date has passed, or an expected mark whose expiry date has passed | **Mark as expected** with no expiry date or one still ahead, an active **Snooze until…**, **Dispute…** |

See [how to read a finding](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations#how-to-read-a-finding) for how a scan picks a class and an evidence badge.

<Note>
Disputed findings stay visible and flagged, but their savings leave the total. CloudThinker re-checks them first on the next scan. If your dispute was right, the agent archives the finding: it leaves the board and the total for good, without moving to a status column. Otherwise the evidence is refreshed and the **Disputed** mark stays until you select **Clear mark**.
</Note>

## Why it is an upper bound

Two findings can estimate savings against the same spend. Say a rightsizing finding saves $200/mo and a Savings Plan finding on the same compute saves $150/mo. After you rightsize, the plan covers a smaller footprint, so doing both saves less than $350. Optimize doesn't flag overlapping findings for you.

In practice:

- Don't subtract the total from the month-end forecast on the **`<Month> spend`** card on Overview (for example **October spend**). The forecast says where the bill is heading; the savings total says what open work might be worth.
- Don't report the total as realized savings or ROI. Optimize doesn't measure savings after a change.
- Before adding two estimates together, open both findings and check whether they touch the same resources.
- Prioritize by class, effort, and risk, not by the dollar amount alone.

## Keep the total accurate

Work findings off the total as your team decides on them. Each change updates the total for everyone, so the number is only as honest as the board.

| When your team | Do this on **Findings** | What the total does |
|---|---|---|
| Starts work on a finding | Move it to **In Progress** | Nothing. The finding still counts |
| Ships the change | Move it to **Implemented** | Drops by the finding's estimate |
| Decides against the change | Move it to **Ignored** | Drops by the finding's estimate |
| Knows the spend is intentional, or wants to revisit it later | **Mark as expected**, or **Snooze until…** a date | Drops, then counts again once an expiry or wake date passes |
| Thinks the finding is wrong | **Dispute…** it | Drops, while the finding stays visible for the next scan to re-check |
| Knows the real figure | **Edit** the finding and change **Potential savings ($/mo)** | Follows your figure from then on |

[Findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations) covers how to move, mark, and edit a finding, and what each mark asks you for. Moving and editing need the workspace Developer role or higher, and marking a finding as expected, snoozed, or disputed needs Admin. Organization Owners and Admins hold workspace Admin in every workspace.

<Tip>
Optimize keeps no record of realized savings. Before you move a finding to **Implemented**, note its estimate in your ticket or select **Download PDF** on **Findings**. Then confirm the real reduction as shown in [Prove savings after a change](#prove-savings-after-a-change).
</Tip>

## Send findings to your workflow

For a file to attach to a review, select **Download PDF** on **Findings** or on [Cost Health](/guide/cost-optimization/overview#key-concepts). For a written report now or on a schedule, use [Reports](/guide/cost-optimization/reports). For a recurring summary, use one of the two Optimize digests.

| Digest | Sent | Covers | Goes to | Email by default |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Optimize weekly digest** | Mondays | Cost Health, new findings, expiring commitments, and anomalies not yet reviewed | The [workspace owner](/guide/security/roles). A workspace the organization owns may have none | Off |
| **Optimize monthly digest** | The 2nd | Last month's spend, new findings, anomalies not yet reviewed, Cost Health | Workspace Admins, including organization Owners and Admins | On |

Turn email, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or Google Chat on or off per digest on [Notifications](/guide/notifications).

Optimize has no built-in button that opens a ticket or a pull request from a finding, and no CSV export of findings.

## Prove savings after a change

Optimize reports potential savings only. To show a real reduction:

1. Wait for a complete billing month after the change. A partial month understates the result, and provider billing data can lag (AWS Cost Explorer by 24 to 48 hours).
2. In **Optimize → [Cost Explorer](/guide/cost-optimization/analytics)**, compare the last full month before the change with the first full month after it. **Group by** takes one dimension at a time: start with **Service** to find the service that dropped, then switch to **Region** to see where it landed.

Optimize's Cost Explorer doesn't break spend down by resource or tag. To isolate one resource, use your provider's own tools: resource-level AWS Cost Explorer or the Cost and Usage Report, the BigQuery billing export on GCP, or Azure Cost Management.

## Troubleshooting

[Optimize troubleshooting](/guide/cost-optimization/troubleshooting#findings-and-the-savings-total) lists every savings-total symptom with its cause, check, and fix. These two need more detail.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The total changed and nobody ran a scan">
    **Cause:** the total is shared across the workspace, so anyone's decision moves it. It goes up when a snooze date passes, a teammate clears a mark, a teammate moves a finding from **Implemented** or **Ignored** back to **Pending** or **In Progress**, or a teammate raises an edited estimate. It goes down when a teammate moves a finding to **Implemented** or **Ignored**, marks it, or lowers its estimate, and when a scan upholds a dispute.

    **Check:** open the finding. Its **Activity** tab lists status changes and edits, and a marked finding shows who set the mark and when. Select the **hidden** toggle in the **Findings** toolbar to see marked findings.

    **Fix:** move, edit, or clear the mark again if the change was a mistake.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The total is $0, too high, or doesn't match Overview">
    **Cause:** at $0, no finding meets all three conditions in [Which findings count](#which-findings-count), so the board is probably all **No action**, marked, or already **Implemented** or **Ignored**. A total that looks too high usually has findings estimating savings against the same spend. A **Findings** total below **Overview** means a **Service** filter is set.

    **Check:** the **Quick wins** and **Needs review** cards, the status columns, and the filter bar above the board.

    **Fix:** clear the **Service** filter so the two totals match, and compare the resources on large findings before you add them up. See [Why it is an upper bound](#why-it-is-an-upper-bound).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
