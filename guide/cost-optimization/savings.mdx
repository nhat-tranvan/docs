**Potential monthly savings** adds up the monthly estimates on every open finding (one savings opportunity a scan found) for one cloud account. Use it to size the work in front of you, not as a promise of a smaller bill.

Every Optimize page shows one account at a time. To switch, open the account menu next to the page title (it shows the current account's name) and pick another. Totals use the account's billing currency: USD on AWS and GCP, and the billing scope's currency on Azure.

## Where the number appears

| Location | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Optimize** (Overview), **Potential monthly savings** card | Every open, actionable finding for the selected account. The line under it shows how many open findings make up the total. |
| **Optimize → Findings**, summary cards | The same total, plus separate **Quick wins** and **Needs review** cards, each with its own count and `/mo potential` amount. |

For example, an account might show **$4,320** and **18 open findings** on Overview. On **Findings**, that splits into **Quick wins** at $1,100/mo and **Needs review** at $3,220/mo. The figures are illustrative.

There's no savings total across accounts. To see every account in one place, generate a **Savings opportunities** report on [Reports](/guide/cost-optimization/reports) with **All accounts** scope. Each account gets its own section.

On **Findings**, the summary follows the **Service** filter, so picking one service shows that service's share. The **Quick wins** and **Needs review** cards (Findings has no class tabs), search, the effort and risk filters, and paging don't change the summary, so the header stays stable while you browse.

Each summary card on **Findings** also compares the current total with the total recorded when the last scan completed. In this line, "actions" means counted findings:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `+2 actions, +$X/mo vs last scan` | Two more counted findings, and the total went up |
| `-1 action, -$X/mo vs last scan` | One fewer counted finding, and the total went down |
| **No change since last scan** | Same count and amount |
| **No previous scan baseline** | The account has only one completed scan |

The delta moves for any reason the total moves: new findings, findings you moved or marked, edited estimates, and lapsed snoozes.

Run a new scan after your team ships changes, or after you dispute findings, since the agent re-checks disputed findings on the next scan. Select **Re-scan** on **Overview** or on **Findings**. Both start the same account scan, and the comparison then measures against the total recorded at that scan. An account scan can take tens of minutes. **Open analysis** opens the latest scan's conversation, while it runs and after. While a scan you started is running, the scan button itself reads **Open CloudThinker's conversation** and opens the same place. The button reads **Run scan** until the account's first scan, then **Re-scan**.

## How estimates are calculated

During a scan, CloudThinker reads the account's billed spend for each service and inspects the resources behind it with read-only commands. The permissions it uses are listed per provider: [AWS](/guide/connections/aws#required-permissions), [GCP](/guide/connections/gcp#required-permissions), [Azure](/guide/connections/azure#required-permissions). Each estimate comes from those measurements, not from a fixed rate card, and each finding's write-up shows the arithmetic.

For example, a finding for two idle NAT gateways billed at $33/mo each would show an estimate of $66/mo, with the gateway IDs and the traffic it measured as evidence. The figures are illustrative.

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Unit | Per month. Yearly or term savings are divided by 12 |
| Spend basis | What you're actually billed. On AWS that's net unblended cost, so discounts and existing RI or Savings Plans coverage are already reflected |
| Billing source and currency | Differ by provider. See [Provider differences](/guide/cost-optimization/overview#provider-differences) |
| Where to check the math | Open the finding. The **Overview** tab in its detail panel shows the evidence, the estimate, and the affected resource. **Open analysis** shows the read-only commands the scan ran |

Check a finding's evidence badge before you quote its number to finance. **Verified** means the scan read the live resource and confirmed the values, so it's the one to quote. **Inferred** comes from billing and configuration data without reading the resource, and **Assumed** means part of it wasn't checked, so confirm those yourself first. See [Findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations#how-to-read-a-finding).

## Which findings count

A finding counts toward the total only when all three conditions hold.

| Condition | Counted | Not counted |
|---|---|---|
| Class | **Quick win**, **Needs review** | **No action** (the scan checked the spend and proposes no change) |
| Status | **Pending**, **In Progress** | **Implemented**, **Ignored** |
| Mark | No mark, a snooze whose date has passed, or an expected mark whose expiry date has passed | **Mark as expected** with no expiry date or one still ahead, an active **Snooze until…**, **Dispute…** |

A finding is a **Quick win** only when it is reversible, low effort, and backed by verified evidence. A finding that misses any one of those is **Needs review**. See [Findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations) for how classes and evidence badges work.

<Note>
Disputed findings stay visible on the board, flagged for a re-check, but their savings drop out of the total. On the next scan, CloudThinker re-checks disputed findings first. If your dispute was right, the agent archives the finding: it leaves the board and the total for good, without moving to a status column. Otherwise it updates the finding with fresh evidence and the **Disputed** mark stays. That mark never clears by itself: select **Clear mark** once you accept the result.
</Note>

## Why it is an upper bound

Two findings can estimate savings against the same spend. Say a rightsizing finding saves $200/mo and a Savings Plan finding on the same compute saves $150/mo. After you rightsize, the plan covers a smaller footprint, so doing both saves less than $350. Optimize doesn't flag overlapping findings for you.

In practice:

- Don't subtract the total from the month-end forecast on the **`<Month> spend`** card on Overview (for example **October spend**). The forecast says where the bill is heading. The savings total says what open work might be worth.
- Don't report the total as realized savings or ROI. Optimize doesn't measure post-change savings.
- Before adding two estimates together, open both findings and check whether they touch the same resources.
- Prioritize by class, effort, and risk, not by the dollar amount alone.

## Keep the total accurate

Work findings off the total as your team decides on them. Each change updates the total for everyone in the workspace. Moving and editing findings need the workspace Developer role or higher, and marking a finding as expected, snoozed, or disputed needs Admin. Organization Owners and Admins hold workspace Admin in every workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account's findings">
    Go to **Optimize**, pick the account in the account menu next to the page title, and select **Review opportunities** on the **Potential monthly savings** card. This opens **Findings** for that account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move work through the board">
    Findings move through four statuses: **Pending** and **In Progress** are open and count toward the total, **Implemented** and **Ignored** don't.

    In the **Table** view, select the **…** button at the end of a finding's row and choose **Move to → In Progress** while someone works on it. In the **Kanban** view, drag the card to that column instead. The finding stays in the total.

    When the change ships, move it to **Implemented**. If you decide not to do it, move it to **Ignored**.

    **Success state:** the finding leaves the open columns and the total drops by its estimate.

    <Tip>
    Optimize keeps no record of realized savings. Before you move a finding to **Implemented**, note its estimate in your ticket or select **Download PDF** on **Findings**. Then confirm the real reduction as shown in [Prove savings after a change](#prove-savings-after-a-change).
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark findings that aren't real savings">
    In the **Table** view, use the same **…** menu. In **Kanban**, cards have no menu: open the card and select **Mark finding** at the bottom of its detail panel. Pick the mark that fits:

    | Mark | Use it when | On the board | Counts toward total |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | **Mark as expected** | The spend is intentional, such as a reserved test fleet | Hidden by default, or until the expiry date passes if you set one | No, then Yes once an expiry date passes |
    | **Snooze until…** | You want to revisit it on a set date | Hidden until the date passes | No, then Yes once the date passes |
    | **Dispute…** | The finding looks wrong and the agent should re-check it | Stays visible, flagged | No |

    Each mark asks for a reason of at least a short sentence. **Snooze until…** also needs a wake date, from tomorrow onward. **Mark as expected** takes an optional expiry date. Once it passes, the finding comes back to the board and counts again, like a lapsed snooze. The agent reads your dispute reason when it re-checks.

    **Success state:** the total no longer includes the finding's estimate. To review hidden findings later, select the **hidden** toggle in the **Findings** toolbar (for example **3 hidden**). To undo a mark, choose **Clear mark** in the row menu, or **Change mark** in the finding's detail panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct an estimate">
    If your team knows the real number, open the finding, select **Edit** at the bottom of its detail panel (next to **Mark finding**), and change **Potential savings ($/mo)**.

    **Success state:** the total reflects the new figure and the change shows on the finding's **Activity** tab.

    Later scans never overwrite an edited value. If a scan measures a different figure, CloudThinker reports it in the scan's conversation instead. Select **Open analysis** on **Overview** or **Findings** to read it. There's no button that restores the scanned figure. To go back, edit the value again using the figure from that conversation. When the resource changes, re-check your edit yourself, or the total keeps the old number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send findings to your workflow

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| A regular summary in chat or email | The Optimize weekly or monthly digest. See [Notifications](/guide/notifications) |
| A file to attach to a review | **Download PDF** on **Findings**, or on [Cost Health](/guide/cost-optimization/overview#key-concepts) |
| A written report for a team or a schedule | [Reports](/guide/cost-optimization/reports) |

| Digest | Sent | Goes to | Email by default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Optimize weekly digest** | Mondays: Cost Health, new findings, expiring commitments, new anomalies not yet reviewed, and a count of older ones still waiting for review | The workspace owner | Off |
| **Optimize monthly digest** | The 2nd: last month's spend, new findings, new anomalies not yet reviewed (plus a count of older ones still waiting), Cost Health | Workspace Admins, including organization Owners and Admins | On |

Turn email, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or Google Chat on or off per digest on [Notifications](/guide/notifications).

Optimize has no built-in button that opens a ticket or a pull request from a finding, and no CSV export of findings.

## Prove savings after a change

Optimize reports potential savings only. To show a real reduction:

1. Wait for a complete billing month after the change. A partial month understates the result, and provider billing data can lag (AWS Cost Explorer by 24 to 48 hours).
2. In **Optimize → [Cost Explorer](/guide/cost-optimization/analytics)**, compare the last full month before the change with the first full month after it. **Group by** takes one dimension at a time: start with **Service** to find the service that dropped, then switch to **Region** to see where it landed.

Optimize's Cost Explorer doesn't break spend down by resource or tag. To isolate one resource, use your provider's tools: AWS Cost Explorer with resource-level data or the Cost and Usage Report on AWS, the BigQuery billing export on GCP, or Azure Cost Management on Azure.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The total is $0 but the board has findings">
    No finding meets all three conditions in [Which findings count](#which-findings-count). The visible findings are probably **No action**, marked (expected, snoozed, or disputed), or already **Implemented** or **Ignored**, so no **Pending** or **In Progress** finding is left. Check the class tabs on **Findings**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The account has never been scanned">
    The total stays at $0 until the first scan finishes. Select **Run scan** on **Overview**. After the first scan, the button reads **Re-scan**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Findings total doesn't match Overview">
    A **Service** filter is set on **Findings**. Clear it and the two totals match.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The total went up without a new scan">
    The total is shared across the workspace, so any of these raises it:

    - A snooze date passed. Snoozed findings count again automatically.
    - A teammate cleared a mark.
    - A teammate moved a finding from **Implemented** or **Ignored** back to **Pending** or **In Progress**.
    - A teammate raised an edited estimate.

    Open the finding to see who changed it. Its **Activity** tab lists status changes and edits, and a marked finding shows who set the mark and when.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The total dropped and I didn't change anything">
    A teammate probably moved a finding to **Implemented** or **Ignored**, marked it, or lowered its estimate. Check the **Implemented** and **Ignored** columns, and select the **hidden** toggle in the **Findings** toolbar, then open the finding: its **Activity** tab lists status changes and edits, and a mark shows who set it. A dispute the agent upheld on a scan also removes a finding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The total looks too high">
    Some findings may estimate savings against the same spend. See [Why it is an upper bound](#why-it-is-an-upper-bound), and compare the resources on large findings before you add them up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't move, mark, or edit a finding">
    Moving and editing a finding need the workspace Developer role or higher. Marking a finding as expected, snoozed, or disputed needs Admin. Viewers can read findings but not change them. Ask a workspace Admin to change your role, or to apply the mark for you. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The card says No previous scan baseline">
    The comparison needs a previous completed scan for that account. It appears after the next scan finishes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
