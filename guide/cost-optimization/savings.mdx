**Potential monthly savings** adds up the monthly estimates on every open finding (one savings opportunity a scan found) for one cloud account. Use it to size the work in front of you, not as a promise of a smaller bill.

## Where the number appears

| Location | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Optimize** (Overview), middle card | Every open, actionable finding for the selected account. The line under it shows how many open findings make up the total. |
| **Optimize → Findings**, summary cards | The same total, plus separate **Quick wins** and **Needs review** cards, each with its own count and `/mo potential` amount. |

There's no savings total across accounts. To see every account in one place, generate a **Savings opportunities** report on [Reports](/guide/cost-optimization/reports) with **All accounts** scope. Each account gets its own section.

On **Findings**, the summary follows the **Service** filter, so picking one service shows that service's share. Class tabs, search, the effort and risk filters, and paging don't change the summary, so the header stays stable while you browse.

Each summary card also compares the current total with the total saved at the last completed scan:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `+2 actions, +$X/mo vs last scan` | The total went up |
| `-1 action, -$X/mo vs last scan` | The total went down |
| **No change since last scan** | Same count and amount |
| **No previous scan baseline** | The account has only one completed scan |

The delta moves for any reason the total moves: new findings, findings you moved or marked, edited estimates, and lapsed snoozes. To get a fresh baseline, select **Re-scan** on **Findings**.

## How estimates are calculated

There's no fixed formula. During a scan, CloudThinker reads the account's billed spend for each service, inspects the resources with read-only commands, and writes each finding's estimate from what it measured.

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Unit | USD per month. Yearly or term savings are divided by 12 |
| Spend basis | What you're actually billed. On AWS that's net unblended cost, so discounts and existing RI or Savings Plans coverage are already reflected |
| Where to check the math | The finding's **Overview** tab shows the evidence, the estimate, and the affected resource. **Open analysis** shows the read-only commands the scan ran |

Check a finding's evidence before you quote its number to finance. See [Findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations) for evidence badges.

## Which findings count

A finding counts toward the total only when all three conditions hold.

| Condition | Counted | Not counted |
|---|---|---|
| Class | **Quick win**, **Needs review** | **No action** (the scan checked the spend and proposes no change) |
| Status | **Pending**, **In Progress** | **Implemented**, **Ignored** |
| Mark | No mark, or a snooze whose date has passed | **Mark as expected**, an active **Snooze until…**, **Dispute…** |

A finding is a **Quick win** only when it is reversible, low effort, and backed by verified evidence. A finding that misses any one of those is **Needs review**. See [Findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations) for how classes and evidence badges work.

<Note>
Disputed findings stay visible on the board, flagged for a re-check, but their savings drop out of the total until you clear the mark. A disputed estimate is unverified, so it is never counted.
</Note>

## Why it is an upper bound

Two findings can estimate savings against the same spend. For example, a rightsizing finding and a commitment coverage finding can both target the same compute. If you act on both, you save less than the two estimates added together. The product says this in the card's help text: closing every open action saves less than the total.

In practice:

- Don't subtract the total from the month-end forecast on the spend card. The forecast says where the bill is heading. The savings total says what open work might be worth.
- Don't report the total as realized savings or ROI. Optimize doesn't measure post-change savings.
- Before adding two estimates together, open both findings and check whether they touch the same resources.
- Prioritize by class, effort, and risk, not by the dollar amount alone.

## Keep the total accurate

Work findings off the total as your team decides on them. Each change updates the total for everyone in the workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account's findings">
    Go to **Optimize**, select the account, and select **Review opportunities** on the **Potential monthly savings** card. This opens **Findings** for that account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move work through the board">
    Open a finding's actions menu and choose **Move to → In Progress** while someone works on it. The finding stays in the total.

    When the change ships, choose **Move to → Implemented**. If you decide not to do it, choose **Move to → Ignored**.

    **Success state:** the finding leaves the open columns and the total drops by its estimate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark findings that aren't real savings">
    From the same menu, pick the mark that fits:

    | Mark | Use it when | On the board | Counts toward total |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | **Mark as expected** | The spend is intentional, such as a reserved test fleet | Hidden by default | No |
    | **Snooze until…** | You want to revisit it on a set date | Hidden until the date passes | No, then Yes once the date passes |
    | **Dispute…** | The finding looks wrong and the agent should re-check it | Stays visible, flagged | No |

    **Success state:** the total no longer includes the finding's estimate. To review hidden findings later, select **Show hidden findings** on the board. To undo a mark, choose **Clear mark**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct an estimate">
    If your team knows the real number, open the finding, select **Edit**, and change **Potential savings ($/mo)**. Editing needs the workspace Developer or Admin role.

    **Success state:** the total reflects the new figure and the change shows in [Activity](/guide/activity).

    Later scans never overwrite an edited value. If a scan measures a different figure, CloudThinker reports it in the scan conversation instead. When the resource changes, re-check your edit yourself, or the total keeps the old number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send findings to your workflow

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| A regular summary in chat or email | The Optimize weekly and monthly digests. They list new findings and the top action for each account, go out only when something changed, and follow your [notification](/guide/notifications) channels (email, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat) |
| A file to attach to a review | **Download PDF** on **Findings** or **Cost Health** |
| A written report for a team or a schedule | [Reports](/guide/cost-optimization/reports) |

Optimize has no built-in button that opens a ticket or a pull request from a finding, and no CSV export of findings.

## Prove savings after a change

Optimize reports potential savings only. To show a real reduction:

1. In [Cost Explorer](/guide/cost-optimization/analytics), group by service and compare the last full month before the change with the first full month after it.
2. Wait for a complete billing month after the change. A partial month or provider billing lag understates the result.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The total is $0 but the board has findings">
    The visible findings are probably **No action**, disposed (expected, snoozed, or disputed), or not yet scanned. Check the class tabs on **Findings**. If the account has never been scanned, select **Run scan** on **Optimize**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Findings total doesn't match Overview">
    A **Service** filter is set on **Findings**. Clear it and the two totals match.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The total went up without a new scan">
    A snooze date passed. Snoozed findings count again automatically once the date is behind you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't move, mark, or edit a finding">
    These actions need the workspace Developer or Admin role. Viewers can read findings but not change them. Ask a workspace Admin to change your role. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The card says No previous scan baseline">
    The comparison needs a previous completed scan for that account. It appears after the next scan finishes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
