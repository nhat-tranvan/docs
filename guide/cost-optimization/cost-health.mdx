Cost Health is a daily score out of 100 for one cloud account. CloudThinker checks the account against five FinOps capabilities and shows the evidence behind every point.

## Why Cost Health

- **One number per account.** The score, its stage, and its trend tell you whether cost practice is getting better.
- **Every point has evidence.** Each check lists the resources and spend lines that pass or fail, so you can verify the score yourself.
- **Fixes come ranked.** **What raises your score** lists the changes worth the most points first.
- **Unchecked spend never looks clean.** Spend CloudThinker couldn't check lowers coverage instead of raising the score.

## Open Cost Health

In the sidebar, select **Optimize → Cost Health**, then pick the cloud account next to the page title. The **Cost Health** card on **Overview** links to the same page.

## The five capabilities

The capabilities and their checks follow the FinOps Framework. Each check has a weight, and the weights in a capability add up to 100.

| Capability | Checks (weight) | Money it flags |
|---|---|---|
| **Rate optimization** | Commitment coverage (40), Commitment utilization (40), Commitment expiry (20) | Committable On-Demand spend |
| **Workload optimization** | Idle compute (30), Unattached storage (25), Stale snapshot (15), Previous-generation instance (15), Idle database (15) | Spend in idle or stale resources |
| **Allocation** | Tagged spend share (50), Untagged resource (30), Shared cost rule (20) | Untagged spend, which isn't money lost |
| **Anomaly management** | Anomaly review time (40), Explained anomaly (30), Spend alert (30) | Spend in anomaly gaps |
| **Forecasting** | Forecast error (50), Budget coverage (30), Forecast horizon (20) | Spend that is forecast badly |

Checks about spend lines and commitments weigh each row by its last 30 days of spend. Checks about resources, anomalies, or the account count each row once.

## How the score is set

CloudThinker measures and the scoring rules score. CloudThinker gives each resource or spend line a verdict, and the score comes from those verdicts with fixed rules. CloudThinker never picks the number itself.

1. **Coverage.** CloudThinker counts how much of the in-scope spend it could check. Below 50% coverage, a capability shows **Not measured** and gets no score.
2. **Checks.** Each check scores the passing share of its measured rows. The capability score is the weighted average of its checks.
3. **Stage.** The score maps to a FinOps maturity stage. The page shows a plain label next to it.

| Stage | Label | Meets |
|---|---|---|
| **Crawl** | **Needs work** | Measured, score under 50 |
| **Walk** | **On track** | Score 50 or more |
| **Run** | **Strong** | Score 80 or more, with 80% of spend measured |

The top of the page shows the overall score, its stage, and **% of spend measured**. It also shows the change since the last score with the capabilities that moved it, for example **Rate optimization 49 → 61**. A first score reads **First score**, and the sparkline shows the last 30 days.

## What raises your score

**What raises your score** lists the fixes worth the most points, three at a time. Select **Show n more moves** for the rest. Each card shows:

- The points the overall score would gain if you fix that gap, worked out from the same rows. **Gains may overlap**, so two fixes together can be worth less than their sum.
- The capability and how its score would change, for example **Rate optimization 0 → 40**.
- The monthly spend involved, labelled by kind.
- **Review finding** when a [finding](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations) already covers the gap, or **Ask CloudThinker** to plan the fix in a conversation.

| Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **committable** | Steady spend paid at the On-Demand rate that a commitment could cover |
| **wasted** | Spend on idle, unattached, or old resources |
| **no owner** | Spend nobody owns. Not money lost, but nobody can act on it |
| **at risk** | Spend that is not watched, budgeted, or forecast well |

Optimize never adds money across kinds, because a committable dollar and an untagged dollar mean different things.

## Read a capability

Below the moves, one card per capability shows its score, its stage, and a short summary. Select a card to open its detail.

Each check tile shows the points it earned out of its weight and one of four states:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pass** | Every measured row passes |
| **Fail** | At least one measured row fails the check |
| **Unmeasured** | CloudThinker couldn't check the rows |
| **Not scored** | The check has no rows on this account, with the reason |

Select a tile to filter the evidence to that check. The evidence table has four tabs, 25 rows a page:

| Tab | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **Gaps** | Rows that fail a check. This is the default |
| **Passing** | Rows that pass |
| **Not measured** | Rows CloudThinker couldn't check. They lower coverage, never the score |
| **Out of scope** | Rows no check applies to, such as spend no commitment can cover. They count in neither the score nor coverage |

Open a row to see its subject, the reason for its verdict, and its source. **Ask CloudThinker why** on the capability, or **Ask CloudThinker** on a row, opens a conversation about it. **Open finding** goes to the finding that covers it. The capability, check, tab, and page stay in the URL, so you can share the exact view.

## Before the first measurement

The first full scan measures the account. Until then, the page shows the provider's own score, when the provider has one.

What you may see before or between measurements:

| What you see | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **No account score yet · 1 of 3 pillars measured** | A provider score from three pillars (**Rate optimization**, **Resource efficiency**, **Cost allocation**) while CloudThinker hasn't measured the account | Select **Run full scan**. Azure has no provider score |
| A capability shows **Not measured** | Less than half of its spend could be checked | Read the reason on the capability. When a setting blocks it, a notice under the score names it, with **Open Settings** |
| **n days old** on the score | The daily run hasn't refreshed the score since then. The line below says why when the last run stopped | Check [refresh health](/guide/cost-optimization/settings#refresh-health) |

A score that isn't ready never shows as zero.

## Download and share

Select **Download PDF** in the page header to save Cost Health for the selected account as a PDF. Any workspace member can download it. To send Cost Health on a schedule, use [Optimize reports](/guide/cost-optimization/reports).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why did my score drop when nothing changed?">
    The score is measured again each day. New spend, a new resource, or an expiring commitment can fail a check. The change line under the score names the capabilities that moved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a capability Not measured on a large account?">
    CloudThinker checks the largest spend first, inside a time budget per run. When less than half of a capability's spend is checked, it gets no score. The next daily run starts where the last one stopped.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does fixing a gap raise the score at once?">
    No. The score changes after the next daily run measures the account again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
