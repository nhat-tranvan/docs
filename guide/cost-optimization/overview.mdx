Optimize reads the billing data of one cloud account at a time and turns it into a spend snapshot, a health score, and a ranked list of savings you can act on. It works with [AWS](/guide/connections/aws), [GCP](/guide/connections/gcp), and [Azure](/guide/connections/azure), and the scan that finds savings is read-only.

<OptimizeScene />

## How it works

1. **You connect a cloud account.** CloudThinker needs read access to billing data. GCP and Azure also ask you to pick a billing source (a BigQuery billing export table or an Azure billing scope) the first time.
2. **CloudThinker runs the first scan.** It pulls up to 13 months of billing history, then checks each billed service with read-only commands. The scan runs in the background, so you can leave the page.
3. **Optimize scores and ranks.** You get a Cost Health score, a list of findings (savings opportunities, each with an estimate), and a daily baseline that flags unusual cost changes.
4. **You decide what happens next.** You review each finding, ask CloudThinker to analyze or implement it, or record why it needs no action. Nothing changes in your cloud account during a scan.
5. **A daily refresh keeps numbers current.** Once cost data exists, CloudThinker pulls new billing data once a day until you turn the refresh off.

## What you can do

Every page lives under **Optimize** in the sidebar and stays scoped to the account you pick in the selector next to the page title.

| Page | What it answers | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | What did this month cost so far, where will it finish, and what should I fix first? | This page |
| **Cost Health** | Which cost practices does this account get right, which checks fail, and what raises the score? | [Key concepts](#key-concepts) |
| **Findings** | Which savings opportunities are open, how much is each worth, and who is working on it? | [Findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations) |
| **Anomalies** | Which services spent more than expected on a given day, and why? | [Anomalies](/guide/cost-optimization/anomalies) |
| **Cost Explorer** | How has spend changed over the last 6 or 12 completed months by service, region, or account? | [Cost Explorer](/guide/cost-optimization/analytics) |
| **Reports** | Can I send a written cost report now, or on a schedule? | [Reports](/guide/cost-optimization/reports) |

The **Overview** page shows three cards (month-to-date spend with a forecast, **Potential monthly savings**, and **Cost Health**), then **Start with these actions**, **Where spend goes**, and **Recent anomalies**.

## How savings are proposed and approved

Optimize never applies a saving on its own. A finding moves through a short workflow that you control.

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| Proposed | The scan saves a finding with evidence, an estimated monthly saving, effort, and risk. It's labeled **Quick win** only when the change is reversible, low effort, and backed by verified evidence. Everything else is **Needs review**. |
| Triaged | You move the finding between **Pending**, **In Progress**, **Implemented**, and **Ignored** on the Findings board or table. |
| Acted on | On a finding's page, **Actions** offers **Impact Analytics**, **Implement**, **Generate Guidelines**, and **Custom Prompt**. Each one opens a CloudThinker conversation that first checks the finding is still valid. |
| Approved | Any change CloudThinker makes to your cloud goes through your workspace's [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) rules and mode, the same as any other agent action. |

<Note>
**Potential monthly savings** is an upper bound, not a guarantee. Findings can overlap, so closing every open finding saves less than the total. See [Savings estimates](/guide/cost-optimization/savings).
</Note>

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Finding | One savings opportunity on one account, with evidence and an estimated monthly saving |
| Cost Health | A daily score out of 100 with a maturity stage (**Crawl**, **Walk**, or **Run**). The **Cost Health** page lists the passing and failing checks behind it. A score that isn't ready never shows as zero |
| Anomaly | A day where a service's finalized spend went above its expected range, measured against a rolling baseline |
| Billing source | Where Optimize reads cost data: AWS Cost Explorer, a GCP BigQuery billing export, or Azure Cost Management |
| Daily refresh | The switch in the page header (**Updated … · Daily refresh on/off**) that controls the once-a-day billing pull |

## Provider differences

| Provider | Billing source | Currency |
|---|---|---|
| AWS | AWS Cost Explorer, which can lag usage by 24 to 48 hours | USD |
| GCP | Cloud Billing Standard export in BigQuery | Converted to USD on import |
| Azure | Cost Management subscription or billing scope | The billing scope's currency |

Billing data that arrives late never shows as zero spend. Optimize keeps the last good data and shows when it was updated.
