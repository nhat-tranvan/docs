**Pays only for what you use.**

Optimize is the Frontier Investigation Agent for cloud cost: it reads the billing data of one cloud account at a time and turns it into a spend snapshot, a health score, and a ranked list of savings you can act on. It works with [AWS](/guide/connections/aws), [GCP](/guide/connections/gcp), and [Azure](/guide/connections/azure), and the scan that finds savings is read-only.

<OptimizeScene />

## How it works

1. **You connect a cloud account.** CloudThinker needs read access to billing data. GCP and Azure also ask you to pick a billing source (a BigQuery billing export table or an Azure billing scope) the first time.
2. **CloudThinker runs the first scan.** It pulls up to 13 months of billing history, then checks each billed service with read-only commands. The scan runs in the background, so you can leave the page.
3. **Optimize scores and ranks.** You get a Cost Health score, a list of findings (savings opportunities, each with an estimate), and a daily baseline that flags unusual cost changes.
4. **You decide what happens next.** You review each finding, ask CloudThinker to analyze or implement it, or record why it needs no action. Nothing changes in your cloud account during a scan.
5. **A daily refresh keeps numbers current.** Once cost data exists, CloudThinker pulls new billing data once a day. A Developer or Admin can turn it off from the **Updated** button in the page header, which shows the last update time and whether **Daily refresh** is on.

## What you can do

Every page lives under **Optimize** in the sidebar, in three groups: **Account** (Overview, Cost Health), **Actions** (Findings, Anomalies), and **Analytics** (Cost Explorer, Reports). Each page stays scoped to the account you pick in the selector next to the page title.

| Page | What it answers | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | What did this month cost so far, where will it finish, and what should I fix first? | [Quickstart, step 4](/guide/cost-optimization/quickstart) |
| **Cost Health** | Which cost practices does this account get right, which checks fail, and what raises the score? | [Cost Health](/guide/cost-optimization/cost-health) |
| **Findings** | Which savings opportunities are open, how much is each worth, and who is working on it? | [Findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations) |
| **Anomalies** | Which services spent more than expected on a given day, and why? | [Anomalies](/guide/cost-optimization/anomalies) |
| **Cost Explorer** | How has spend changed over the last 6 or 12 completed months by service or region in one account? (To compare accounts, use an **All accounts** report.) | [Cost Explorer](/guide/cost-optimization/analytics) |
| **Reports** | Can I send a written cost report now, or on a schedule? | [Reports](/guide/cost-optimization/reports) |

**Settings** holds the billing source and refresh health for an account. It isn't in the sidebar: see [Optimize settings](/guide/cost-optimization/settings).

The **Overview** page shows three cards (month-to-date spend with a forecast, **Potential monthly savings**, and **Cost Health**), then **Start with these actions**, **Where spend goes**, and **Recent anomalies**.

## How savings are proposed and approved

A scan only proposes. It saves each finding with evidence, an estimated monthly saving, effort, and risk, and nothing in your cloud account changes until someone selects **Implement** on a finding. In between, your team triages the finding on the board and picks what CloudThinker should do with it: analyze its impact, write an implementation plan, answer a question about it, or make the change. [Findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations) covers the classes, the four statuses, and each action.

**Implement** is an agent action on your cloud, so it goes through the same [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) rules and mode as any other. If every **Implement** should wait for a person, switch Approvals to **Manual**.

<Note>
**Potential monthly savings** is an upper bound, not a guarantee. Findings can overlap, so closing every open finding saves less than the total. See [Savings estimates](/guide/cost-optimization/savings).

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Finding | One savings opportunity on one account, with evidence and an estimated monthly saving |
| Cost Health | A daily score out of 100 across five FinOps capabilities: **Rate optimization**, **Workload optimization**, **Allocation**, **Anomaly management**, and **Forecasting**. Stages follow the FinOps Framework: **Crawl** (**Needs work**) under 50, **Walk** (**On track**) at 50 or more, **Run** (**Strong**) at 80 or more with 80% of spend measured. Before CloudThinker's first measurement, the card shows the provider's three-pillar score. See [Cost Health](/guide/cost-optimization/cost-health) |
| Anomaly | A day where a service's finalized spend went above its expected range, measured against a rolling baseline (the median of the previous 14 days). See [how detection works](/guide/cost-optimization/anomalies#how-detection-works) |
| Billing source | Where Optimize reads cost data: AWS Cost Explorer, a GCP BigQuery billing export, or Azure Cost Management |

## Provider differences

| Provider | Billing source | Currency |
|---|---|---|
| AWS | AWS Cost Explorer, which can lag usage by 24 to 48 hours | USD |
| GCP | Cloud Billing Standard export in BigQuery | Converted to USD on import, row by row, using the export's own `currency_conversion_rate` |
| Azure | Cost Management subscription or billing scope | The billing scope's currency |

Billing data that arrives late never shows as zero spend. Optimize keeps the last good data and shows when it was updated.

## Multi-account setups

Each cloud account is its own connection, and every Optimize page shows one account at a time.

- **AWS Organizations:** Optimize reads only the connected account's own spend, even when it's the management (payer) account. Connect each member account you want covered. See [Work across multiple AWS accounts](/guide/use-cases/multi-aws-accounts).
- **Cross-account view:** generate a report on **Optimize → Reports** with **All accounts** scope. Each account gets its own section, and different currencies are never added together. There's no single savings total across accounts.

## What Optimize covers

**Covered:**

- Savings findings on each billed service, down to resource level (idle, oversized, or unused resources)
- Commitment coverage: RI and Savings Plans on AWS, committed use discounts on GCP, reservations on Azure
- Tagging and cost allocation checks in Cost Health
- Daily anomaly detection per service

**Not covered:**

- Clouds other than AWS, GCP, and Azure
- Cost split by Kubernetes namespace or workload. Clusters show up as their billed nodes and services
- Realized savings or ROI after a change
- Cost drops. Anomaly detection flags increases only

## Data and access

- **What's stored:** the billing rows Optimize pulls, plus findings, Cost Health results, and anomaly records. They're kept in your workspace, so pages and reports load without calling your provider again.
- **Who sees it:** every workspace member can read every Optimize page. Running scans and working findings needs Developer, and connecting an account or marking a finding needs Admin. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles).

For where CloudThinker hosts data and how it's protected, see [Security](/guide/security/overview). When a scan, a billing source, or a permission fails, see [Optimize troubleshooting](/guide/cost-optimization/troubleshooting).
