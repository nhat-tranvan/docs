By the end of this page you'll have one cloud account in Optimize, its first savings findings, and a cost report you can send to a teammate. Allow **about 10 minutes**, plus however long your provider takes to return billing data.

## Prerequisites

- An AWS account, GCP project, or Azure subscription you can grant read access to. With AWS Organizations, Optimize reads only the connected account's own spend, even on the management (payer) account, so connect each account you want covered. See [Work across multiple AWS accounts](/guide/use-cases/multi-aws-accounts).
- The right [workspace role](/guide/workspace-users) for each step:

| Role | What it can do in Optimize |
|---|---|
| Admin | Connect a cloud account, plus everything a Developer can do |
| Developer | Run scans, turn the daily refresh on or off, move and edit findings, schedule reports, create share links |
| Viewer | Read every Optimize page and open reports |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Optimize and connect a cloud account">
    In the sidebar, click **Optimize**. On an empty workspace, **Overview** shows a three-step setup and a **Connect a cloud account** button (or **Connect AWS**, **Connect GCP**, **Connect Azure** when the provider is already chosen). Click it and fill in the connection dialog without leaving Optimize.

    Grant these read-only permissions. Optimize needs both the billing read and the resource read, because the scan checks each billed service with read-only commands.

    | Provider | Grant | Where |
    |---|---|---|
    | AWS | AWS managed `ReadOnlyAccess` (the [AWS setup script](/guide/connections/aws) attaches it). It includes the Cost Explorer reads Optimize needs | The IAM role in each account |
    | GCP | **Viewer**, **BigQuery Data Viewer**, and **BigQuery Job User** | The project that holds the billing export |
    | Azure | **Reader** and **Cost Management Reader**. On a Microsoft Customer Agreement, also **Billing profile reader** on the billing profile | The subscription |

    Using a custom AWS policy instead of `ReadOnlyAccess`? Add the Cost Explorer actions in [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting). See the [GCP](/guide/connections/gcp) and [Azure](/guide/connections/azure) guides for the setup commands.

    **Success state:** the page changes to **Your AWS account is connected** (or **GCP project**, **Azure subscription**) with a **Run first scan** button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a billing source (GCP and Azure only)">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="AWS">
        Nothing to do. Optimize reads the connected account through AWS Cost Explorer.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="GCP">
        Pick the **GCP billing export table** in BigQuery that holds your Cloud Billing Standard export, then click **Use this export**.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Azure">
        Pick the **Azure billing scope** (a subscription or billing scope your app registration can read with Cost Management Reader), then click **Use this scope**.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    **Success state:** the setup prompt closes and the first-scan screen appears.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run the first scan">
    Click **Run first scan**. CloudThinker pulls your billing history (up to 13 months), then checks each billed service for savings using read-only commands. Nothing in your cloud account changes.

    The scan runs in the background, so you can leave the page. To watch it work, click **Open CloudThinker's conversation**. Optimize stores the billing data it pulls in your workspace, so pages and reports load without calling your provider again.

    After this scan, CloudThinker pulls new billing data once a day. To make this a one-time look, click **Updated …** in the page header and turn **Daily refresh** off.

    **Success state:** **Overview** fills in. If no scan has finished yet, the cards show a waiting state, not zeros.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the Overview">
    Look at the three cards at the top:

    | Card | What it tells you |
    |---|---|
    | **(Current month) spend**, for example **October spend** | Gross spend so far this month, with a forecast for month end |
    | **Potential monthly savings** | The upper bound across open findings. Findings can overlap |
    | **Cost Health** | A score out of 100 with a stage: **Needs work**, **On track**, or **Strong** |

    Below them, **Start with these actions** lists the highest-value open findings, **Where spend goes** shows the biggest services from the last completed month, and **Recent anomalies** shows unusual cost changes from the last 30 days.

    **Success state:** you see a dollar figure in each card and at least one row under **Start with these actions**, or **No open cost actions** if the scan found nothing to fix.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Cost Health and your first finding">
    Click **See what raises your score** to open **Cost Health**. It lists the checks behind the score, grouped as **Gaps**, **Passing**, **Not measured**, and **Out of scope**. Fix gaps first: they're what move the score. For the pillars and stage thresholds, see [Key concepts](/guide/cost-optimization/overview#key-concepts).

    Then go back to **Overview** and click the top action. Each finding shows its estimated monthly saving, effort, and risk, plus a **Quick win** or **Needs review** label. A quick win is reversible, low effort, and backed by verified evidence.

    **Success state:** you can explain in one sentence what the top finding would change and how much it might save.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate and share a report">
    In the sidebar, click **Optimize → Reports**. Leave the scope on **Selected account** and click **Savings opportunities**. CloudThinker opens a new conversation and writes the report from your saved findings.

    To share it with teammates in your workspace, send them to the **Artifacts** gallery, where the report is saved. They sign in to read it.

    For someone outside the workspace, click the report's share icon, then **Create share link**. Anyone with that URL can open the report without signing in, and the link has no expiry: it stays public until you click **Revoke**. See [Sharing](/guide/sharing).

    <Note>
    A report shows account IDs, spend figures, resource names, and architecture details. Read it as an outsider would before you create a public link.
    </Note>

    **Success state:** a teammate opens the report from **Artifacts**, or from the share link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're done when…

- [ ] **Overview** shows spend, potential savings, and a Cost Health score for your account
- [ ] You opened at least one finding and read its evidence
- [ ] A **Savings opportunities** report exists and a teammate has opened it

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="AWS Cost Explorer access is required">
    **Cause:** the connected role can't call `ce:GetCostAndUsage`.

    **Fix:** attach `ReadOnlyAccess`, or add this policy to a custom role, then click **Retry scan**. It also grants the commitment reads, which otherwise fail quietly and make commitment coverage look fine when it isn't.

    ```json
    {
      "Version": "2012-10-17",
      "Statement": [
        {
          "Effect": "Allow",
          "Action": [
            "ce:GetCostAndUsage",
            "ce:GetCostForecast",
            "ce:GetReservationCoverage",
            "ce:GetReservationUtilization",
            "ce:GetSavingsPlansCoverage",
            "ce:GetSavingsPlansUtilization",
            "savingsplans:DescribeSavingsPlans"
          ],
          "Resource": "*"
        }
      ]
    }
    ```

    On a new AWS account, Cost Explorer must be turned on in the AWS console first, and it can take up to 24 hours to start returning data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The scan finished but there is no cost data">
    **Cause:** the provider returned no spend for the last full month or the current month, or billing data hasn't arrived yet. AWS Cost Explorer can lag usage by 24 to 48 hours.

    **Fix:** wait, then click **Run scan again**. For GCP, confirm the BigQuery export table you picked is the Standard export and has rows.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't see Run scan or the daily refresh switch">
    **Cause:** your workspace role is Viewer. Running scans and changing the refresh need Developer or Admin.

    **Fix:** ask a workspace Admin for the Developer role. See [Workspace users](/guide/workspace-users).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
