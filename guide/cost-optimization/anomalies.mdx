A cost anomaly is a day when one service cost noticeably more than its recent normal, based on finalized billing data. **Optimize → Anomalies** lists them for one cloud account so you can confirm each spike, record what it meant, and hand the cause analysis to CloudThinker.

## Prerequisites

- An Optimize connection with billing data. See the [Optimize quickstart](/guide/cost-optimization/quickstart).
- At least 14 days of finalized daily billing history for each service. Until then, the page shows **Baseline preparing** and a count of finalized days, instead of claiming there are no anomalies. A service you start using later also needs its own 14 days before it can be flagged, even on an older account.
- Workspace role Developer or higher to review or investigate. Viewers can read the list and evidence. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles).

## How detection works

Optimize compares each service's daily cost with the median of the previous 14 days. It scores the day by how far it sits above that median, measured in scaled median absolute deviations (MAD). A day is flagged only when both gates pass:

- **Unusual:** the score is above 3.5.
- **Large enough:** the increase is at least 20 or 15% of the baseline, whichever is higher.

Only increases are flagged. To spot a drop that could mean an outage or a broken workload, compare months in [Cost Explorer](/guide/cost-optimization/analytics) or ask CloudThinker about the service.

Severity comes from the size of the increase and the score:

| Severity | Rule |
|---|---|
| High | Increase above 500, or score above 6 |
| Medium | Increase above 100, or score above 4.5 |
| Low | Anything else that passed detection |

<Note>
The amounts (20, 100, 500) are plain numbers in the account's billing currency and aren't converted from USD. On an Azure scope billed in a currency such as JPY or VND, they're small, so the 15% gate does most of the filtering and more anomalies come out as Medium or High.
</Note>

The numbers on an anomaly (actual cost, baseline, estimated impact, severity) always come from saved billing data, never from an agent's judgment. Billing data arrives late, so this is a daily review tool, not real-time alerting.

## Review an anomaly

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Anomalies">
    In the sidebar, select **Optimize → Anomalies** and pick the cloud account in the page header.

    **Success state:** summary cards show the **Needs review** count, total **Estimated impact**, and **High priority** count for the range.

    <Frame caption="Anomalies for an AWS account with no open anomalies in the last 30 days">
      <img src="/images/cost-optimization/anomalies/01-anomalies.webp" alt="Anomalies page with Needs review, Estimated impact, and High priority cards, the Billing data through date, the All, Needs review, Investigating, Reviewed, and Retracted tabs, and No anomalies detected" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the window">
    Choose **30 days** or **90 days**. Use the tabs (**All**, **Needs review**, **Investigating**, **Reviewed**, **Retracted**) and the **Severity** and **Disposition** filters to narrow the list.

    The **Needs review** tab means nobody has recorded what this spike meant yet. It is unrelated to the **Needs review** [finding class](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations#how-to-read-a-finding) on **Findings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the evidence">
    Select an anomaly. The detail panel shows:

    | Field | Meaning |
    |---|---|
    | Actual cost | What the service cost on the flagged days |
    | Rolling baseline | The 14-day median it was compared with |
    | Estimated impact | Actual cost minus baseline. Not a savings estimate or invoice credit. |
    | Deviation | How far above baseline, as a percentage |
    | Detector score | The MAD score from [How detection works](#how-detection-works). Above 3.5 passes the first gate |
    | Billing data through | The last finalized billing date the detection used |
    | Top contributors | The regions that made up most of the cost. Regions only: for resources or usage types, use **Investigate with CloudThinker** |

    A chart plots actual daily cost against the baseline and the **Expected upper bound**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the linked findings">
    If an agent has tied the spike to a savings finding, an **Explained by** list appears. Select a finding to open its record. See [Optimize Findings](/guide/cost-optimization/recommendations).
  </Step>
</Steps>

One anomaly can cover a multi-day episode, up to 31 days. The daily refresh extends the same record while the increase continues. Detection runs on newly pulled billing data, so turning **Daily refresh** off also stops new anomalies from being found.

## Record what the change meant

Select **Mark reviewed** in the detail panel and pick one disposition:

| Disposition | Use it when |
|---|---|
| Unexpected change | The increase needs follow-up or a fix |
| Expected change | It matches a planned change, such as a launch or migration |
| Insignificant | The evidence is valid but not worth more work |

**Success state:** the anomaly moves to the **Reviewed** tab and leaves the **Needs review** count. A **Marked …** message, such as **Marked Expected change**, offers **Undo**.

To change your answer, select **Change review**. To put it back in the queue, choose **Reopen review**. The **Review reopened** message also offers **Undo**. Reopening keeps the history.

## Investigate with CloudThinker

Select **Investigate with CloudThinker** in the detail panel. The anomaly moves to **Investigating** right away, and CloudThinker, the built-in assistant, opens a new conversation that already contains the anomaly evidence and the exact cloud connection. It then reads live cost breakdowns and change history in that account. The investigation asks for reads, and any change CloudThinker proposes goes through your [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) rules.

**Success state:** the conversation ends with a likely cause and the evidence for it, or a plain statement that the available access or data can't establish one. CloudThinker doesn't guess a cause.

Ask follow-ups in the same conversation:

```text
Which resources inside this service drove the increase, and when were they created or changed?
Is the increase still going on today, or did it stop after the flagged days?
```

Later investigations of the same anomaly can reuse what earlier conversations found. Use **Open in Cost Explorer** to compare the spike with [completed-month spend](/guide/cost-optimization/analytics).

## Retracted anomalies

If billing data is restated and no longer supports an anomaly, the agent retracts it with a recorded reason. Retracted records are never deleted. Find them on the **Retracted** tab, where each one shows the reason and the time it was withdrawn. An anomaly in **Investigating** or **Reviewed** can't be retracted, because a person is already working on it.

## Get notified

There's no alert per anomaly. Two Optimize digests list new anomalies that nobody has reviewed yet, plus a count of older ones still waiting for review. Turn each one on or off on [Notifications](/guide/notifications).

| Digest | Who gets it | When | Email by default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Optimize weekly digest** | One person: the [workspace owner](/guide/security/roles) | Mondays, only when something changed | Off |
| **Optimize monthly digest** | Workspace Admins only | The 2nd of each month, covering last month | On |

A workspace the organization owns may have no owner. Under the defaults, the owner gets no weekly email until they turn it on, and Developers and Viewers get neither digest. Everyone else has to check **Optimize → Anomalies** or the **Recent anomalies** widget on **Overview**.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No anomalies appear for a new account">
    **Cause:** detection needs 14 finalized days of history per service. **Check:** the page shows **Baseline preparing** with how many finalized days are available. **Fix:** wait for billing history to build up. If the account is older, check that the daily refresh is on in the Optimize page header.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A spike I can see in my cloud console isn't listed">
    **Cause:** the provider hasn't finalized that day's billing yet, the increase is below the size threshold, or it's a decrease. **Check:** compare the date with **Billing data through**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mark reviewed or Investigate with CloudThinker is missing">
    **Cause:** your role is Viewer, or the anomaly is retracted. **Fix:** for a Viewer, ask a workspace Admin for the Developer role. A retracted record is read-only: check the **Retracted** tab for the reason.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An alert says Optimize refresh is paused or data may be stale">
    **Cause:** the last billing refresh failed, for example because of a missing permission or an unreadable billing source. The last good evidence stays readable, and the cloud connection stays connected. **Fix:** follow the alert. Use **Copy policy** if it shows a missing permission, **Check connection** to review credentials, then **Retry refresh**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
