Connect Slack once and anyone in a mapped channel can mention `@CloudThinker` to ask about your cloud, then keep talking in the thread. The same connection delivers [notifications](/guide/notifications), such as alerts and code review summaries, to the channels you choose.

## Prerequisites

- A CloudThinker organization **Owner** or **Admin**. Other roles can view the Slack page but can't connect or change it.
- A Team plan or above. On lower plans the Slack card shows **Requires Team plan or above** and an **Upgrade Plan** button. See [pricing](/guide/billing/pricing).
- Permission to install apps in your Slack workspace. If your Slack workspace uses app approval, a Slack Workspace Owner or app manager has to approve CloudThinker.
- CloudThinker hosted at app.cloudthinker.io. A self-hosted install shows **Not available on self-host** on the Slack card.

Each Slack workspace can connect to one CloudThinker organization.

## Connect Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Slack integration">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Integrations**, find the **Slack** card, and click **Go to configure**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect with Slack">
    Slack opens its authorization screen. Pick the Slack workspace and click **Allow**.

    Connecting replaces any Slack workspace this organization connected before.

    **Success state:** you return to the Slack page with a **Slack connected successfully** message, and the **Setup** card shows your Slack workspace name and Team ID under **Connect to Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the bot to a channel">
    In Slack, open the channel your team will use and run:

    ```text
    /invite @CloudThinker
    ```

    CloudThinker maps the channel to a CloudThinker workspace automatically. It picks the organization owner's default workspace, or the oldest workspace if the owner has none.

    **Success state:** the channel appears under **Configure channel-workspace mappings** on the Slack page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check which workspace each channel uses">
    Under **Configure channel-workspace mappings**, open the **Select workspace** menu on each channel row and pick the workspace whose connections that channel should use.

    **Success state:** a **Channel mapping updated** message appears and the row shows the new workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test message">
    In the channel, mention the bot with a question:

    ```text
    @CloudThinker What drove our cloud spend up last week?
    ```

    **Success state:** CloudThinker replies in a thread under your message. Reply in that thread to follow up. You don't need to mention the bot again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A channel the bot can read but that has no mapping still runs requests, in the organization's default workspace and with that workspace's connections. Map every channel the bot is in, and remove the bot from channels that shouldn't reach your infrastructure.
</Warning>

## Ask CloudThinker in Slack

Every request starts with `@CloudThinker`. After that, you write the same requests you'd type in the web app.

<PromptSyntaxBasics />

```text
@CloudThinker Which EC2 instances ran under 10% CPU for the last 14 days?
@CloudThinker Summarize open critical security findings for production
@CloudThinker Why did checkout latency spike at 14:00 UTC today?
```

What to expect in the thread:

| You see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **is typing...** | CloudThinker is working on the request |
| A reply in the thread | The answer. The full conversation also appears in the CloudThinker web app |
| **Permission required to use** a tool, with **View in CloudThinker** | The request needs an action that your [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) setup holds for a person. Open the link and approve or reject it in the web app |

CloudThinker answers mentions in channels and replies in its own threads. To start a new topic, mention `@CloudThinker` in a new message.

## Send notifications to Slack

Slack is also a destination for workspace notifications.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open notification settings">
    On the Slack page, under **Configure notifications**, click **Go to notification settings**. You can also open **Notifications** from the app sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a default channel for the workspace">
    Under **Slack Notification Channel**, search for and select a channel. You can pick any public channel, or a private channel the bot was invited to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Override per notification type (optional)">
    Pick a different channel or severity threshold for a specific type, such as alerts. See [Notifications](/guide/notifications) for the full list of types.

    **Success state:** the next matching event posts in the chosen channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Code review notifications

When [Review](/guide/code-review/overview) finishes a pull request or merge request on a connected repository, CloudThinker can post a summary to Slack:

- A count of findings by severity
- Up to 5 unresolved findings, each with its title, file path, and line number
- How many earlier findings are now resolved
- A **View Review** button that opens the review in CloudThinker
- **No issues found** when the review is clean

Choose the channel and severity threshold for reviews in [Notifications](/guide/notifications).

## Manage the connection

| Task | How |
| --- | --- |
| Move a channel to another workspace | Change **Select workspace** on its row in **Configure channel-workspace mappings** |
| Stop routing a channel | Remove the row. The bot stays in the channel, so also remove it in Slack if it shouldn't answer there |
| Reconnect for new permissions | If the page shows **Slack needs one more permission**, click **Reconnect Slack**. Existing messages and notification channels keep working |
| Disconnect | Click **Disconnect from Slack** at the bottom of the Slack page |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Slack authorization not completed">
    Slack denied or cancelled the authorization, or your Slack workspace requires admin approval. Ask a Slack Workspace Owner or app manager to approve CloudThinker in Slack's app management, then click **Connect with Slack** again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Slack workspace already connected">
    That Slack workspace is already connected to another CloudThinker account. A Slack workspace can connect to only one. Disconnect it from the other account first, or use a different Slack workspace.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not authorized">
    Only organization Owners and Admins can configure Slack. Ask one of them to connect it, or to change your [organization role](/guide/security/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The bot doesn't reply">
    Check that the bot is in the channel (`/invite @CloudThinker`) and that you mentioned it in a new message. A message without the mention gets a reply only inside a thread CloudThinker already started.

    If CloudThinker replies **No workspace is configured for this channel**, open the Slack page and set a workspace on the channel's row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The answer uses the wrong account or cluster">
    The channel is mapped to a different workspace than you expected. Check its row under **Configure channel-workspace mappings** and change **Select workspace**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You don't have access to this incident in CloudThinker">
    Incident war-room channels only run requests from people who have a CloudThinker account in that workspace with access to incidents and chat. CloudThinker matches you by email, so use the same email in Slack and CloudThinker, or ask a workspace Admin for access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Notifications don't arrive">
    Check that a **Slack Notification Channel** is set for the workspace, that the notification type is on, and that the event meets the severity threshold. For a private channel, invite the bot first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
