## Getting started

| Symptom | Quick check | Full fix |
|---|---|---|
| You want to try CloudThinker before connecting production | Connect a staging or sandbox account with a read-only role first. | [Quickstart](/quickstart) |
| You can't find where to add a connection | In the sidebar, click **More → Workspace settings → Connections**. | [Add a connection](/guide/connections/overview#add-a-connection) |
| An agent can't find a connection you added | Connections belong to one workspace. Check the workspace name in the sidebar. | [Workspaces](/guide/workspaces#troubleshooting) |
| A new connection's Discovery section is still empty | Discovery runs once per connection, for up to 30 minutes. It pauses if the credit balance is too low. | [After you connect](/guide/connections/overview#what-happens-after-you-connect) |
| Optimize shows no cost data after a scan | On a new AWS account, Cost Explorer can take up to 24 hours to start, and it lags usage by 24 to 48 hours. | [Optimize quickstart](/guide/cost-optimization/quickstart#troubleshooting) |

## Sign-in, SSO and members

| Symptom | Quick check | Full fix |
|---|---|---|
| A user can't sign in after you required SSO | Is the user assigned to the CloudThinker app in your IdP? | [SSO](/guide/security/sso#troubleshooting) |
| Your IdP reports an invalid ACS or reply URL, or an audience mismatch | The values must match exactly: `https://`, no trailing slash, case-sensitive. | [SAML SSO](/guide/security/sso-saml#troubleshooting) |
| "Redirect URI mismatch", Issuer Match or Discovery Reachable fails | Copy `issuer` straight from the discovery document. The issuer must be reachable from the public internet. | [OIDC SSO](/guide/security/sso-oidc#troubleshooting) |
| A user wasn't provisioned, or someone who left can still sign in | Look for a `FAILED` entry in **View sync logs**. Unassign leavers on the app itself, not only the group. | [SCIM](/guide/security/scim#troubleshooting) |
| The invite button says Seats full, or you can't remove a member | Pending invitations use seats too. Admins can't remove other Admins or the Owner. | [Organization](/guide/organization#troubleshooting) |
| A teammate can't see a workspace | Developers and Viewers only see workspaces they were added to. | [Workspaces](/guide/workspaces#troubleshooting) |
| The person isn't in the Add Member list | They may not be in the organization yet, or they're an organization Owner or Admin who already has access. | [Workspace members](/guide/workspace-users#troubleshooting) |
| A Viewer can't type in the chat | Viewers are read-only. Change their **Workspace Role** to **Developer**. | [Workspace members](/guide/workspace-users#troubleshooting) |

## Connections, chat and commands

| Symptom | Quick check | Full fix |
|---|---|---|
| A connection won't verify, or shows **Error** | Open the connection: the detail panel shows the error. Fix the cause at the provider, then click **Retry** or **Replace credentials**. | [When a connection breaks](/guide/connections/overview#when-a-connection-breaks), then your provider: [AWS](/guide/connections/aws#troubleshooting), [Azure](/guide/connections/azure#troubleshooting), [GCP](/guide/connections/gcp#troubleshooting), [Kubernetes](/guide/connections/kubernetes#troubleshooting), [GitHub](/guide/connections/github#troubleshooting) |
| A command doesn't expand, or the `/` menu shows Nothing matches | A skill with the same name wins the slug. Check the slug's spelling on **More → Workspace settings → Commands**. | [Commands](/guide/commands#troubleshooting) |
| A credential name is rejected, or you can't see the `$` picker | Keys take uppercase letters, digits and underscores only. The `$` picker needs the workspace Admin role. | [Credentials](/guide/credentials#troubleshooting) |
| The Slack bot doesn't reply, or answers with the wrong account | Is the bot in the channel (`/invite @CloudThinker`), and did you mention it in a new message? | [Slack](/guide/slack-integration#troubleshooting) |
| The Teams bot doesn't reply, or a team isn't linked to a workspace | Your Teams admin settings may block custom apps. | [Microsoft Teams](/guide/teams-integration#troubleshooting) |

## Approvals and automations

| Symptom | Quick check | Full fix |
|---|---|---|
| A write still asks you even though a Rule allows it | The run uses **Manual**, which asks before every write. | [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview#troubleshooting) |
| You don't see the Approve button | Viewers can't approve, and a Rule may send the ask to a group you aren't in. The card names who can. | [Approvers](/guide/approvals/approvers#troubleshooting) |
| An automation refused a write instead of asking | Automations default to **Auto · Rules + AI · Refuse**. Pick an **Ask me** choice, which works today. An Allow Rule for that write helps only once [Rule enforcement](/guide/approvals/rules#troubleshooting) is on for your workspace. | [Approval mode FAQ](/guide/approvals/approval-mode#faq) |
| A run sits on Required Approval, or an automation turned itself off | Open the run's conversation from **Runs**. Ten failed runs in a row turn an automation off. | [Autonomous operations](/guide/automation/autonomous-agents#troubleshooting) |
| The run succeeded but nothing posted in Slack | Read the run's conversation first. Then check Slack is on for **Agent alerts** in **Notifications**. | [Automation quickstart](/guide/automation/quickstart#troubleshooting) |

## Products

**Resolve** handles production incidents. Its intake layer, [Pulse](/guide/pulse/overview), takes alerts from your tools and groups them into clusters. **Review** reviews pull requests. **Optimize** covers cloud cost. **Cyber** tests your applications for security flaws. **Memory** is what CloudThinker remembers about your workspace between chats.

| Symptom | Quick check | Full fix |
|---|---|---|
| Resolve: Pulse shows No signals yet | Check your monitoring tool's delivery log, then paste the webhook URL again and fire a test alert. | [Alerts not arriving](/guide/incident/troubleshooting#alerts-not-arriving) |
| Resolve: every alert opens its own cluster | A repeat joins the open cluster only when source, alert type and resource ID all match. | [Clusters not forming](/guide/incident/troubleshooting#clusters-not-forming) |
| Resolve: incidents wait in Not started | **On duty** is paused. Check the header of **Resolve → Investigation**. | [Investigation not starting](/guide/incident/troubleshooting#investigation-not-starting) |
| Resolve: analysis failed, or an investigation looks stuck | Click **Watch live** on the incident. If the timeline is moving, it isn't stuck. | [Investigation stuck](/guide/incident/troubleshooting#investigation-stuck) |
| Resolve: a runbook command waits for approval or was blocked | Commands from Confluence, GitHub and GitLab always require approval. | [Runbook not running](/guide/incident/troubleshooting#runbook-not-running) |
| Review didn't post on a pull request | Draft PRs aren't reviewed. Check the repository's **Review** switch in **Review → Settings → Repositories**. | [No comment on a new PR](/guide/code-review/troubleshooting#no-comment-on-a-new-pr) |
| Review shows **Reconnect your Git provider** | Click the alert's button and reconnect, or paste a new token with the same scopes. | [Provider auth errors](/guide/code-review/troubleshooting#provider-auth-errors) |
| Optimize: a spike you can see in your cloud console isn't listed | Compare the date with **Billing data through**. New accounts need 14 finalized days first. | [Anomalies](/guide/cost-optimization/anomalies#troubleshooting) |
| Optimize: the findings total is $0 or doesn't match Overview | A **Service** filter on **Findings** changes the total. Clear it. | [Savings](/guide/cost-optimization/savings#troubleshooting) |
| Cyber: target ownership stays at Needs proof, or protected pages aren't tested | Some DNS providers append the zone name, so the domain appears twice in the TXT record name. | [Cyber quickstart](/guide/security/cyber-quickstart#troubleshooting) |
| Cyber: a fixed finding came back, or Awaiting retest never clears | A later scan proved the problem again. Open the app's **Runs** link to see which run. | [Cyber findings](/guide/security/cyber-findings#troubleshooting) |
| Memory shows Not ready yet, or an edit says the item changed | Click **Retry** after a few seconds, or **Reload** and make the edit again. | [Memory](/guide/memory#troubleshooting) |

## Workers and the CLI

| Symptom | Quick check | Full fix |
|---|---|---|
| The Worker shows Offline, or verification failed | Run `cloudthinker worker status --outpost <name>`, or `cloudthinker worker doctor` in the folder. | [Workers](/guide/workers#troubleshooting) |
| A teammate gets `EXECUTOR_TARGET_PRIVATE` | They tried to use your personal Worker. Create a shared Worker instead. | [Workers](/guide/workers#troubleshooting) |
| `cloudthinker: command not found` | The shell started before `~/.local/bin` was added to `PATH`. Open a new shell. | [CLI overview](/guide/cli/overview#troubleshooting) |
| A command says you are not logged in (exit code 3) | The credential is missing or expired. Run `cloudthinker login`, then `cloudthinker whoami`. | [CLI authentication](/guide/cli/authentication#troubleshooting) |
| A headless run exits with code 5 | The run paused for an approval. Open its `web_url` and decide. | [Headless chat](/guide/cli/headless-chat#troubleshooting) |
| A headless prompt is rejected as too long | The prompt plus piped input is over 50,000 characters. | [Headless chat](/guide/cli/headless-chat#troubleshooting) |

Every CLI exit code (0 to 5, and 130) and what to do about it is in [Exit codes](/guide/cli/headless-chat#exit-codes).

## Admin and billing

| Symptom | Quick check | Full fix |
|---|---|---|
| You don't see Audit Logs, or the export has fewer rows than expected | Audit Logs needs the organization Admin role. Events older than 90 days are deleted. | [Audit logs](/guide/security/audit-logs#troubleshooting) |
| A guardrail pattern won't save, or Guardrails isn't in Admin Settings | The error names the check that failed. Saving needs the workspace Admin role, and **Admin Settings** is in the menu only for organization Owners and Admins. A workspace Admin who is neither opens **Guardrails** from a direct link. | [Custom guardrails](/guide/security/custom-guardrails#troubleshooting) |
| You can't change the workspace BYOK setting | You aren't a workspace Admin, or the Owner requires the organization key everywhere. The page says which. | [BYOK](/guide/byok#troubleshooting) |
| A workspace kept spending past its credit limit | That's expected on **Notify only**, which never blocks. Switch the workspace to **Throttle** to stop paid spend at the limit. | [How enforcement works](/guide/billing/credit-limits#how-enforcement-works) |
| A workspace stopped running agents but chat still works | It's on **Throttle** and hit its limit, so only free daily credits are left. | [Credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits#troubleshooting) |

## Collect diagnostics

Check these before you ask for help. They often show the cause on their own.

- **Automation run:** open it from **Runs** and read its conversation for the error.
- **Incident:** click **Watch live** to see each check as it runs.
- **Connection:** open it. The detail panel shows the last error.
- **Who changed what:** **Admin Settings → Audit Logs** (organization Admin role, Scale plan or higher).
- **CLI:** `cloudthinker whoami` and `cloudthinker auth status` show who you're logged in as and your saved workspaces. For a headless run, `cloudthinker chat status <run_id>` shows its state.
- **Worker:** `cloudthinker worker doctor` in the Worker's folder prints what failed and how to fix it.
