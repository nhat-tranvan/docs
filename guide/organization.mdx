An organization is the top-level account in CloudThinker. It owns billing, seats, and the member list, and it contains one or more [workspaces](/guide/workspaces) where the actual work happens.

## Prerequisites

- Most tasks on this page need an organization **Owner** or **Admin**. Developers and Viewers don't see Admin Settings in their menu, even when they're Admin of a workspace through an override. The one page such a workspace Admin can open there is **Guardrails**, from a direct link. A workspace Admin manages that workspace in **More → Workspace settings**, including its own BYOK key.
- Anyone except the Owner can [leave an organization](#leave-an-organization).

## How organizations and workspaces fit together

When you sign up, CloudThinker creates an organization named after you (for example, "Jane's Organization") and makes you its Owner. Onboarding then creates your first workspace inside it.

| Lives in the organization | Lives in each workspace |
|---|---|
| Plan, billing, and seats | [Connections](/guide/connections/overview) and [credentials](/guide/credentials) |
| Member list and organization roles | [Custom agents](/guide/agents/customize), [skills](/guide/skills/overview), and [knowledge](/guide/knowledge) |
| [BYOK](/guide/byok), [SSO and provisioning](/guide/security/sso), [audit logs](/guide/security/audit-logs) | Conversations, artifacts, and module data (Optimize, Review, Cyber, Resolve) |
| [Usage](/guide/billing/usage) and [credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits) (set here, one cap per workspace) | [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview), [notifications](/guide/notifications), and workspace members |

Approvals are configured inside each workspace. Credit limits are set by an Owner or Admin in Admin Settings, with one cap per workspace. Keep production and non-production in separate workspaces when one needs stricter approvals or a tighter budget. See [choose a layout](/guide/workspaces#choose-a-layout).

## Open Admin Settings

Organization controls live in **Admin Settings**, separate from the per-workspace Chat Settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account menu">
    Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar. The menu lists your organizations on the left and the selected organization's workspaces on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Admin settings">
    With your current organization selected, click **Admin settings**.

    **Success state:** the sidebar switches to the **Admin Settings** list, starting with **Organization** and **Workspaces**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What each Admin Settings page is for, and who sees it:

| Page | Use it to | Visible to |
|---|---|---|
| **Organization** | Rename the organization, see seats and workspace count, manage members | Owner, Admin |
| **Workspaces** | Create, open, configure, and delete workspaces. This is the only place a workspace can be deleted | Owner, Admin |
| **Billing** | Change plan, payment method, and seats | Owner |
| **Usage** | Track credit consumption | Owner, Admin |
| **BYOK** | Set the organization's own LLM credentials, the default for every workspace. A workspace Admin can choose a workspace key instead in **More → Workspace settings → BYOK**, unless the Owner requires the organization key. See [BYOK](/guide/byok#organization-and-workspace-keys) | Owner |
| **Identity and access** | Domain verification, SSO, and provisioning. Only the Owner can change anything here | Owner, Admin (Admins can't make changes) |
| **Audit Logs** | Review who changed what | Owner, Admin |
| **Capabilities** | Choose who decides whether Advisor, Blind Spot, and Conversation recaps run in your workspaces. See [Capabilities](/guide/capabilities) | Owner (an Admin who opens its link sees it locked) |

Some pages, such as **Credit limits**, **Guardrails**, **Sandboxes**, and **Capabilities**, show only when they're enabled for your organization.

## Invite members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite dialog">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Organization** and click **Invite Members** in the **Members** section. You can also click **Invite members** in the account menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add email addresses">
    Type an address and press Enter or a comma to add it. You can invite up to 10 people at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Pick **Admin**, **Developer**, or **Viewer** under **Role**. Developer is the default. You can't invite someone as Owner.

    One role applies to every address in this batch. To invite people with different roles, send separate batches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick workspaces for Developers and Viewers">
    Click **Select workspaces** and choose the workspaces they should join. Admins don't need this, because they get every workspace automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send Invitations**.

    **Success state:** each invitee appears in the invitations list with a status and an expiry date, and receives an invitation email. Invitations expire after 7 days. Pending invitations sit in the **Pending** tab of the **Members** section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If you invite a Developer or Viewer without selecting a workspace, they join the organization but can't open any workspace until you grant access. See [workspace members](/guide/workspace-users).
</Warning>

**What the invitee does:** they open the link in the email and sign in or sign up with the address you invited. A different address is rejected. Someone who already belongs to another organization keeps it, and both show in their account menu.

## Organization roles

Every member has one organization role. Owners and Admins are Admins in every workspace. Developers and Viewers see the organization name and the workspaces they're assigned to. They don't see the organization member list, only the members of their workspaces in each workspace's **Members** tab.

| Permission | Owner | Admin | Developer | Viewer |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Edit organization name and description | ✓ | ✓ | | |
| Invite and remove members, change roles | ✓ | ✓† | | |
| Create and delete workspaces | ✓ | ✓ | | |
| Admin access to every workspace | ✓ | ✓ | | |
| View usage, set credit limits | ✓ | ✓ | | |
| Manage plan, billing, and seats | ✓ | | | |
| Configure BYOK and SSO | ✓ | | | |
| Change [capabilities](/guide/capabilities) for every workspace | ✓ | | | |
| Request an ownership transfer | ✓ | | | |
| Work in assigned workspaces | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | View only* |

\*Viewers can open conversations, findings, and dashboards, but can't chat with agents, run commands, or change anything except installing a [Skills Hub](/guide/skills/overview) skill.

†Admins can change roles for and remove Developers and Viewers only. Admins can invite a new member as Admin, but only the Owner can promote an existing member to Admin, change an Admin's role, or remove an Admin.

People added through SSO just-in-time sign-in or SCIM get the **Auto-assigned organization role** you set on the connection (Viewer by default). With SCIM, you can also set a role per IdP group. See [SSO](/guide/security/sso) and [SCIM group mapping](/guide/security/scim#map-groups-to-workspaces).

You can raise or lower a Developer's or Viewer's role inside a single workspace. See [workspace roles](/guide/workspace-users#workspace-roles). "Member" is a legacy role name and works like Developer.

## Manage members and invitations

All of these live in **Admin Settings → Organization**.

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Rename the organization | Click **Update** next to **Organization name** in the **Organization overview**, edit **Organization Name** (and optionally **Description**), then click **Save Changes** |
| Change a member's role | Pick a new value in the **Role** dropdown on their row |
| Change which workspaces a Developer or Viewer can open | Click **Edit Workspace Access** on their row |
| Remove a member | Click **Remove Member** and confirm. They lose access to every workspace in the organization |
| Activate a member | Click **Activate Member** on a row with **Inactive** status |
| Resend or cancel an invitation | Open the actions menu on the invitation row, then click **Resend Invitation** or **Cancel Invitation** |

A member shows as **Inactive** when they were provisioned (for example, through SSO) while the organization had no free seat. Inactive members don't use a seat. Activating them uses one.

### What happens when you remove someone

| Item | What happens |
|---|---|
| Workspace access | Removed from every workspace in the organization. Pending workspace invitations to their email are canceled |
| [Automations](/guide/automation/automations) they own | Turned off. Each one shows "Its owner no longer has access to this workspace." A workspace Admin clicks **Take ownership**, then turns it back on. It then runs as the new owner, with their access |
| Approvals | They're taken out of every approver group, so pending requests go to the remaining approvers |
| Workspaces they created | Stay in the organization with everything in them. Owners and Admins keep access |
| Their seat | Freed |

<Warning>
Before you remove someone who owns scheduled Automations, decide who takes them over. Otherwise those runs stop silently until an Admin takes ownership.
</Warning>

## Seats

Each active member and each pending invitation holds a seat. Free has one seat, and Team, Scale, and Scale Plus are billed per seat. An invitation expires after 7 days and then stops holding a seat. **Resend Invitation** sends a new link that's valid for another 7 days. **Total seats** in the organization overview shows how many you've bought and how many are free. The Owner can click **Manage** there to add seats. When every seat is used, the invite button shows **Seats full**. See [plans and pricing](/guide/billing/pricing).

## Control spend

Credits are pooled at the organization level, and you cap each workspace's share of them in **Admin Settings → Credit Limits**. The page shows only when credit limits are enabled for your organization.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Default monthly limit per workspace** | A credit cap for every workspace without its own override, or **Unlimited** |
| **Per-workspace limits** | A different cap or enforcement mode for one workspace |
| **When a workspace hits its monthly limit** | **Throttle** stops paid credit spend in that workspace. Chat on the Light model continues on the 100 free daily credits. See [usage](/guide/billing/usage). **Notify only** blocks nothing |
| **Alert the organization owner at** | 80%, 100%, or both. Alerts go to the Owner only, in-app and by email |

<Warning>
On **Throttle**, a workspace that hits its limit stops running agents on paid credits, mid-incident or not. Put production on **Notify only** or give it a high cap, and use **Throttle** for sandboxes.
</Warning>

Track consumption in **Admin Settings → Usage**. See [credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits) and [usage](/guide/billing/usage).

## Transfer ownership

Each organization has one Owner, who alone manages billing, seats, BYOK, and SSO. There's no transfer button in the app. To move ownership, have the current Owner email [support@cloudthinker.io](mailto:support@cloudthinker.io) with the organization name and the new Owner's email.

- The new Owner must already be an active member of the organization.
- The old Owner becomes an Admin.

<Tip>
Request the transfer before the Owner leaves your company, while they can still send the email.
</Tip>

## Leave an organization

Admins, Developers, and Viewers can leave. Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar, select the organization on the left, then click **Leave organization** at the bottom of its menu and confirm.

Leaving has the same effects as being removed. See [what happens when you remove someone](#what-happens-when-you-remove-someone).

The Owner can't leave. [Transfer ownership](#transfer-ownership) first. Members provisioned through SCIM are removed in your identity provider instead.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see Admin settings in the account menu">
    **Cause:** your organization role is Developer or Viewer (being Admin of a workspace through an override doesn't add Admin Settings), or you've selected an organization other than the one you're working in.

    **Fix:** select your current organization in the account menu. If it still doesn't appear, ask an Owner to make you an Admin. For workspace settings such as the workspace BYOK key, use **More → Workspace settings** instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The invite button says Seats full">
    **Cause:** active members plus pending invitations have used every seat.

    **Fix:** cancel invitations nobody will accept, remove members who left, or ask the Owner to add seats from **Total seats → Manage**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't remove a member">
    **Cause:** one of these rules applies:

    - Admins can't remove other Admins or the Owner.
    - The organization Owner can't be removed.
    - Members provisioned through SCIM have to be removed in your identity provider. The error reads "This member was provisioned via SCIM."

    **Fix:** ask the Owner, or deprovision the user in your identity provider. See [SCIM](/guide/security/scim).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A new teammate signed in but sees no workspace">
    **Cause:** they're a Developer or Viewer with no workspace assigned.

    **Fix:** go to **Admin Settings → Organization**, click **Edit Workspace Access** on their row, select the workspaces, and click **Save**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
