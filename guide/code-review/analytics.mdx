Review Insights shows review activity, recurring findings, and evidence-backed trends across your repositories. Open it from **Review → Insights** in the navigation.

## Insights tabs

Review Insights has two tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|--------------|
| **Review Patterns** | Which finding domains — Security, Performance, Correctness, and Patterns — affect your reviewed merge requests, with links to the finding evidence |
| **Reports** | Which repository-level review reports are ready |

Review Patterns opens by default.

## Review Patterns

Review Patterns is the coaching view. It shows, per developer and per domain, how often reviewed work merged without findings — and links each rate to the findings behind it.

### What counts

Review Patterns counts completed [Advanced Reviews](/guide/code-review/setup#review-modes) on merge requests with a recorded outcome. Fast Reviews, incomplete reviews, pipeline findings, and merge requests from bot authors are not counted.

Older merge requests without a recorded outcome stay excluded — Review Patterns never guesses an outcome from timestamps.

### Reading the rates

The view covers four domains: Security, Performance, Correctness, and Patterns. Each domain shows the finding-free merge-request rate together with the exact counts behind it.

A domain rate is:

```text
finding-free merge requests / reviewed merge requests
```

One merge request counts once in a selected period, even when it was reviewed more than once — findings from all of its counted reviews feed the domain rates, and several findings in one domain still count the merge request once. Resolved and unresolved findings count equally.

A developer with fewer than three counted merge requests stays visible with **Not enough data** and the reviewed count. Their merge requests still contribute to the pooled team rate.

### Main coaching priority

The page headlines the domain with the highest team-wide affected merge-request rate. An exact tie breaks on the Critical and High finding counts, and every domain still tied appears. Severity never weights a rate.

### Explore evidence

Select a developer-domain cell to see the developer and pooled team across the selected weekly or monthly buckets. The expanded view lists the associated findings with severity, resolution state, merge-request identity, and a review link.

A resolved finding remains historical coaching evidence.

## Periods and states

The weekly and monthly controls apply to Review Patterns. The view keeps your selections while data loads.

| View | Available periods |
|------|-------------------|
| Weekly | This week, or the last 4, 8, or 12 weeks |
| Monthly | Last 3, 6, or 12 months |

The view handles thin periods explicitly:

- No counted reviews in the period: the page explains that the period has no data.
- One or two counted merge requests: the view shows counts without a percentage.
- No counted merge requests in a trend bucket: the chart shows a gap labelled **No data**.
- If loading fails, the affected panel offers **Retry** and keeps your period selection.
