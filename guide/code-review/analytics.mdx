Review Insights turns your merged pull requests (PRs) and merge requests (MRs) into team-level numbers: who shipped what, how clean it was at merge, and which kind of finding keeps coming back. Use it in a weekly team review or a 1:1 to pick one thing to coach, then click through to the findings behind each number.

## Prerequisites

- Review connected to at least one repository, with some merged PRs or MRs. See [Review setup](/guide/code-review/setup).
- Any workspace role (Admin, Developer or Viewer) to view Insights. Changing the report schedule needs the workspace **Admin** role. See [roles](/guide/security/roles).
- For the **Review Patterns** tab: the review mode set to **Advanced** or **Auto**, because that tab counts only Advanced Reviews. With **Auto**, only the changes Auto sends to an Advanced Review count. See [review modes](/guide/code-review/setup#choose-a-review-mode).

<Note>
Everyone with a workspace role, including Viewers, can see the leaderboard with developer names, and there's no setting to hide it. Check this with your team before you roll Review out.
</Note>

## Open Insights

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Insights">
    Click **Review** in the left navigation, then **Insights**. The page header reads **Quality trends, patterns and contributions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date range">
    Open the **Date range** menu in the top right. Ranges come in two groups:

    | Group | Ranges | Trend charts bucket by |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Weekly** | This Week, Last 4 Weeks (default), Last 8 Weeks, Last 12 Weeks | Week |
    | **Monthly** | Last 3 Months, Last 6 Months, Last 12 Months | Month |

    Weeks start on Monday at 00:00 UTC. **This Week** has no trend, so it hides the **Details** and **Review Patterns** tabs. Pick a longer range to bring them back.

    The range is the only filter. Insights always covers every repository in the workspace. For one repository's view, use its PDFs in the **Reports** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    | Tab | Answers |
    |---|---|
    | **Leaderboard** (default) | Who shipped how much, and how clean it was at merge |
    | **Details** | How one metric moved per developer over the range |
    | **Review Patterns** | Which finding domain affects the most reviewed changes, per developer |
    | **Reports** | Per-repository PDF reports and the actions they track |

    **Success state:** summary cards load at the top of **Leaderboard** and **Details**, and the tab you picked shows data for the range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The tab, range and metric live in the page URL, so you can bookmark a view or paste it into a team channel and others see the same thing. Insights has no CSV or API export. Share the URL, or the report PDFs from the **Reports** tab.

## Summary cards

**Leaderboard** and **Details** open with four cards for the selected range:

| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Active Authors** | Developers with merged changes in the range |
| **Merge Requests** or **Pull Requests** | Merged changes (the label follows your Git provider) |
| **Found in &lt;range&gt;**, for example **Found in last 4 weeks** | Findings raised in the selected range |
| **Team Quality Score** | Average of the review scores (1 to 10) CloudThinker gives each merged change. Higher is cleaner |

A strip under the cards adds up to three signals, each shown only when there's data for it:

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **avg to merge** | Average time from opening a change to merging it |
| **accurate findings** | Share of thumbs-up among all thumbs-up and thumbs-down votes on findings |
| **review rating** | Average star rating given to reviews, out of 5 |

No votes in the range means no **accurate findings** signal, rather than 0%. Ask your team to vote on findings if you want this number.

## Leaderboard

The **Developer Leaderboard** ranks developers "by contribution and quality, as scored at merge". Scores are frozen when a change merges, so later edits to findings don't move past rankings.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Rank** | Position in the selected range |
| **Move** | Places gained or lost against the previous period. Hover to see which window it compares |
| **Developer** | The change author |
| **Contribution Score** | Quality and Impact against the team, averaged. **1.00** is the team average |
| **Changes** | Merged PRs or MRs |
| **Quality Score** | Average review score, 1 to 10, across the developer's changes at merge |
| **Impact** | Total size of the shipped changes, from files and lines changed |
| **Findings** | Total findings. Hover for the Critical, High, Medium and Low split |

Bot authors are left out of every Insights number. Review spots them by username: a GitHub `[bot]` suffix, a trailing `-bot`, `_bot` or `.bot`, or a GitLab project or group access-token user. A service account with any other name is counted, and you can't exclude accounts from the workspace.

### How the scores are built

| Score | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Impact** (per change) | Files changed × 6 + lines added × 0.14 + lines deleted × 0.28, with a floor of 1. These weights are set for the whole CloudThinker installation, not per workspace |
| **Contribution Score** | (developer's Quality Score ÷ team average Quality Score + developer's total Impact ÷ team average Impact) ÷ 2 |

For example, a developer with a Quality Score of 8 on a team averaging 7, and a total Impact of 300 against a team average of 200, scores (1.14 + 1.50) ÷ 2 = **1.32**.

Use the leaderboard to spot who to support and what to coach, not as a performance rating: Impact measures size, not value.

## Details

**Details** charts one metric per developer over the range, then lists each week or month in a table.

1. Choose the metric from the **Metric** menu: **Contribution Score**, **Quality Score**, **Changes** or **Total Impact**.
2. Read the chart titled, for example, **Quality Score by Developer**.
3. Scroll to the table for the team totals per week or month: **Merge Requests** or **Pull Requests**, **Team Quality**, **Total Impact**, **Findings** and **Authors**.

Use it to check whether a dip is one bad week or a trend.

## Review Patterns

**Review Patterns** is the coaching view. For each developer it shows how often their reviewed changes merged without a finding in each of four domains:

| Domain | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Security** | Security vulnerabilities and unsafe behavior |
| **Performance** | Slow paths, excess work and scaling risks |
| **Correctness** | Logic errors and missed edge cases |
| **Patterns** | Codebase conventions and maintainable structure |

### What counts

Only finished, non-draft Advanced Reviews on changes with a recorded outcome count. Fast Reviews, unfinished reviews, pipeline findings and bot authors are left out. If nothing qualifies, the tab shows **No confirmed Advanced Reviews in this period**.

A domain rate is:

```text
changes with no finding in that domain / reviewed changes
```

A change counts once per range, even if it was reviewed several times. Several findings in one domain still count that change once. Resolved and open findings count the same, and severity never weights a rate.

### Main coaching priority

The top of the tab names the domain with the highest team-wide share of affected changes. On an exact tie, Critical and High finding counts break it. If they tie too, every tied domain is listed.

### Explore the evidence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a cell">
    Click a developer's cell in a domain column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the trend">
    A **Weekly trend** or **Monthly trend** chart shows that developer against the pooled **Team** line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the findings">
    The evidence table lists each finding with **Severity**, **Finding**, **Location**, **Review** and **Status** (**Open** or **Resolved**). Click **Load more evidence** for older ones, and follow the **Review** link to the change.

    **Success state:** you can name the specific findings behind a rate before you bring it up with the developer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A resolved finding stays in the evidence. It still shows what the review caught.

### Thin data

| You see | Why |
|---|---|
| **Not enough data** | The developer has fewer than 3 reviewed changes in the range. Their changes still count toward the **Team** rate |
| **No data** | No reviewed changes in that bucket. Charts show a gap |
| **No findings in this domain** | Every selected review was clean for that domain |

## Reports

**Reports** gives each repository two PDFs each week: a **Technical PDF** for engineers and technical leads, and a **Product PDF** for product and engineering leaders. Rows are grouped by period, with one row per repository showing **Repository**, **Generated**, **Trends**, **Actions**, **Status** and **PDFs**. The date range control is hidden on this tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose how reports run">
    Turn on **Scheduled reports** to generate them every Monday from 09:00 UTC (each workspace starts within 30 minutes of that). Click **Generate now** for a run today. Changing the schedule needs the workspace Admin role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read a report">
    Open a row to see the **Repository report** panel. It shows **Trends**, such as "Findings down", and **Tracked actions**: actions from this and earlier reports, each marked open, improved, resolved, regressed or stale. The row also shows the repository's health: **Healthy**, **Watch** or **At risk**, with a score out of 10, or **Not rated yet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the PDFs">
    Download the **Technical PDF** or **Product PDF** from the panel or the row. A failed row offers **Retry**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until the first run, the tab shows **No reports yet**. After a scheduled run, workspace admins get a **Reports ready** notification. Turning the schedule off keeps existing reports. Review also sends a separate **Weekly Review digest** email of the week's review activity. Manage both under Review in [notification settings](/guide/notifications).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Details and Review Patterns tabs are missing">
    **This Week** is selected, and it has no trend. Pick **Last 4 Weeks** or longer in **Date range**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Review Patterns says No confirmed Advanced Reviews in this period">
    Your changes were reviewed in **Fast** mode, or none finished in the range. Go to **Review → Settings**, and in the **Review** section set **Review mode** to **Advanced** or **Auto**. Then check back once new changes merge. Earlier Fast Reviews never appear here.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A developer shows Not enough data">
    They have fewer than 3 reviewed changes in the range. Widen the range, or read the **Team** rate instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The leaderboard is empty or missing someone">
    Insights counts merged changes only. Open PRs and closed-without-merge PRs don't appear, and neither do bot authors. Check the PR merged inside the selected range.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Review Patterns unavailable">
    Your account doesn't have Review view permission in this workspace. Ask a workspace Admin to check your role and membership. See [roles](/guide/security/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A panel says Could not load">
    Click **Retry** in that panel. Your range and tab stay selected.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
