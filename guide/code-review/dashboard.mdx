The Review dashboard shows what needs attention across your workspace's repositories. Start here to choose the next change to review or finding to fix.

## Prerequisites

- [Review connected](/guide/code-review/setup) to at least one repository.
- Review view permission in the workspace. Ask a workspace Admin to check your role if Review is unavailable.
- Completed reviews for the numbers. A new workspace can show empty tiles until reviews finish.

## Read At a glance

Click **Review** in the left navigation. **Overview** opens first. Its top strip, **At a glance**, sums up this week:

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Fixed before merge** | The share of findings fixed before merge, with how many of how many findings and the period it covers |
| **Review time saved** | Estimated review time saved this week, compared with last week |
| **Fixes written by AI** | Fixes CloudThinker pushed this week, compared with last week |
| **Risky releases stopped** | Releases on hold now, by name. It reads **None on hold now** when there are none |

**Fixed before merge** and **Risky releases stopped** appear only when there is data for them. Click a tile to open its detail: the first three open [Review Insights](/guide/code-review/analytics), and **Risky releases stopped** opens the held releases on [Releases](/guide/code-review/releases). **All insights** beside the heading also opens Insights.

The rate counts findings, not changes. A change with several findings counts several times.

## Work the Needs attention list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the list">
    **Needs attention** lists the items waiting on a decision, with their total beside the heading. Each row shows its kind (**Change**, **Finding**, **Release** or **Infra changes**), the change reference, how long ago it started, and a status: **Open**, **In review**, **Proposed change** or **On hold**. Findings and changes carry a severity icon.

    The list shows five rows. Click **Show all** to see the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the next action">
    Click the row title or its action:

    | Action | What it opens |
    |---|---|
    | **Review change** | The change in the [Review workspace](/guide/code-review/review-workspace) |
    | **Review fix** | The follow-up MR/PR that holds a proposed fix, in your Git provider |
    | **Open source** | The finding's code in your Git provider |
    | **Review release** | The release report. See [Releases](/guide/code-review/releases) |
    | **Review infra changes** | The **Infra** tab |

    **Success state:** you have opened the change, release or finding behind a row and know its next action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To ask a question about these numbers, click **Ask Review** in the page header. For developer comparisons, historical quality metrics, and repository PDFs, open [Review Insights](/guide/code-review/analytics).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Could not load At a glance">
    Click **Retry**. If the error continues, ask an Admin to check your workspace access and Review connection. The **Needs attention** list loads on its own and can still work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Could not load items needing attention">
    Click **Retry**. If **Show all** fails to load more rows, the message **Could not load more items** appears and the rows already loaded stay on the page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add a repository to start reviewing code">
    No repository is connected yet. Click **Add repository** and follow [Manage Review Repositories](/guide/code-review/repository-management).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No tracked items need a decision right now">
    Nothing is waiting on you. To inspect completed reviews or resolved findings, open the **Changes** tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fixed before merge is missing">
    The tile needs findings from finished reviews. Check back after reviews finish, or open a review directly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
