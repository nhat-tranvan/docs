Codebase Atlas turns your merged pull requests (PRs) and merge requests (MRs) into a living map of your repository. Open it to see how the codebase is moving this week and which areas need a look.

<AtlasScene />

## Why use Codebase Atlas

- **See the week on one screen.** Merged work, momentum and the kind of work, without opening each MR.
- **Know where changes land.** A CTO or team lead sees which parts of the product moved, and how much.
- **Spot what needs attention.** Open findings and quality show beside each area.
- **Understand the architecture.** The map shows components and how they use each other, which helps new engineers find their way.
- **Connect code to the business.** Atlas also maps your product's business flows, so Review can flag a change that breaks one. See [Business flow checks](/guide/code-review/business-flow).

## Prerequisites

- A repository connected through [Review setup](/guide/code-review/setup), with its setup finished.
- Review view permission to read an Atlas. Turning Atlas on or changing its branch needs Review edit permission; ask a workspace Admin if the controls are unavailable. See [roles](/guide/security/roles).

## Turn on Atlas

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Codebase Atlas">
    Click **Review** in the left navigation, then open **Codebase Atlas**. Select a repository from the picker at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a branch and build">
    In **Build your Codebase Atlas**, choose the **Branch** to follow. The default branch is selected for you. Click **Build Atlas**.

    The page shows **Building your Codebase Atlas**. You can leave the page; the Atlas appears when it finishes.

    **Success state:** the page shows your repository's Atlas and its merged changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Atlas then updates every day. The setup card shows the daily update time.

To turn Atlas on or off for many repositories, open **More actions → Manage repositories**. In the **Atlas branch** column, pick a branch for each repository, or pick **Off**. See [repository management](/guide/code-review/repository-management).

## Read the week

Atlas opens on the **Review** view for the **Last 7 days**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a period">
    Open the period picker for **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Custom range**. Use the previous and next arrows to step through periods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the period">
    The first section sums up the period in three tiles:

    | Tile | What it shows |
    |---|---|
    | **Momentum** | Merged changes compared with the same point last period |
    | **Focus** | The areas that took most of the work |
    | **Kind of work** | The split between **New**, **Fixed** and **Upkeep** |

    Click **Analyze in Chat** to ask CloudThinker what advanced and what needs a decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See where and who">
    **Where and who** lists merges by area. Switch to **By person** to see each person's work across areas. Click **Open map** to see the same period on the map.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Explore the map

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the map">
    Choose **Map** in the view switcher, beside **Review** and **People**. The **Summary** cards above the map show **Merged MRs**, **Largest Impact share**, **Needs a look** and **Quality** for the period.

    Use **Zoom in**, **Zoom out** and **Fit map to screen** to move around a large map.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an area or component">
    Click a group to expand it and see its components. Click a component to open its panel.

    The **Overview** tab shows its numbers, the components it uses and is used by, and changes that need attention. The **Changes** tab lists every merged change in the period, with search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a change">
    Select a change to see its Impact, Quality, summary and what changed. Click **Open review** to see its findings in the [Review workspace](/guide/code-review/review-workspace).

    **Success state:** you can trace an area of the map to the merged change behind it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With nothing selected, the **Overview** panel shows the whole period. Click **Overview** at the top right to reopen it. Impact and Quality use the same scores as [Review Insights](/guide/code-review/analytics).

From a component panel, **Copy changelog** copies the period's changes for that area, and **Open in Insights** opens [Review Insights](/guide/code-review/analytics).

To share what you see, open **More actions → Copy link**. The link keeps the repository, period and selected component.

## Explore people

Choose **People** in the view switcher to see who changed which areas in the period. Select a person or a cell to open their changes. A component panel also has a **People** tab with its contributors.

Use it to find who knows an area, or where one person's work landed. It shows contribution, not performance. Bot changes are counted separately.

## How it works

1. **Map.** CloudThinker reads the branch you chose and draws its components.
2. **Place.** Each merged change lands on the component it touched.
3. **Score.** Each change carries its Review Impact, Quality and open findings.
4. **Update.** Atlas refreshes every day.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No merges in the selected period">
    **Cause:** nothing merged into the Atlas branch in that period. The map stays visible.

    **Fix:** click **View** with the dates of the last merge, or choose a longer period such as **Last 30 days**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Building your Atlas">
    **Cause:** the first build or a daily update has not finished.

    **Fix:** wait, or leave the page and come back. If the page stops updating, click **Refresh** when it is offered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your assessment needs another try">
    **Cause:** the first build did not finish. The page names the reason, for example **Branch unavailable** or **Took too long**.

    **Fix:** check the repository connection and the branch, then click **Retry assessment**. Your repository and branch choice are saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Atlas branch no longer exists">
    **Cause:** the branch was renamed or deleted.

    **Fix:** open **More actions → Manage repositories** and pick another branch in the **Atlas branch** column.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Connect a repository first">
    **Cause:** no repository has finished Review setup.

    **Fix:** click **Review settings** and finish or retry setup for the repository.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ask an admin to build the Atlas">
    **Cause:** your role can view Review but cannot edit it.

    **Fix:** ask a workspace Admin to build the Atlas or update your role.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
