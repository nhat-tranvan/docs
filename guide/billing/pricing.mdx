CloudThinker plans are priced per seat (one seat per member) and include a monthly pool of credits, the unit that agent work consumes. Start on Free, then upgrade when you need more than direct chat on the Light model.

## Choose your plan

|  | **Free** | **Team** | **Scale** | **Scale+** | **Enterprise** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly billing** | Free | $25 / seat | $100 / seat | $200 / seat | Custom |
| **Yearly billing** | Free | $20 / seat | $80 / seat | $160 / seat | Custom |
| **Premium credits** | None | 200 / seat | 1,000 / seat | 2,000 / seat | Custom |
| **Daily free credits** | 100 / day | 100 / day | 100 / day | 100 / day | Custom |
| **Seats** | 1 | 1 to 5 | 5 to 100 | 5 to 100 | 20+ |
| **Workspaces** | 1 | 1 | Up to 5 | Up to 10 | Unlimited |
| **Models** | Light only | Light, Pro, Ultra | Light, Pro, Ultra | Light, Pro, Ultra | Light, Pro, Ultra |
| **Deployment** | SaaS | SaaS | SaaS | SaaS | Self-host or BYOC |
| **Support** | Community | Community | Priority, 99.9% SLA | Priority, 99.9% SLA | Dedicated, 99.95% SLA |

How to read the table:

- **Yearly billing saves 20%** on the seat price. Credits are the same either way.
- **Premium credits are one pool for the whole organization.** Every workspace draws from it. To stop one workspace from using it all, set [credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits).
- **Daily free credits** cover chat on the Light model only. [Usage](/guide/billing/usage) explains which runs use which credits.
- **Scale and Scale+ share one card** on the plans page. Pick **1x** for Scale or **2x** for Scale+.
- **Over 100 seats** means Enterprise. Click **Contact Sales** on the Enterprise card.

The three chat apps share one plan gate, so a plan that unlocks Slack also unlocks Teams and Google Chat. On Free, any run other than direct chat is refused with "The Free plan only supports direct chat." One exception: a Free organization with a promotional Review grant can run Review.

## Which plan to pick

Pick the lowest plan that covers what you need on day one:

| You want to | Lowest plan |
|---|---|
| Try CloudThinker in chat at no cost | Free |
| Run Review, Resolve, Optimize, or Cyber, or answer from Slack | Team |
| Sign in with your identity provider, keep an audit trail, or cap spend per workspace | Scale |
| Same as Scale with twice the credits per seat | Scale+ |
| Run CloudThinker on your own infrastructure | Enterprise |

## Upgrade your plan

Only the organization **Owner** can change the plan. Admins can view usage but don't see **Billing**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Billing**. The page is titled **Subscription & Billing** and shows your current plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the plans page">
    Click **Upgrade Plan**. The **Pricing Plans** page lists every plan for your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick monthly or yearly">
    Use the switcher to choose **Billing Monthly** or **Billing Yearly**. Prices on the cards update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Click the plan's button, for example **Upgrade to Team**. On the Scale card, pick **1x** or **2x** first. Finish checkout.

    **Success state:** the plan's button reads **Current Plan**, and **Admin Settings → Billing** shows the new plan name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add seats, change interval, or download invoices after you upgrade, see [Subscription](/guide/billing/subscription).

## Start a Team trial

Free organizations that are eligible see **Start 14-Day Free Trial** on the Team card. Click it to get Team features for 14 days.

- The sidebar counts down the days left, for example "Team trial · 5 days left".
- Pay-As-You-Go stays off during the trial.
- To keep Team after the trial, open **Admin Settings → Billing → Upgrade Plan** and click **Add payment method** on the Team card.

<Warning>
  If you don't add a payment method, the organization returns to Free when the trial ends. Runs other than direct chat stop, and Pro and Ultra models lock.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see Billing in Admin Settings">
    **Cause:** only the organization Owner can open **Billing**. **Fix:** ask your Owner to upgrade. Admins still see **Usage** and **Credit limits**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A plan button says Closed to new signups">
    That plan no longer accepts new subscriptions. Existing subscribers keep it. Choose Team, Scale, or Scale+ instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A plan card says One fixed price for your whole team">
    That plan is billed per organization, not per seat. The price on the card is the full price. Read its feature list on the card before you choose.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pro and Ultra are locked in the model picker">
    You're on Free, which runs the Light model only. Upgrade to Team or above to unlock Pro and Ultra.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
