CloudThinker offers flexible per-seat plans, from a free daily tier for individuals to Enterprise for large multi-cloud teams. Start free, then scale as you grow.

<Info>
  Start free with **100 credits every day** — [credits](/guide/billing/usage) are the
  currency agent work consumes. The free daily credits cover the Light model and chat,
  enough to explore the platform at no cost, no credit card needed.
</Info>

<Tip>Save **~20%** with annual billing on any paid plan (seat price only). The Team plan includes a **14-day free trial** — no charge until day 15.</Tip>

Credits are a single pool shared across all workspaces in your organization. Scale caps at 100 seats — beyond 100, move to Enterprise. On the plans page, Scale and Scale+ share one **Scale** card: pick **1x** for Scale or **2x** for Scale+.

The Free plan runs direct chat only: web chat, Rooms, and CLI chat. Other runs, such as Review, RCA, and Automations, need a paid plan. A Free organization with a promotional Review grant can also run Review.

A plan with a ✓ in the **Messaging integrations** row is **messaging-eligible**: Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Google Chat share this one gate, so a plan that unlocks one unlocks all three.
