CloudThinker plans are priced per seat (one seat per member) and include a monthly pool of credits, the unit that agent work consumes. Start on Free, then upgrade when you need more than direct chat on the Light model.

## Choose your plan

|  | **Free** | **Team** | **Scale** | **Scale Plus** | **Enterprise** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly billing** | Free | $25 / seat | $100 / seat | $200 / seat | Custom |
| **Yearly billing** | Free | $20 / seat | $80 / seat | $160 / seat | Custom |
| **Premium credits** | None | 200 / seat | 1,000 / seat | 2,000 / seat | Custom |
| **Daily free credits** | 100 / day per organization | 100 / day per organization | 100 / day per organization | 100 / day per organization | Custom |
| **Seats** | 1 | 1 to 5 | 5 to 100 | 5 to 100 | 20+ |
| **Workspaces** | 1 | 1 | Up to 5 | Up to 10 | Unlimited |
| **Models** | Light only | Light, Pro, Ultra | Light, Pro, Ultra | Light, Pro, Ultra | Light, Pro, Ultra |
| **Deployment** | SaaS | SaaS | SaaS | SaaS | SaaS, plus dedicated, self-hosted, and air-gapped deployments in early access. See [Deployment options](/guide/deployment/overview) |
| **Support** | Community | Community | Priority, 99.9% SLA | Priority, 99.9% SLA | Dedicated, 99.95% SLA |

How to read the table:

- **Yearly billing saves 20%** on the seat price. Credits are the same either way.
- **Premium credits are one pool for the whole organization.** Every workspace draws from it. To stop one workspace from using it all, set [credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits).
- **Daily free credits** cover chat on the Light model only. The organization shares one allowance of 100, which resets at 00:00 UTC. Unused daily credits don't carry over. [Usage](/guide/billing/usage) explains which runs use which credits.
- **There's no fixed credit price per run.** Cost depends on how much work a run does and its [model tier](/guide/billing/usage#what-makes-a-run-cost-more). To size a plan, check **Admin Settings → Usage → By Feature** after a week of real use.
- **Metered overage costs $0.10 per credit.** On Team and above, the Owner can turn on [Pay-As-You-Go](/guide/billing/usage#pay-as-you-go) and set a monthly cap, so runs keep going once the plan's credits are spent.
- **Scale and Scale Plus share one card** on the plans page. **1x** is Scale ($100 / seat, 1,000 credits per seat). **2x** is Scale Plus ($200 / seat, 2,000 credits per seat, up to 10 workspaces). Features are the same on both.
- **Workers are self-serve and in beta.** A workspace Admin turns them on, and they run agent file and shell work on a machine you control. Only the dedicated, self-hosted, and air-gapped options in the **Deployment** row are early access. See [Workers](/guide/workers).
- **The SLA is an uptime commitment**, and it's the figure the plans page shows: 99.9% on Scale and Scale Plus, 99.95% on Enterprise. Free and Team don't include one.
- **Enterprise starts at 20 seats**, and it's the only option above 100 seats. Click **Contact Sales** on the Enterprise card and send the contact form, or email support@cloudthinker.io.

## Which plan to pick

Pick the lowest plan that covers what you need on day one:

| You want to | Lowest plan |
|---|---|
| Try CloudThinker in chat at no cost | Free |
| Run Review, Resolve, Optimize, or Cyber, or answer from Slack | Team |
| Sign in with your identity provider, keep an audit trail, or cap spend per workspace | Scale |
| Same as Scale with twice the credits per seat | Scale Plus |
| Run CloudThinker on your own infrastructure (early access) | Enterprise |

## Upgrade your plan

Only the organization **Owner** can change the plan. Admins can view usage but don't see **Billing**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Billing**. The page is titled **Subscription & Billing** and shows your current plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the plans page">
    Click **Upgrade Plan**. The **Pricing Plans** page lists every plan for your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick monthly or yearly">
    Use the switcher to choose **Billing Monthly** or **Billing Yearly**. Prices on the cards update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Click the plan's button, for example **Upgrade to Team**. On the Scale card, pick **1x** or **2x** first. A confirmation dialog shows the price before anything is charged. Click its button to confirm. [Change your plan](/guide/billing/subscription#change-your-plan) explains what each button label means, including when Stripe asks for a card.

    **Success state:** the plan's button reads **Current Plan**, and **Admin Settings → Billing** shows the new plan name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add seats, change interval, or download invoices after you upgrade, see [Subscription](/guide/billing/subscription).

## Start a Team trial

Free organizations that have never used a trial see **Start 14-Day Free Trial** on the Team card. Click it to get Team features for 14 days.

- The sidebar counts down the days left, for example "Team trial · 5 days left". On **Admin Settings → Billing**, the plan card shows **Free trial until \<date\>**.
- You can't buy or reduce seats during the trial. The seat controls read **Seat management available after trial**.
- Pay-As-You-Go stays off during the trial.
- To keep Team after the trial, open **Admin Settings → Billing → Upgrade Plan** and click **Add payment method** on the Team card.

<Warning>
  If you don't add a payment method, the organization returns to Free when the trial ends. Runs other than direct chat stop, and Pro and Ultra models lock.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see Billing in Admin Settings">
    **Cause:** only the organization Owner can open **Billing**. **Fix:** ask your Owner to upgrade. Admins still see **Usage** and **Credit limits**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A plan button says Closed to new signups">
    That's an older plan that isn't in the table above, such as Business. It no longer accepts new subscriptions, and existing subscribers keep it. Choose Team, Scale, or Scale Plus instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A plan card says One fixed price for your whole team">
    That's an older flat-rate plan that isn't in the table above. It's billed per organization, not per seat, so the price on the card is the full price. Read its feature list on the card before you choose.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pro and Ultra are locked in the model picker">
    You're on Free, which runs the Light model only. Upgrade to Team or above to unlock Pro and Ultra.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
