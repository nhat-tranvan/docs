Team and Scale each have a fixed monthly price for your organization, with unlimited members and a shared credit pool. Enterprise pricing is tailored to your organization.

## Choose your plan

| | **Team** | **Scale** | **Enterprise** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly price for your organization** | $99 | $499 | Private offer |
| **Monthly credits** | 1,000 | 5,000 | Pay-As-You-Go |
| **Members** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Workspaces** | 3 | 10 | Unlimited |
| **Isolated sandbox size** | Medium | Large | Custom |
| **Deployment** | SaaS | SaaS | Self-host / BYOC |
| **BYOK** | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **SSO / SCIM** | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Audit log** | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Pay-As-You-Go** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Support** | Email support | Priority support | Dedicated support |
| **SLA** | — | 99.9% | 99.95% |

Adding members does not change your monthly plan price or credit allowance. Every workspace draws from the same organization credit pool. See [Usage](/guide/billing/usage) to track credit use and [credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits) to manage spending by workspace.

For Enterprise pricing and deployment, [contact us](https://cloudthinker.io/contact).

## Free access

Free remains available for direct chat on Light with 100 daily credits shared by the organization. These credits reset at 00:00 UTC and do not carry over.

## Which plan to pick

Pick the lowest plan that covers what you need on day one:

| You want to | Lowest plan |
|---|---|
| Try CloudThinker in chat at no cost | Free |
| Run Review, Resolve, Optimize, or Cyber, or answer from Slack | Team |
| Sign in with your identity provider, keep an audit trail, or cap spend per workspace | Scale |
| Run CloudThinker on your own infrastructure (early access) | Enterprise |

## Upgrade your plan

Only the organization **Owner** can change the plan. Admins can view usage but don't see **Billing**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Billing**. The page is titled **Subscription & Billing** and shows your current plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the plans page">
    Click **Upgrade Plan**. The **Pricing Plans** page lists every plan for your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Click the plan's button, for example **Upgrade to Team**. A confirmation dialog shows the price before anything is charged. Click its button to confirm. [Change your plan](/guide/billing/subscription#change-your-plan) explains what each button label means, including when Stripe asks for a card.

    **Success state:** the plan's button reads **Current Plan**, and **Admin Settings → Billing** shows the new plan name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To manage payment details or download invoices after you upgrade, see [Subscription](/guide/billing/subscription).

## Start a Team trial

Free organizations that have never used a trial see **Start 14-Day Free Trial** on the Team card. Click it to get Team features for 14 days.

- The sidebar counts down the days left, for example "Team trial · 5 days left". On **Admin Settings → Billing**, the plan card shows **Free trial until \<date\>**.
- Pay-As-You-Go stays off during the trial.
- To keep Team after the trial, open **Admin Settings → Billing → Upgrade Plan** and click **Add payment method** on the Team card.

<Warning>
  If you don't add a payment method, the organization returns to Free when the trial ends. Runs other than direct chat stop, and Pro and Ultra models lock.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see Billing in Admin Settings">
    **Cause:** only the organization Owner can open **Billing**. **Fix:** ask your Owner to upgrade. Admins still see **Usage** and **Credit limits**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A plan button says Closed to new signups">
    That's an older plan that isn't in the table above, such as Business. It no longer accepts new subscriptions, and existing subscribers keep it. Choose Team or Scale instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A plan card says One fixed price for your whole team">
    Team and Scale use one monthly price for the whole organization. The card shows the full plan price, which includes unlimited members.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pro and Ultra are locked in the model picker">
    You're on Free, which runs the Light model only. Upgrade to Team or above to unlock Pro and Ultra.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
