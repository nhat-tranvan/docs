Manage your CloudThinker plan, seats, and invoices from **Admin Settings → Billing**. This page covers changing plans, how seats work, and where to find payment history.

## View your current plan

Open **Admin Settings → Billing** to see your current plan, credit usage, billing cycle, upcoming charges, and invoice history in one place.

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    Navigate to **Admin Settings → Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change Plan">
    Compare the available plans. See [Pricing](/guide/billing/pricing) for what each plan includes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your new plan">
    Choose the plan and billing interval (monthly or annual).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the changes and confirm">
    **Success state:** the Billing page shows your new plan and next billing date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Plan changes take effect immediately. Upgrades are prorated — you pay only for the remainder of the current billing period. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
</Info>

### Switch your billing interval

The same **Change Plan** flow switches an existing plan between monthly and annual billing. Annual billing reduces the seat price by roughly 20% — review the confirmation screen for when the new interval takes effect.

### Free trial

New Team subscriptions include a **14-day free trial** — you are not charged until day 15. [Pay-As-You-Go](/guide/billing/usage#pay-as-you-go) stays locked until the trial ends.

## Add team members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Organization settings">
    Navigate to **Admin Settings → Organization**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Members">
    Enter the email addresses of the people you want to add.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select roles and workspace access">
    Send the invitations. When a new member joins beyond your current seat count, a seat is added automatically with prorated billing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How seats work

A **seat** is a paid slot on your subscription. Each seat grants its full credit allocation immediately upon purchase.

- **Only active members use seats.** Pending invitations don't count toward your seat usage.
- **Pre-purchase seats for more credits.** Buy extra seats to boost your organization's credit pool, even before team members join.
- **Member departures.** Credits stay intact for the current cycle. Seat count adjusts at the next renewal.

See [Pricing](/guide/billing/pricing) for per-plan seat ranges and credit allocations.

## Invoices and payments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    Navigate to **Admin Settings → Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage your payment method">
    Add or update the card your subscription charges. Enterprise billing is arranged directly with the CloudThinker team — [contact us](https://cloudthinker.io/contact).
  </Step>
  <Step title="View and download invoices">
    Filter your invoice history by date range and export invoices for accounting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Billing cycle

| Plan type | When you're billed |
|---|---|
| Monthly plans | On your subscription start date each month |
| Annual plans | Once per year, at a discounted seat price |
| [Pay-As-You-Go](/guide/billing/usage#pay-as-you-go) usage | Metered and charged at the end of each billing cycle |
