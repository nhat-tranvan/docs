Manage your organization’s subscription from **Settings → Organization → Billing**. See [Pricing](/guide/billing/pricing) for monthly prices, credits, and workspace limits.

## Who can manage billing

The organization **Owner** and **Admins** can open **Settings → Organization → Billing** and see the plan, credit limits, and invoices. Only the Owner can change plans, update payment details, or turn on Pay-As-You-Go. An Admin sees **Owner access required.** under **Plan and payment**. Other members don't see **Billing**. If you need a change and aren't the Owner, ask them. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles) for what each role can do.

## Read the Billing page

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Plan and payment** | Your plan name and price, with a status line: `Renews <date>`, `Free trial until <date>`, or `Ends <date>` if you canceled. **Compare plans** jumps to **Plans** |
| Payment method | The card on file, with a **Card declined** badge if the last charge failed, and **Manage in Stripe**, which opens the Stripe billing portal in a new tab for card, billing details and cancellation |
| **Account credit** | Shown when you have a balance: a dollar amount applied to your next invoice automatically. It's money, separate from the usage credits that agent work consumes |
| **Credit limits** | Per-workspace credit budgets and the **Pay as you go** switch. See [Credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits) and [Pay-As-You-Go](/guide/billing/usage#pay-as-you-go) |
| **Plans** | Every plan for your organization, with a **Monthly** or **Yearly** switch, where you compare and change plans |
| **Invoices** | Your invoices, newest first, each with its date, period, amount, status, and a **View** link |

Team, Scale, and Enterprise include unlimited members. Invite members from **Settings → Organization → Members**. Adding members does not increase the plan price or credit pool.

The Free plan has no Stripe subscription, so it shows no payment method.

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plans">
    Go to **Settings → Organization → Billing** and click **Compare plans**. The page scrolls to **Plans**, which lists every plan, with your current plan marked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select the plan and review the price shown for your organization. Compare what each plan includes on [Pricing](/guide/billing/pricing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the confirmation">
    The confirmation dialog shows a price preview before anything is charged. The button tells you the outcome:

    | Button | Meaning |
    |---|---|
    | **Subscribe - $X/month** | First paid subscription |
    | **Confirm & Pay $X** | Upgrade with a prorated charge today: the price difference for the time left in the current period |
    | **Confirm ($X Credit)** | The change leaves you credit, applied to a later invoice |
    | **Confirm (No Charge)** | Nothing is due today |
    | **Continue to payment** | No card on file yet, so Stripe Checkout opens to collect one |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click the button.

    **Success state:** a "Pricing updated successfully" message appears, and **Plan and payment** shows the new plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To talk about Enterprise, click **Contact sales** on the Enterprise card under **Plans** and send the contact form, or email support@cloudthinker.io.

### Downgrades

You can't downgrade from **Plans**. Picking a lower plan shows **Downgrades Not Available**. To move down, cancel your current subscription (see [Cancel your subscription](#cancel-your-subscription)), then subscribe to the lower plan. Or click **Contact Support** in that alert.

### Switch between monthly and yearly on an older subscription

Under **Plans**, pick **Yearly** in the interval switch, then click your current plan's button. The dialog is titled **Switch to Yearly Billing**. Moving from yearly to monthly isn't self-service: the page tells you to email support to switch.

**Success state:** "Billing interval updated successfully" appears.

## Start a Team trial

Free organizations that have never used a trial can start a 14-day trial of Team from **Plans**. [Start a Team trial](/guide/billing/pricing#start-a-team-trial) on Pricing covers who's eligible, what changes during the trial, and how to keep Team afterwards.

## Add or remove seats

<Note>
  This section applies only to older subscriptions billed per seat. New Team, Scale, and Enterprise plans include unlimited members and do not require seat purchases.
</Note>

A seat is one paid member slot on a per-seat plan. The Free plan includes 1 seat. Members and **pending invitations both use a seat**, so an unaccepted invite still counts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Seat Allocation">
    Go to **Settings → Organization → Billing**. On the **Seats** row under **Plan and payment**, click **Manage**. The **Seat Allocation** dialog shows **Purchased**, **In Use** and **Available**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy or reduce seats">
    Click **Buy More Seats** to add seats, or **Reduce Seats** to remove unused ones. Enter the number and confirm.

    **Success state:** the **Available** count updates. After a reduction you see "Seats removed successfully".
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can only remove unused seats. If every seat is taken, cancel pending invitations or remove members first. Reducing seats keeps the premium credits you already have.
</Note>

When seats run out, **Invite Members** on the Members page is locked and shows **No seats available**. Click it to open **Seat Allocation** and buy more. Some plans also have a maximum seat count. At that cap you see **Plan seat limit reached**, and you need to upgrade to add more people. Check your existing subscription for its seat limits. [Pricing](/guide/billing/pricing) lists the current plans for your organization.

To invite people once you have seats, see [Organization](/guide/organization#invite-members).

## Update your card or billing details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Stripe portal">
    On **Settings → Organization → Billing**, click **Manage in Stripe**. If a payment failed, the same button reads **Update payment method**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the card or details in Stripe">
    Make the change in the Stripe tab, then return to CloudThinker.

    **Success state:** the payment method row shows the new card and the **Card declined** badge is gone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancel your subscription

Click **Manage in Stripe** and cancel from the Stripe billing portal.

**Success state:** the status line under **Plan and payment** changes to **Ends \<date\>**. You keep your plan until that date.

## Download invoices

The **Invoices** section on **Billing** shows your 10 latest invoices. Click **Show all** to see the rest. A status of `paid` means it's settled, and `open` means it's still due. Click **View** to open the Stripe-hosted invoice, where you can download a PDF.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't see Billing in Settings">
    Only the organization Owner and Admins can open **Billing**, and only the Owner can change it. Ask your Owner to make the change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A Payment failed banner appears">
    Your last charge didn't go through. Click **Update payment method**, fix the card in Stripe, and the banner clears once payment succeeds. Act quickly: if the payment stays unpaid, the organization can be moved to the Free plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your plan was downgraded">
    The payment wasn't recovered, so the organization moved to the Free plan. The alert calls it the Starter plan, which is the older name for Free. The previous subscription is canceled and can't be restored. Click **Compare plans** to subscribe again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The confirm button says Add a card to continue or Update card to continue">
    The change needs a working card. Click **Manage in Stripe**, add or fix the card, then run the plan change again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The confirm button says Resolve seat limit">
    On an older per-seat subscription, the plan you picked allows fewer seats than you use now. Remove members or cancel pending invitations, or pick a larger plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Finish your pending plan update before choosing another plan">
    An earlier plan change is still activating. Reopen the same plan under **Plans** to finish it before you pick a different one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
