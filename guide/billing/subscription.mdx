Your subscription is the plan your organization pays for (Free, Team, Scale, Scale+ or Enterprise) plus the number of seats on it. You manage all of it on **Admin Settings → Billing**, a page titled **Subscription & Billing**.

## Who can manage billing

Only the organization **Owner** can open **Admin Settings → Billing**, change plans, buy seats or update payment details. Other members who try to open it land on **Admin Settings → Organization** instead. If you need a change and aren't the Owner, ask them. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles) for what each role can do.

## Read the Subscription & Billing page

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Current Plan** | Plan name and price per interval, with the per-seat breakdown (`$X/seat × 5 seats`) |
| Renewal line | `Auto-renews: <date>`, `Free trial until <date>`, or `Ends <date>` if you canceled |
| **Upgrade Plan** | Opens **Pricing Plans**, where you compare and change plans |
| **Manage in Stripe** | Opens the Stripe billing portal in a new tab for card, billing details and cancellation |
| Payment method | The card on file, with a **Card declined** badge if the last charge failed |
| **Account Credit** | A dollar balance on your account, applied to your next invoice automatically. It's money, separate from the usage credits that agent work consumes |
| **Invoices** | Every invoice with **Date**, **Total**, **Status** and a **View** link |

Two related settings live elsewhere: seats are under **Admin Settings → Organization** (see [Add or remove seats](#add-or-remove-seats)), and the Pay-As-You-Go switch and cap are on **Admin Settings → Usage** (see [Pay-As-You-Go](/guide/billing/usage#pay-as-you-go)).

The Free plan has no Stripe subscription, so it shows only **Upgrade Plan**.

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pricing Plans">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Billing** and click **Upgrade Plan**. The **Pricing Plans** page lists every plan, with your current plan marked at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan and billing interval">
    Choose monthly or yearly billing, then select the plan. Compare what each plan includes on [Pricing](/guide/billing/pricing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the confirmation">
    The confirmation dialog shows a price preview before anything is charged. The button tells you the outcome:

    | Button | Meaning |
    |---|---|
    | **Subscribe - $X/month** | First paid subscription |
    | **Confirm & Pay $X** | Upgrade with a prorated charge today: the price difference for the time left in the current period |
    | **Confirm ($X Credit)** | The change leaves you credit, applied to a later invoice |
    | **Confirm (No Charge)** | Nothing is due today |
    | **Continue to payment** | No card on file yet, so Stripe Checkout opens to collect one |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click the button.

    **Success state:** a "Pricing updated successfully" message appears and you're returned to **Subscription & Billing**, which shows the new plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To talk about Enterprise, click **Contact Sales** on the Enterprise card on **Pricing Plans** and send the contact form, or email support@cloudthinker.io.

### Downgrades

You can't downgrade from **Pricing Plans**. Picking a lower plan shows **Downgrades Not Available**. To move down, cancel your current subscription (see [Cancel your subscription](#cancel-your-subscription)), then subscribe to the lower plan. Or click **Contact Support** in that alert.

### Switch between monthly and yearly

Use the same **Upgrade Plan** flow and pick your current plan with the other interval selected. The dialog is titled **Switch to Yearly Billing** (yearly saves 20%) or **Switch to Monthly Billing**.

**Success state:** "Billing interval updated successfully" appears.

## Start a Team trial

Free organizations that have never used a trial can start a 14-day trial of Team from **Pricing Plans**. [Start a Team trial](/guide/billing/pricing#start-a-team-trial) on Pricing covers who's eligible, what changes during the trial, and how to keep Team afterwards.

## Add or remove seats

A seat is one paid member slot on a per-seat plan. The Free plan includes 1 seat. Members and **pending invitations both use a seat**, so an unaccepted invite still counts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Seat Allocation">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Organization**. Under **Total seats**, click **Manage**. The **Seat Allocation** dialog shows **Purchased**, **In Use** and **Available**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy or reduce seats">
    Click **Buy More Seats** to add seats, or **Reduce Seats** to remove unused ones. Enter the number and confirm.

    **Success state:** the **Available** count updates. After a reduction you see "Seats removed successfully".
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can only remove seats nobody is using. If every seat is taken, cancel pending invitations or remove members first. Reducing seats keeps the premium credits you already have.
</Note>

When seats run out, **Invite Members** on the Organization page is locked and shows **No seats available**. Click it to open **Seat Allocation** and buy more. Some plans also have a maximum seat count. At that cap you see **Plan seat limit reached**, and you need to upgrade to add more people. Seat ranges per plan are on [Pricing](/guide/billing/pricing).

To invite people once you have seats, see [Organization](/guide/organization#invite-members).

## Update your card or billing details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Stripe portal">
    On **Admin Settings → Billing**, click **Manage in Stripe**. If a payment failed, the same button reads **Update payment method**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the card or details in Stripe">
    Make the change in the Stripe tab, then return to CloudThinker.

    **Success state:** the payment method row shows the new card and the **Card declined** badge is gone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancel your subscription

Click **Manage in Stripe** and cancel from the Stripe billing portal.

**Success state:** the renewal line on **Current Plan** changes to **Ends \<date\>**. You keep your plan until that date.

## Download invoices

The **Invoices** table on **Subscription & Billing** lists every invoice, 10 per page. A status of `paid` means it's settled, and `open` means it's still due. Click **View** to open the Stripe-hosted invoice, where you can download a PDF.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't see Billing in Admin Settings">
    Only the organization Owner can manage billing. Ask your Owner to make the change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A Payment failed banner appears">
    Your last charge didn't go through. Click **Update payment method**, fix the card in Stripe, and the banner clears once payment succeeds. Act quickly: if the payment stays unpaid, the organization can be moved to the Free plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your plan was downgraded">
    The payment wasn't recovered, so the organization moved to the Free plan. The alert calls it the Starter plan, which is the older name for Free. The previous subscription is canceled and can't be restored. Click **Upgrade Plan** to subscribe again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The confirm button says Add a card to continue or Update card to continue">
    The change needs a working card. Click **Manage in Stripe**, add or fix the card, then run the plan change again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The confirm button says Resolve seat limit">
    The plan you picked allows fewer seats than you use now. Remove members or cancel pending invitations, or pick a larger plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Finish your pending plan update before choosing another plan">
    An earlier plan change is still activating. Reopen the same plan on **Pricing Plans** to finish it before you pick a different one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
