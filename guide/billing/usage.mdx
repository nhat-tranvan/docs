CloudThinker meters agent work in credits. Every chat message, Review, Resolve, Optimize, or Cyber run, and automation consumes credits from your organization's balance. This page explains where those credits come from, what makes a run cost more, and how to read the **Usage** page so you know who and what is spending them.

## Credit types

Your organization can hold up to three kinds of credits, plus an optional paid top-up.

| Credit type | Where it comes from | What it can pay for | Resets |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | 100 free credits on every plan | Chat on the **Light** model only | Every day |
| **Bonus** | Referrals and promotional offers | All models and all features | Never expires |
| **Premium** | Your plan's per-seat allocation (see [Pricing](/guide/billing/pricing)) | All models (Light, Pro, Ultra) and all features | Each billing cycle |
| **Pay-As-You-Go** | Metered top-up at $0.10 per credit, if turned on | All models and all features | Monthly cap resets each cycle |

Premium credits are one pool for the whole organization. Every workspace draws from it, so a single busy workspace can use most of it. To stop that, set [credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits) per workspace.

<Info>
  Annual plans still refresh premium credits monthly, on the anniversary day of the period start. The counter resets. No extra credits are granted.
</Info>

## Which credits a run uses

CloudThinker spends credits in this order and moves to the next pool only when the current one is empty or can't pay for the run:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Daily credits">
    Used first, but only for chat on the Light model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bonus credits">
    Used next, for any model and any feature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Premium credits">
    Your plan's monthly pool, for any model and any feature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay-As-You-Go">
    Charged at $0.10 per credit, only if [Pay-As-You-Go](#pay-as-you-go) is turned on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Example:** a Team member asks a quick question in chat on Light. That run draws from daily credits. The same member then starts a Review run on Pro. Daily credits can't pay for it, so it draws from bonus credits if you have any, then premium credits.

## What makes a run cost more

Each run executes on a model tier, and the tier multiplies its credit charge. The same task costs more than five times as much on Ultra as on Light.

| Tier | Credit multiplier | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Light** | 0.3x | Fast, low-cost answers to simple questions |
| **Pro** (default) | 1.0x | Everyday work |
| **Ultra** | 1.7x | Hard problems that need the most reasoning |

To switch tier in chat, open the settings control on the message box and pick **Light**, **Pro**, or **Ultra**. Each row shows its multiplier. Your choice is remembered in your browser and applies to the runs you start next.

Rules that decide which tier a run can use:

- **Free plan runs Light only.** Pro and Ultra show as locked until you upgrade.
- **Pro and Ultra never use daily credits**, even in chat. They always draw bonus, premium, or Pay-As-You-Go credits.
- **Automations never run on Light.** The [automation](/guide/automation/automations) tier picker offers Pro and Ultra only. An older automation saved on Light runs as Pro.
- **Custom agents carry their own tier**, set in the **Model Tier** field when you [create the agent](/guide/agents/customize).

<Tip>
  To stretch your premium credits, keep routine questions on Light and save Ultra for investigations where Pro gave a weak answer.
</Tip>

## Read the Usage page

**Admin Settings → Usage** shows how much credit your organization has left and where the rest went. The organization Owner and Admins can open it. Only the Owner sees **Billing**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Usage">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Usage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your balances">
    The cards at the top show what's left in **Premium credits**, **Daily credits**, and **Bonus credits**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a time range">
    In the **Credit Usage** chart, choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, or **Last 90 days**, or set your own dates. You can group the chart **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Yearly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find who and what spent it">
    Under **Usage Breakdown**, switch between **By Feature**, **By User**, and **By Workspace**. All three tabs cover the same time range as the chart.
  </Step>
</Steps>

How to use each view:

| View | Question it answers | What to do next |
|---|---|---|
| **By Feature** | Which capability used the credits | Move routine work to a cheaper tier, or slow down a noisy automation |
| **By User** | Which member is spending the most | Check whether they need Ultra, or whether that spend is expected |
| **By Workspace** | Which workspace draws the most from the shared pool | Set a [credit limit](/guide/billing/credit-limits) on it |

Use the **All features** filter on the chart to narrow the trend to a single feature.

## Alerts you get without checking

You don't have to watch the Usage page. CloudThinker sends these on its own:

| Alert | When it arrives |
|---|---|
| **Credit usage warning** | Your organization passes **80%** of its premium credits for the cycle. Sent once per cycle, in-app and by email. |
| **Pay-As-You-Go cap warning** | Pay-As-You-Go spend reaches **80%** of your monthly cap. |
| **Renewal reminder** | About **3 days** before your billing period renews, when more than 10% of your credits are still unused. |
| **Workspace limit alert** | A workspace crosses **80%** or **100%** of its monthly [credit limit](/guide/billing/credit-limits), when those alerts are turned on. |

## Pay-As-You-Go

Pay-As-You-Go tops up your balance automatically once daily, bonus, and premium credits run out, so agent work keeps running.

| | Details |
|---|---|
| **Rate** | $0.10 per credit |
| **Plans** | Team, Scale, Scale+, and Enterprise |
| **Monthly cap** | You set it. Minimum $100. The maximum depends on your plan, and your plan's default is pre-filled. |
| **Billing** | Metered. You pay for what you used at the end of each billing cycle. |
| **Who can change it** | The organization Owner |

<Warning>
  Pay-As-You-Go isn't available on Free and stays off during a Team trial. Upgrade to Team or above to turn it on.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pay-As-You-Go">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Billing** and open **Pay-As-You-Go**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Switch Pay-As-You-Go on. The change takes effect right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the monthly cap">
    Enter a monthly cap of at least $100 and save.

    **Expected result:** once the cap is reached, Pay-As-You-Go stops until the next billing cycle, and runs that need more credits are blocked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The cap resets each billing cycle. Lowering it mid-cycle doesn't refund usage you've already incurred.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I have daily credits left but a run still used premium credits">
    Daily credits only pay for chat on the Light model. A Pro or Ultra run, or any Review, Resolve, Optimize, Cyber, or automation run, draws bonus, premium, or Pay-As-You-Go credits instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Premium credits ran out early in the cycle">
    Open **Admin Settings → Usage** and check **By Workspace** and **By User** for the biggest spender. Move routine work to Light, then cap heavy workspaces with [credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits) or turn on [Pay-As-You-Go](#pay-as-you-go).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Runs stopped even though Pay-As-You-Go is on">
    You've likely hit your monthly cap. Pay-As-You-Go stays stopped until the next billing cycle unless the Owner raises the cap in **Admin Settings → Billing → Pay-As-You-Go**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't find Billing or the Pay-As-You-Go switch">
    Only the organization Owner sees **Billing**. Admins can still open **Usage** and **Credit limits**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pro and Ultra are locked in the model picker">
    You're on Free, which runs Light only. Upgrade to Team or above. See [Pricing](/guide/billing/pricing).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
