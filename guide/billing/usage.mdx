Credits are the currency for AI agent operations. Each interaction with an agent consumes credits based on complexity.

## How credits work

Credits are included with your plan and apply to all agent conversations and API usage:

- Every plan gets **100 free daily credits** that refresh every day, for the **Light** [model tier](#model-tiers-and-credit-cost) and chat only.
- **Premium credits** (your plan's per-seat allocation) unlock **all models and all features** — Light, Pro, and Ultra model tiers across every app capability (agents, code review, incident RCA, dashboards, integrations, and API usage).

## Model tiers and credit cost

Every agent run executes on a model tier, and the tier multiplies the run's credit charge. The same task costs more than five times as much on Ultra as on Light.

| Tier | Credit multiplier | Built for |
|------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Light** | 0.3× | Fast, cost-effective answers for simple tasks |
| **Pro** (default) | 1.0× | The balance of intelligence and speed for everyday work |
| **Ultra** | 1.7× | Maximum reasoning for complex problems |

**Where to switch:** in chat, open the settings control on the message box and pick **Light**, **Pro**, or **Ultra** — each row shows its multiplier. Your last choice is remembered in your browser and applies to the runs you start next.

Four rules shape which tier a run can use:

- **Free plan runs Light only.** Pro and Ultra appear locked in the picker until you upgrade.
- **Daily free credits are Light-and-chat only.** A Pro or Ultra run always draws paid credits, even in chat — see [Consumption order](#consumption-order).
- **Automations never run Light.** The tier picker for [automations](/guide/automation/automations) offers Pro and Ultra only, and an older automation saved on Light runs as Pro.
- **Custom agents carry their own tier.** Set it in the **Model Tier** field when you [create the agent](/guide/agents/customize).

## Consumption order

When you use credits, they are consumed in this order:

1. **Daily credits** — 100 free credits that reset every day, for the **Light model and chat only**. Available on all plans.
2. **Bonus credits** — promotional credits from referrals or special offers. They work with **all models** and **never expire**.
3. **Premium credits** — your plan's per-seat credit allocation, reset each billing cycle. Unlike daily credits, premium credits work with **all models** (Light, Pro, Ultra) and **every app feature** — no model or feature restrictions.
4. **Pay-As-You-Go** — automatic top-up when all other credits are exhausted. Charged at $0.10/credit. Only if [PAYG](#pay-as-you-go) is enabled.

## Read the Usage dashboard

**Admin Settings → Usage** answers "who and what is spending our credits". It shows:

- **Credit cards** — your remaining **Premium**, **Daily**, and **Bonus** balances at a glance.
- **Usage trend** — a chart over any date range, with quick presets for 7, 30, and 90 days plus your current billing cycle.
- **Usage Breakdown** — three tabs slicing the same range **By Feature** (which capability consumed the credits), **By User**, and **By Workspace**.

To go beyond monitoring and cap what a workspace can spend, set [credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits).

## What CloudThinker tells you and when

You don't have to watch the dashboard — CloudThinker sends these signals on its own:

| Signal | When it arrives |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Credit usage warning** | Your organization passes **80%** of its premium credits for the cycle. Sent once per cycle, in-app and by email. |
| **PAYG cap warning** | Pay-As-You-Go spend reaches **80%** of your monthly cap. |
| **Renewal reminder** | About **3 days** before your billing period renews, when more than 10% of your credits are still unused. |
| **Workspace limit alert** | A workspace crosses **80% or 100%** of its monthly [credit limit](/guide/billing/credit-limits), when those alerts are enabled. |

And each credit pool resets on its own schedule:

| Pool | Resets |
|------|--------|
| Daily credits | Every day |
| Premium credits | Each billing cycle. Annual plans still refresh monthly, on the anniversary day of the period start — the counter resets, no extra credits are granted. |
| Bonus credits | Never expire |

## Pay-As-You-Go

Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG) automatically tops up your balance when all your other credits are exhausted, so agent operations keep running.

|                          | Details                                                                        |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Rate**                 | $0.10 per credit                                                               |
| **Availability**         | Team, Scale, Scale+, and Enterprise plans                                      |
| **Spending cap** | Set your own monthly limit — minimum \$100; maximum depends on your plan tier |
| **Billing**              | Metered — you only pay for what you use at the end of each billing cycle       |
| **Control**              | Enable or disable anytime from your subscription settings                      |

<Warning>
  PAYG is not available on the Free plan, and is locked during the trial period.
  Upgrade to Team or above to enable it.
</Warning>

### Enable or disable PAYG

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    Navigate to **Admin Settings → Billing** and open **Pay-As-You-Go**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle PAYG">
    Turn PAYG on (or off).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The change takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Set your spending cap

The monthly cap limits how much PAYG can charge in a single billing cycle. Once the cap is reached, PAYG stops until the next cycle.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the monthly cap">
    In **Admin Settings → Billing → Pay-As-You-Go**, set your **monthly cap**. The minimum is **\$100/month**; the maximum depends on your plan tier, and your plan's default cap is pre-filled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Usage above the cap is blocked until the next billing cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Caps are per billing cycle and reset each cycle. Lowering the cap mid-cycle
  does not refund usage already incurred.
</Info>
