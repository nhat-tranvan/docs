Pulse analytics tells you whether your alert pipeline is doing useful work. In one tab you see how many raw events came in, how many Pulse held back as noise, how many reached an incident or were resolved, and which sources send the most volume.

A few terms used on this page:

- **Signal**: one raw event Pulse received, such as a single Datadog alert or CloudWatch alarm.
- **Cluster**: a group of related Signals that Pulse believes describe the same problem.
- **Routed**: a Cluster that Pulse linked to an incident, so it went on to investigation.

See [Clusters](/guide/pulse/clusters) for the full lifecycle.

## Open Pulse analysis

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Insights">
    In the sidebar, open **Resolve → Insights**. You need a workspace role that can view incidents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the Pulse tab">
    Click the **Pulse analysis** tab. The other tabs (**Overview**, **Accuracy & trust**, **Runbook analytics**) are covered in [Resolve insights](/guide/incident/insights).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the time window">
    Use the window selector at the top right. Options run from **Last 1 hour** to **Last 30 days**. The default is **Last 7 days**.

    **Success state:** four metric cards appear, followed by the **Top noisy sources** table. If no Signals arrived in the window, the table reads "No signals ingested in this period".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What each metric means

| Card | What it measures | How it's calculated |
|---|---|---|
| **Correlation yield** | How much of your alert volume led somewhere | Signals whose Cluster was linked to an incident or resolved, divided by all Signals. It counts Signals, not Clusters, and covers every source (not just the top 10). |
| **Cluster MTTR** | How long problems stay open | Median time from a Cluster's first Signal to its resolution, across the Clusters resolved in the window. The card shows how many resolved Clusters it's based on. |
| **Signals ingested** | Raw volume | Total raw events Pulse received in the window. |
| **Noise suppressed** | How much Pulse filtered out | Suppressed Signals divided by all Signals seen (visible plus suppressed). The subtitle shows visible Signals collapsing into Clusters, for example `1,240 signals → 38 clusters`. |

<Note>
**Noise suppressed** is not the same number as the noise reduction figure on the **Overview** tab. Overview measures raw events collapsing into incidents. Pulse analysis measures suppressed Signals over all Signals seen. Compare each number only with itself over time.
</Note>

## Top noisy sources

The table ranks the 10 sources that sent the most Signals in the window.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Source** | The raw source name, such as `webhook.datadog` or a CloudTrail stream with account and region. Hover a long name to see all of it. |
| **Signals** | Signals from that source in the window |
| **Suppressed** | Share of that source's Signals Pulse suppressed |
| **Clusters routed** | Share of that source's Signals whose Cluster was linked to an incident or resolved |

One integration can show up as several rows. A single AWS connection, for example, can produce a separate stream per account and region.

## Read the results

Use the numbers to decide where to spend tuning time.

| What you see | What it usually means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A source with high **Signals** and near 0% **Clusters routed** | It sends a lot and almost none of it matters | Open its Signals in **Resolve → Pulse**. Tighten the alert at the source, or review its [suppression](/guide/pulse/clusters) |
| Low **Correlation yield** overall | Most Signals never reach an incident or a resolution | Check whether the top sources are noise, before you change correlation settings |
| **Cluster MTTR** rising week over week | Problems are staying open longer | Check ownership and routing in [On-call](/guide/incident/oncall), and look for Clusters nobody picked up |
| A sudden jump in **Noise suppressed** for one source | A new event pattern, or a suppression rule that is now too broad | Inspect a sample of the suppressed Signals before you change anything |

Treat these as pointers, not verdicts. Look at the actual Clusters and Signals before you change a source or a suppression rule.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Every card shows zero | No Signals arrived in the window. Widen the window, or confirm a source is sending in [Connect Pulse sources](/guide/pulse/setup). |
| **Cluster MTTR** is empty | No Clusters were resolved in the window. It fills in once Clusters resolve. |
| "Failed to Load Pulse Analysis" | Click **Retry**. If it keeps failing, reload the page. |
| You can't see **Insights** | Your role doesn't include incident access. Ask a workspace Admin. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles). |
