---
title: Report a Security Incident
description: Report a suspected security incident in CloudThinker SaaS and learn how CloudThinker responds.
---

Report suspected vulnerabilities, unauthorized access, or exposed data in CloudThinker SaaS to [security@cloudthinker.io](mailto:security@cloudthinker.io). If you cannot reach the security address, use the [CloudThinker contact form](https://cloudthinker.io/contact) and indicate that your request concerns a security incident.

## What to include

- Your organization and workspace names, if applicable.
- What happened, when it happened, and your time zone.
- The affected users, features, or resources.
- Steps to reproduce the issue and any relevant screenshots or logs.

Remove credentials and customer data from screenshots and logs before sending them. Use a private channel for the report.

## What happens next

CloudThinker records and investigates the report, assesses who is affected, and takes steps to contain and resolve the issue. We may ask you for more information through a private channel.

When we identify affected customers, we notify them directly. The notice explains what happened, what we know about the impact, what CloudThinker has done, and any action you should take.
