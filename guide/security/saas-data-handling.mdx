This page covers the CloudThinker-hosted service at `app.cloudthinker.io`, including subscriptions purchased through AWS Marketplace.

## What we collect and why

| Data | How CloudThinker uses it |
|---|---|
| Account and organization details | Sign you in, manage memberships and roles, and send account and service email. |
| Connection credentials | Access the systems you connect, within the permissions you grant. |
| Chats, files, and agent results | Run your requests and make conversations, findings, and artifacts available in your workspace. |
| Data read from connected systems | Investigate your requests using the resources your connections can access. |
| Subscription and usage records | Apply your plan, track credits, and report billable usage to AWS Marketplace when it handles your subscription. |
| Security and audit events | Record SSO sign-ins, permission and security setting changes, and other activity for investigation and review. Sign-ins with an email code, Google, or GitHub aren't recorded as audit events. |
| Product and website analytics | Measure product use, protect sign-in, and measure sign-up campaigns. |

## Where data is stored and processed

CloudThinker hosts the app and its primary database in AWS in Singapore. Uploaded files and artifacts are stored in Amazon S3. CloudThinker keeps agent run traces in a separate, CloudThinker-managed tracing service.

CloudThinker uses service providers for hosting, AI inference, search, email, billing, and analytics. An AI provider receives prompts and relevant agent context when it processes a request. If you bring your own model credentials, the provider processes those requests under your agreement with that provider. See [Bring Your Own Key](/guide/byok) for how fallbacks work.

When your agent uses a connection, it can send data to the service you connected to complete the request. AWS Marketplace processes payment and receives the billing information needed for your Marketplace subscription.

## How data is protected

CloudThinker encrypts customer data in transit and at rest. The application checks organization and workspace permissions before granting access to data. See [Roles and Permissions](/guide/security/roles) and [Data Protection](/guide/security/data-protection).

## Backups and retention

CloudThinker keeps encrypted automated database backups for seven days. Previous versions of files in Amazon S3 are retained for 90 days. CloudThinker SaaS retains audit events for 365 days by default. The [Audit Logs](/guide/security/audit-logs) page describes which events you can view and export.

Retention varies by data type and by purpose. For retention details about a specific type of data, [contact CloudThinker](https://cloudthinker.io/contact).

## Request data deletion

Workspace administrators can remove content such as conversations and files through the app. To request deletion of account or organization data, email [privacy@cloudthinker.io](mailto:privacy@cloudthinker.io). Include your organization name and the records you want deleted. CloudThinker handles deletion requests and contract-end retention under the terms that apply to your subscription. Backup copies expire according to their retention schedule.
