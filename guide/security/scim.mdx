SCIM (System for Cross-domain Identity Management) lets your identity provider (IdP), such as Okta or Microsoft Entra ID, manage CloudThinker accounts for you. Assign someone to the CloudThinker app in your IdP and they join your organization; unassign them and their access ends, with no manual invites or removals.

## Prerequisites

- An organization on the **Scale**, **Scale+** or **Enterprise** plan. See [Pricing](/guide/billing/pricing).
- An active [SSO connection](/guide/security/sso) with a verified domain. The provisioning settings only appear once SSO is active.
- The organization **Owner** role in CloudThinker. Admins can't manage SSO or SCIM. See [Roles and permissions](/guide/security/roles).
- Admin access to the provisioning settings of your CloudThinker app in your IdP.

## What SCIM changes in CloudThinker

| In your IdP | In CloudThinker |
|---|---|
| Assign a person to the CloudThinker app | They're added to your organization with the **Auto-assigned organization role** and get the **Auto-assigned workspaces** |
| Change their name or email | Their CloudThinker profile updates |
| Deactivate them (set inactive) | Their membership is suspended and they lose access to every workspace |
| Reactivate them | Their membership comes back, with the auto-assigned workspaces |
| Unassign or delete them | They're removed from the organization |
| Push a group | The group appears under **Group mappings**, where you pick workspaces for its members |

While SCIM is on, your IdP is the source of truth for who's in the organization:

- **Member invitations are turned off.** Inviting from CloudThinker returns "Member invitations are disabled because this organization uses SCIM provisioning."
- **You can't remove a SCIM-provisioned member in CloudThinker.** The error reads "This member was provisioned via SCIM." Remove them in your IdP instead.
- **Just-in-time provisioning is turned off.** SCIM and just-in-time (accounts created on first SSO sign-in) can't run together. Turning on SCIM switches just-in-time off for you.

You can still change a member's role or workspace access in CloudThinker. See [Organization](/guide/organization).

## Turn on SCIM in CloudThinker

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the provisioning settings">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Identity and access** and find the **Provisioning & directory sync** card under your SSO connection.

    If the card is missing, your SSO connection isn't active yet. Finish [SSO setup](/guide/security/sso) first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose SCIM">
    Select **SCIM** (Sync users and groups from your identity provider). The other options are **Manual** (you invite people yourself) and **Just-in-time** (accounts are created on first SSO sign-in).

    **Success state:** the **SCIM enabled** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the two values">
    The dialog shows:

    - **Bearer token**: the secret your IdP sends to prove its requests are allowed.
    - **SCIM base URL**: the endpoint your IdP calls. It looks like `https://<your-cloudthinker-host>/api/v1/scim/<organization-id>/v2`.

    Copy both, then click **Done**.

    **Success state:** the card shows **Directory sync (SCIM)** with "SCIM directory sync is active."
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
The bearer token is shown only once. If you close the dialog without copying it, click **Rotate token** to get a new one.
</Warning>

## Set defaults for new members

Below the provisioning options, two settings decide what a newly provisioned person gets. Set them before your IdP sends its first users.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Auto-assigned organization role** | The organization role new members get: **Viewer**, **Developer** or **Admin** | **Viewer** |
| **Auto-assigned workspaces** | The workspaces new members join | Your default workspace |

Owner can't be assigned this way. For what each role can do, see [Roles and permissions](/guide/security/roles).

## Configure your identity provider

Open the provisioning settings of your CloudThinker app in your IdP (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, OneLogin and others use the same fields) and enter:

| IdP field | Value |
|---|---|
| SCIM connector base URL / Tenant URL | The **SCIM base URL** from CloudThinker |
| Authentication | HTTP header, bearer token |
| Bearer token / Secret token | The **Bearer token** from CloudThinker |
| Unique identifier for users | `userName`, set to the user's email address |

Then turn on the operations you want: create users, update user attributes, deactivate users, and push groups. Run your IdP's **Test connection** if it has one.

**Success state:** assign one test user to the app. Within your IdP's sync interval they appear in **Admin Settings → Organization → Members**, and **View sync logs** shows a `CREATE` entry with status `SUCCESS`.

What CloudThinker's SCIM API supports, so you can match your IdP's options:

| Capability | Supported |
|---|---|
| Users and Groups resources (create, read, replace, patch, delete) | Yes |
| Filters with `eq` on `userName`, `emails.value`, `externalId`, `active` (users) and `displayName` (groups) | Yes, up to 100 results |
| Bulk operations | No |
| Sorting, ETags, password changes | No |

<Note>
If someone already has a CloudThinker account from another organization, CloudThinker adds that existing account to yours only when its email domain is one of your verified SSO domains. Otherwise your IdP gets "User email domain not verified for this organization."
</Note>

## Map groups to workspaces

Groups let one IdP change put a whole team into the right workspaces.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Push groups from your IdP">
    In your IdP, assign the groups to the CloudThinker app and push them.

    **Success state:** each group shows up under **Group mappings** on **Admin Settings → Identity and access**, with its **Group name** and **Members** count. Until then the section reads "No groups synced yet."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick workspaces for each group">
    In the group's row, open **Auto-assigned workspaces** and select the workspaces its members should join.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the group role">
    Set **Auto-assigned organization role** for the group, or leave **Inherit from connection** to use the default role you set above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the row">
    Click **Save** in the **Actions** column.

    **Success state:** current members of the group are added to the selected workspaces, and future members are added when your IdP syncs them into the group.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Group mappings only add workspace access. Removing someone from a group, or removing a workspace from a mapping, doesn't take them out of that workspace. Remove them in [workspace members](/guide/workspace-users), or unassign them from the app in your IdP to remove them everywhere.
</Warning>

After the first sync, check new members' roles in **Admin Settings → Organization → Members** and adjust any that should differ.

## What happens when someone leaves

| IdP action | Membership in CloudThinker | Workspaces | Their automations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deactivate (set inactive) | Suspended | Removed from all | Turned off |
| Reactivate | Active again, if a seat is free | Auto-assigned workspaces only; re-add the rest | Stay off until someone turns them on |
| Unassign or delete | Removed | Removed from all | Turned off |

[Automations](/guide/automation/automations) never change owner on their own. An Admin can take ownership of one whose owner lost access, and it stays off until they turn it on.

<Note>
If your plan uses per-seat billing and every seat is taken, a newly provisioned or reactivated person is added as inactive and can't use CloudThinker yet. Add seats from **Admin Settings → Organization**, then check their status in **Members**.
</Note>

## Rotate or turn off the token

**Rotate the token** when it may have leaked or your policy requires it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Rotate">
    In **Directory sync (SCIM)**, click **Rotate token**, then **Rotate** in the confirmation.

    **Success state:** a dialog shows the new **Bearer token** and the **SCIM base URL**. The old token keeps working for 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your IdP">
    Paste the new token into your IdP's provisioning settings and test the connection. Do it within 24 hours, or syncs start failing with authentication errors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Turn off SCIM** by choosing **Manual** or **Just-in-time** in **Provisioning & directory sync**, then **Disable SCIM** in the confirmation. This revokes the token at once and stops directory sync. Existing members keep their accounts, and you manage them in CloudThinker from then on. Turn off provisioning in your IdP too, or it keeps sending requests that fail.

Token changes (generate, rotate, revoke) and every user or group that SCIM provisions, updates or removes are recorded in [audit logs](/guide/security/audit-logs).

## Check sync activity

Click **View sync logs** in **Directory sync (SCIM)** to open **SCIM sync logs**.

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Timestamp** | When CloudThinker processed the request |
| **Action** | `CREATE`, `UPDATE`, `DEACTIVATE` or `DELETE` |
| **Resource** | `User` or `Group`, with its ID |
| **Status** | `SUCCESS`, `SKIPPED` or `FAILED` |

CloudThinker also checks once a day for SCIM-provisioned people with no sync activity for 7 days, and adds a `SKIPPED` entry for each one so you can confirm they still belong.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Provisioning & directory sync card isn't there">
    The card only shows when your SSO connection is active. Finish [SSO setup](/guide/security/sso), including domain verification. You also need the Owner role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Groups don't appear under Group mappings">
    CloudThinker doesn't pull groups; your IdP has to push them. Assign the groups to the CloudThinker app in your IdP, run a push, then look for `Group` entries in **View sync logs**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A user wasn't provisioned">
    Confirm the user is assigned to the CloudThinker app in your IdP, then look for a `FAILED` entry in the sync logs. Common causes: the token was rotated more than 24 hours ago or revoked, the person's existing account uses an unverified email domain, or the email belongs to a CloudThinker system account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone left but can still sign in">
    Unassign or deactivate them on the CloudThinker app itself. Removing them from a group only changes group membership. Some IdPs sync on a schedule (Microsoft Entra ID runs about every 40 minutes), so wait for the next cycle and check the sync logs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="All syncs fail with authentication errors">
    The token in your IdP is no longer valid: another Owner rotated it more than 24 hours ago, or SCIM was turned off. Click **Rotate token** and paste the new value into your IdP. If SCIM was turned off, select **SCIM** again to get a new token.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cannot enable JIT provisioning while SCIM is active">
    Just-in-time and SCIM can't run together. Choose **Just-in-time** in **Provisioning & directory sync** and confirm **Disable SCIM**, which revokes the token and stops directory sync.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't invite or remove a member">
    That's expected while SCIM is on. Assign or unassign people in your IdP instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
