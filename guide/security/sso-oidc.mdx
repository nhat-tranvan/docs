OpenID Connect (OIDC) is the sign-in layer on top of OAuth 2.0. You register CloudThinker as an OAuth client in your identity provider (IdP), then give CloudThinker three values: **Issuer URL**, **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.

## Prerequisites

- A verified email domain, the organization **Owner** role, and an eligible plan. See [Set up single sign-on](/guide/security/sso).
- Admin access to your identity provider, with permission to create OAuth or OIDC applications.

## What you exchange

| Direction | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CloudThinker → your IdP | Redirect URI: `https://<your-cloudthinker-api-host>/api/v1/sso/oidc/callback` | Register it exactly, as a **Web** redirect URI |
| Your IdP → CloudThinker | **Issuer URL** | The issuer base, not the `.well-known` address. CloudThinker discovers the endpoints from `<issuer>/.well-known/openid-configuration`. |
| Your IdP → CloudThinker | **Client ID** | Required |
| Your IdP → CloudThinker | **Client Secret** | Required for confidential clients (the usual web app setup). Leave empty only for public clients using PKCE. |

CloudThinker requests the `openid`, `email` and `profile` scopes. It reads the user's email from the `email` claim (falling back to `preferred_username`) and the name from `given_name` and `family_name`. It doesn't read groups.

| Client setting | What CloudThinker uses |
|---|---|
| Grant type | Authorization Code |
| PKCE | Always sent, with `S256`, for confidential and public clients |
| Token endpoint authentication | `client_secret_post` (the secret goes in the request body). Leave it out for a public client. |

<Tip>
  The OIDC wizard doesn't display the redirect URI. To find your host, start **Setup SSO**, pick **SAML 2.0**, and click **Next**. The **ACS URL** on the **SP Metadata** step uses the same host: replace `/api/v1/sso/saml/acs` with `/api/v1/sso/oidc/callback`. Then click **Back** and choose OIDC.
</Tip>

## Configure your provider

Go to **Admin Settings → Identity and access → Setup SSO**, choose **OpenID Connect (OIDC)**, and click **Next**. The **IdP Config** step asks for **Display Name** (any label, for example `Okta OIDC`), **Issuer URL**, **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Okta">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create an OIDC app">
        In the Okta Admin Console, go to **Applications → Applications → Create App Integration**. Choose **OIDC - OpenID Connect** and **Web Application**. Under **Grant type**, keep **Authorization Code**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add the redirect URI">
        Under **Sign-in redirect URIs**, paste CloudThinker's redirect URI. Assign the people or groups who need access, then save.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Finish in CloudThinker">
        From the app's **General** tab, copy the **Client ID** and **Client secret**. On the **IdP Config** step, enter:

        - **Issuer URL**: `https://your-org.okta.com`. If you use a custom authorization server, enter its issuer instead, for example `https://your-org.okta.com/oauth2/default`.
        - **Client ID** and **Client Secret**: from the Okta app

        Click **Create Connection**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Microsoft Entra ID">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Register an application">
        In the [Azure portal](https://portal.azure.com), go to **Microsoft Entra ID → App registrations → New registration**. Name it **CloudThinker**. Under **Redirect URI**, select **Web** and paste CloudThinker's redirect URI. Click **Register**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create a client secret">
        Go to **Certificates & secrets → New client secret**. Copy the secret **Value** right away; Entra shows it only once. Note its expiry date.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add the email claim">
        Go to **Token configuration → Add optional claim → ID**, select **email**, and click **Add**. On **API permissions**, confirm Microsoft Graph `openid`, `email` and `profile` are listed.

        Without the `email` claim, CloudThinker falls back to `preferred_username`, which is the user's UPN. That works only when UPNs are on a verified domain.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Finish in CloudThinker">
        On the **IdP Config** step, enter:

        - **Issuer URL**: `https://login.microsoftonline.com/<tenant-id>/v2.0`, using the **Directory (tenant) ID** from the app's overview
        - **Client ID**: the **Application (client) ID** from the overview
        - **Client Secret**: the secret value you copied

        Click **Create Connection**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Google">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Configure the OAuth consent screen">
        In the [Google Cloud console](https://console.cloud.google.com), go to **APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen**. Choose **Internal** as the user type so only your Workspace users can sign in, and add the `openid`, `email` and `profile` scopes.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create an OAuth client">
        In the [Google Cloud console](https://console.cloud.google.com), go to **APIs & Services → Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID**. Choose **Web application** as the application type.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add the redirect URI">
        Under **Authorized redirect URIs**, add CloudThinker's redirect URI and click **Create**. Copy the **Client ID** and **Client secret**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Finish in CloudThinker">
        On the **IdP Config** step, enter:

        - **Issuer URL**: `https://accounts.google.com`
        - **Client ID** and **Client Secret**: from the OAuth client

        Click **Create Connection**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Other OIDC provider">
    Any provider works if it publishes a discovery document at `<issuer>/.well-known/openid-configuration`.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Register CloudThinker as a web client">
        Create an OAuth 2.0 / OIDC web application in your provider. Add CloudThinker's redirect URI and allow the `openid`, `email` and `profile` scopes.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Fill in IdP Config">
        | Field | What to enter |
        |---|---|
        | **Display Name** | Any label, for example `Okta OIDC` |
        | **Issuer URL** | Your provider's issuer, exactly as it appears in the `issuer` field of its discovery document |
        | **Client ID** | The client ID your provider issued |
        | **Client Secret** | The client secret, or empty for a public PKCE client |

        Click **Create Connection**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Success state:** the dialog shows **SSO Connection Created** and the connection is **Active**. Next, run the connection test and a test sign-in from [Set up single sign-on](/guide/security/sso#create-the-connection).

## What the connection test checks

On **Manage → Overview**, **Run connection test** checks your configuration without a full sign-in:

| Check | Passes when |
|---|---|
| **Discovery Reachable** | CloudThinker can fetch `<issuer>/.well-known/openid-configuration` |
| **Issuer Match** | The `issuer` in that document equals your **Issuer URL** |
| **Required Endpoints Present** | The document lists the authorization, token and signing-key endpoints |
| **Jwks Reachable** | CloudThinker can fetch your provider's signing keys |

The test doesn't verify the client secret or redirect URI. Do a real sign-in to confirm those.

## What happens on first sign-in

OIDC follows the same rules as SAML:

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| Provisioning is **Just-in-time** | CloudThinker creates the account on the person's first SSO sign-in, with the **Auto-assigned organization role** (default **Viewer**) and **Auto-assigned workspaces** you set. |
| The person has a pending invitation | The invitation's role and workspaces apply instead. If the organization has no free seat, sign-in fails with "This organization has reached its seat limit." |
| The person already has a CloudThinker account with the same email | CloudThinker links that account to your IdP. No duplicate is created. |
| Provisioning is **Manual** and the person has no account or invitation | Sign-in is refused with "No account found. Contact your administrator for access." Invite them first. |
| You want IdP groups to decide access | OIDC sign-in doesn't read groups. Use [SCIM](/guide/security/scim#map-groups-to-workspaces) to map groups to workspaces. |

## Rotate the client secret

CloudThinker stores one client secret. Most IdPs, including Entra ID and Okta, let two secrets be valid at once, so you can rotate with no downtime:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new secret in your IdP">
    Add a second client secret to the CloudThinker app. Leave the old one in place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it in CloudThinker">
    Open **Manage → Configuration** on the **Single sign-on** card, paste the new value into **Client Secret**, and click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test, then remove the old secret">
    Sign in through SSO in a private window. Once it works, delete the old secret in your IdP.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your IdP allows only one secret, sign-ins fail between replacing it there and saving it in CloudThinker. The Owner can still sign in with an email code. See [If your IdP is unavailable](/guide/security/sso#if-your-idp-is-unavailable).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="'Redirect URI mismatch' from your provider">
    The redirect URI in your provider must match `https://<your-cloudthinker-api-host>/api/v1/sso/oidc/callback` exactly, including `https://` and the full path. Use the tip above to confirm the host.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Issuer Match fails">
    Copy the `issuer` value straight from your provider's discovery document. Common mistakes: pasting the full `.well-known/openid-configuration` address, or leaving out `/v2.0` for Entra ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Discovery Reachable fails">
    CloudThinker must reach your issuer over the public internet. An issuer on a private network or behind a VPN can't be discovered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'Invalid client' or sign-in fails right after the IdP">
    Check the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. Expired secrets are a common cause on Entra ID. Follow [Rotate the client secret](#rotate-the-client-secret).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sign-in fails because no email is returned">
    Your provider isn't returning an `email` or `preferred_username` claim. Allow the `email` scope for the app and make sure the user has an email address in the directory.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'SSO authentication failed. Please try again or contact your administrator.'">
    CloudThinker rejected the ID token or the token exchange. Check that the **Issuer URL** matches the discovery document, the client secret is current, and your IdP accepts `client_secret_post` and PKCE for this client.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
