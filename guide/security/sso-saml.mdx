SAML 2.0 is the XML-based single sign-on standard most enterprise identity providers (IdPs) support. You exchange two sets of values: CloudThinker's service provider (SP) details go into your IdP, and your IdP's details go back into CloudThinker.

## Prerequisites

- A verified email domain, the organization **Owner** role, and an eligible plan. See [Set up single sign-on](/guide/security/sso).
- Admin access to your identity provider.

## What you exchange

The wizard at **Admin Settings → Identity and access → Setup SSO → SAML 2.0** has three steps: **Protocol**, **SP Metadata** and **IdP Config**.

| Direction | Values | Where you find them |
|---|---|---|
| CloudThinker → your IdP | **ACS URL (Assertion Consumer Service)**, ending in `/api/v1/sso/saml/acs`, and **SP Entity ID**, ending in `/api/v1/sso/saml/metadata` | The **SP Metadata** step, each with a copy button |
| Your IdP → CloudThinker | **IdP Entity ID**, **SSO URL**, **IdP Certificate** | Your IdP's SAML app, or its metadata URL |
| Attributes your IdP sends | Email (required), first name, last name | Your IdP's attribute mapping |

<Tip>
  On the **IdP Config** step, paste your IdP's metadata URL into the field at the top and click **Import**. CloudThinker fills **IdP Entity ID**, **SSO URL** and **IdP Certificate**, plus **SLO URL** and **Name ID Format** when the metadata includes them.
</Tip>

CloudThinker reads the user's email from an `email`, `mail`, `emailAddress` or Microsoft `emailaddress` claim, and falls back to the NameID when it's an email. First and last name come from `firstName`/`givenName` and `lastName`/`surname`.

## Configure your provider

Start the wizard, choose **SAML 2.0**, click **Next**, and keep the **SP Metadata** step open while you work in your IdP. Every provider also needs a **Display Name** on the **IdP Config** step; any label works, for example `Okta SSO`.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Okta">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create a SAML app integration">
        In the Okta Admin Console, go to **Applications → Applications → Create App Integration → SAML 2.0**. Name it **CloudThinker**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Configure SAML settings">
        | Okta field | Value |
        |---|---|
        | **Single sign-on URL** | CloudThinker's **ACS URL** |
        | **Audience URI (SP Entity ID)** | CloudThinker's **SP Entity ID** |
        | **Name ID format** | `EmailAddress` |
        | **Application username** | `Email` |
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add attribute statements">
        | Name | Value |
        |---|---|
        | `email` | `user.email` |
        | `firstName` | `user.firstName` |
        | `lastName` | `user.lastName` |
      </Step>
      <Step title="Copy the IdP metadata">
        Save the app. On its **Sign On** tab, copy the metadata URL, or copy the Issuer, Sign-On URL and X.509 certificate.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Assign people and finish in CloudThinker">
        Assign users or groups on the **Assignments** tab. In CloudThinker, click **Next** to reach **IdP Config**, then **Import** the metadata URL, or enter the Issuer as **IdP Entity ID**, the Sign-On URL as **SSO URL**, and the certificate as **IdP Certificate**. Click **Create Connection**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Microsoft Entra ID">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create an enterprise application">
        In the [Azure portal](https://portal.azure.com), go to **Microsoft Entra ID → Enterprise applications → New application → Create your own application**. Name it **CloudThinker**, select **Integrate any other application you don't find in the gallery**, and click **Create**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set up single sign-on">
        Open the app, go to **Single sign-on → SAML**, and edit **Basic SAML Configuration**:

        | Entra field | Value |
        |---|---|
        | **Identifier (Entity ID)** | CloudThinker's **SP Entity ID** |
        | **Reply URL (Assertion Consumer Service URL)** | CloudThinker's **ACS URL** |

        Save.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check attributes and claims">
        Entra's default claims (`emailaddress`, `givenname`, `surname`) work as-is. Confirm `emailaddress` maps to `user.mail` and that your users have the mail attribute set.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Copy the federation metadata">
        Under **SAML Certificates**, copy the **App Federation Metadata Url**. Alternatively, download **Certificate (Base64)** and copy the **Login URL** and **Microsoft Entra Identifier**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Assign users and finish in CloudThinker">
        Assign users or groups under **Users and groups**. In CloudThinker's **IdP Config** step, **Import** the metadata URL, or enter the Entra Identifier as **IdP Entity ID**, the Login URL as **SSO URL**, and the Base64 certificate as **IdP Certificate**. Click **Create Connection**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Google Workspace">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create a custom SAML app">
        In the [Google Admin console](https://admin.google.com), go to **Apps → Web and mobile apps → Add app → Add custom SAML app**. Name it **CloudThinker** and click **Continue**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Copy Google's IdP details">
        On **Google Identity Provider details**, copy the **SSO URL**, **Entity ID** and **Certificate**, or download the metadata. Click **Continue**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter CloudThinker's SP details">
        | Google field | Value |
        |---|---|
        | **ACS URL** | CloudThinker's **ACS URL** |
        | **Entity ID** | CloudThinker's **SP Entity ID** |
        | **Name ID format** | `EMAIL` |
        | **Name ID** | **Basic Information > Primary email** |

        Click **Continue**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Map attributes">
        | Google Directory attribute | App attribute |
        |---|---|
        | Primary email | `email` |
        | First name | `firstName` |
        | Last name | `lastName` |

        Click **Finish**. Then turn **User access** to **ON for everyone**, or for the organizational units that need CloudThinker.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Finish in CloudThinker">
        On the **IdP Config** step, enter Google's **Entity ID** as **IdP Entity ID**, its **SSO URL** as **SSO URL**, and its certificate as **IdP Certificate**. Click **Create Connection**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="AWS IAM Identity Center">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Add a custom SAML application">
        In the AWS console, go to **IAM Identity Center → Applications → Add application → I have an application I want to set up → SAML 2.0**. Name it **CloudThinker**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Copy the IAM Identity Center metadata">
        Under **IAM Identity Center metadata**, copy the SAML metadata file URL, or the sign-in URL, issuer URL and certificate.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the application metadata">
        | IAM Identity Center field | Value |
        |---|---|
        | **Application ACS URL** | CloudThinker's **ACS URL** |
        | **Application SAML audience** | CloudThinker's **SP Entity ID** |

        Submit the application.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Map attributes">
        Open **Actions → Edit attribute mappings** and add:

        | Attribute in the application | Maps to | Format |
        |---|---|---|
        | `Subject` | `${user:email}` | `emailAddress` |
        | `email` | `${user:email}` | `unspecified` |
        | `firstName` | `${user:givenName}` | `unspecified` |
        | `lastName` | `${user:familyName}` | `unspecified` |
      </Step>
      <Step title="Assign users and finish in CloudThinker">
        Assign users or groups to the app. In CloudThinker, **Import** the metadata URL, or enter the issuer URL as **IdP Entity ID**, the sign-in URL as **SSO URL**, and the certificate as **IdP Certificate**. Click **Create Connection**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="OneLogin">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Add a SAML custom connector">
        In the OneLogin admin portal, go to **Applications → Applications → Add App**, search for **SAML Custom Connector (Advanced)**, name it **CloudThinker**, and save.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Configure the connector">
        On the **Configuration** tab:

        | OneLogin field | Value |
        |---|---|
        | **Audience (EntityID)** | CloudThinker's **SP Entity ID** |
        | **Recipient** | CloudThinker's **ACS URL** |
        | **ACS (Consumer) URL** | CloudThinker's **ACS URL** |
        | **ACS (Consumer) URL Validator** | The ACS URL as a regex, for example `^https://your-host/api/v1/sso/saml/acs$` |
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add parameters">
        On the **Parameters** tab, add `email` (Email), `firstName` (First Name) and `lastName` (Last Name), each with **Include in SAML assertion** checked.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Copy the IdP details">
        On the **SSO** tab, copy the **Issuer URL**, the **SAML 2.0 Endpoint (HTTP)** and the X.509 certificate.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Assign users and finish in CloudThinker">
        Add users on the **Users** tab. In CloudThinker, enter the Issuer URL as **IdP Entity ID**, the SAML 2.0 Endpoint as **SSO URL**, and the certificate as **IdP Certificate**. Click **Create Connection**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Other SAML 2.0 provider">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create a SAML app in your provider">
        Put CloudThinker's **ACS URL** in your provider's "Reply URL", "ACS URL" or "Recipient" field, and the **SP Entity ID** in its "Audience" or "Entity ID" field. Send the user's email as the NameID and as an `email` attribute, plus `firstName` and `lastName`. Sign the assertion.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Fill in IdP Config">
        | Field | What to enter |
        |---|---|
        | **Display Name** | Any label, for example `Okta SSO` |
        | **IdP Entity ID** | Your provider's entity ID, sometimes called "Issuer" |
        | **SSO URL** | Your provider's single sign-on endpoint |
        | **IdP Certificate** | The X.509 signing certificate, including the `-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----` and `-----END CERTIFICATE-----` lines |
        | **SLO URL (Optional)** | Your provider's single logout endpoint |
        | **Name ID Format** | **Email Address** (default). Also offered: **Persistent**, **Transient**, **Unspecified** |

        Click **Create Connection**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Success state:** the dialog shows **SSO Connection Created** and the connection is **Active**. Next, run the connection test and a test sign-in from [Set up single sign-on](/guide/security/sso#create-the-connection).

## How CloudThinker handles SAML sign-ins

| Behavior | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Sign-in starts at CloudThinker | Users enter their email on the CloudThinker sign-in page and click **Continue with SSO**. Opening CloudThinker from your IdP's app dashboard (IdP-initiated sign-in) is not accepted. |
| Signed assertions required | Your IdP must sign the SAML assertion. Signing only the outer response isn't enough. |
| Clock tolerance of 120 seconds | Assertions are rejected if your IdP's clock and CloudThinker's differ by more than two minutes. |
| SP Metadata URL after creation | Some IdPs can import an SP metadata URL. It appears on **Manage → Configuration** once the connection exists. |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your IdP reports an invalid ACS or reply URL">
    The ACS URL in your IdP must match CloudThinker's **ACS URL** exactly, including `https://` and with no trailing slash. Copy it again from the **SP Metadata** step or from **Manage → Configuration**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="'Audience mismatch' or 'Entity ID mismatch'">
    The audience or entity ID in your IdP must match CloudThinker's **SP Entity ID** exactly. The comparison is case-sensitive.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Connection test fails on Certificate Valid">
    Paste the full certificate, including the `BEGIN` and `END` lines, with no extra characters. If your IdP rotated its certificate, paste the new one on **Manage → Configuration** and click **Save Changes**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sign-in works from CloudThinker but not from the IdP dashboard">
    That's expected. IdP-initiated sign-in is off. Ask users to start at the CloudThinker sign-in page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sign-in fails with a valid configuration">
    Check that the assertion is signed, the user is assigned to the CloudThinker app in your IdP, and your IdP server's clock is accurate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Names show as email addresses">
    Your IdP isn't sending first and last name. Add `firstName` and `lastName` (or `givenName` and `surname`) in your provider's attribute mapping.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
