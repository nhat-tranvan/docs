Connect Incident.io so CloudThinker can review active incidents, escalations, on-call schedules, and follow-up work. The connection uses browser-based authorization with your incident.io account.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Incident.io account or installation containing the data you want to investigate.
- A workspace role that can add connections. See [Roles](/guide/security/roles).
- Permission to authorize the account or obtain the credentials described below.

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare access">
    Choose the incident.io account and organization that contain the incidents you want CloudThinker to investigate. Confirm that account can view the relevant incidents, teams, on-call schedules, and escalation paths. You authorize the connection in the browser; there is no API-key field to fill in.

    See the [provider documentation](https://docs.incident.io/api-reference/introduction) for its current access requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the connection">
    Open **Connections**, find **Incident.io**, and choose **Connect**. Select **Connect**, sign in to Incident.io in the browser, and review the requested access. Complete authorization and return to CloudThinker. No manually entered API token is required.

    **Success state:** the connection shows **Connected**. This confirms the connection was established; run the verification prompt below to check the data access needed for an investigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the intended scope">
    Run the verification prompt below. Confirm the returned account, project, or resource names belong to the intended scope before asking for a deeper investigation. If a section fails, check its permissions rather than interpreting the failure as an empty result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connection details

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Authorization** | Sign in to incident.io in the browser window opened by Connect. | Your incident.io account |

## Required permissions

The authorized account determines incident, team, schedule, and escalation visibility. Use an account with access to the intended organization and teams. Permission to view incidents does not necessarily grant permission to update them or respond to escalations.

## Agent capabilities

| Capability | What CloudThinker can do |
|------------|-------------------------|
| **Incident triage** | Review active incidents, severity, status, and ownership. |
| **On-call review** | Inspect schedules, current shifts, and escalation paths. |
| **Follow-ups** | Review incident follow-up context and operational readiness. |

On-call gaps need confirmation against the relevant schedule. A bounded incident list does not represent every incident in the organization. Use the returned incident reference to identify the intended incident before requesting a change.

Requested incident creation or updates, escalation responses, and follow-up creation go through the connection’s change controls and [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview). Name the target incident or escalation and the intended change. Investigation and on-call review do not make changes.

### Verify the connection

```text
Summarize active incident.io incidents, current on-call coverage, and open escalations; report any missing permissions
```

Expect named resources or a clear empty result for each successful read. Keep any permission errors separate from the resources that were successfully inspected.

### Example prompts

```text
Show the most urgent active incidents and their current owners
Review the selected team’s on-call schedule and confirm any apparent coverage gap
```

Use a resource returned by the verification step and a short time range where relevant. Narrow requests are easier to verify than account-wide conclusions.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Authorization expired or was revoked">
Reconnect Incident.io and complete browser authorization with the intended account. Confirm that account still has access to the required organization or workspace.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Authorization succeeds but incidents are missing">
Check which account and organization you authorized. Confirm it can view the intended team’s incidents, then reconnect if the wrong account was selected.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Requests fail or results are incomplete">
Check the provider’s availability, the selected resource scope, and any returned permission or rate-limit message. Narrow the request before retrying. For a private API, confirm the network route from CloudThinker itself, not only from your workstation.
</Accordion>

## Security

<ConnectionSecurity />

- Keep access limited to the intended account and resources. Replace or revoke credentials when the connection is no longer needed.
- Review the exact resource and proposed change before approving a write. An uncertain result needs a fresh read before retrying.
