Connect Notion and CloudThinker agents can search the pages and databases you share with them, answer questions from your runbooks and handbooks, and update content when you ask. The connection signs in with a Notion **internal integration secret** (a token that belongs to one Notion workspace), and it only sees the pages you explicitly share with that integration.

## Prerequisites

- A Notion account with permission to create integrations in the workspace. In most workspaces that means a **Workspace Owner**.
- **Full access** or **Can edit** on the pages and databases agents should reach, so you can share them with the integration.
- Edit permission on connections in your CloudThinker workspace.

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an internal integration in Notion">
    Go to [notion.so/profile/integrations](https://www.notion.so/profile/integrations) and click **New integration**. Name it `cloudthinker`, pick the Notion workspace, keep the type **Internal**, and save.

    Copy the **Internal Integration Secret**. It starts with `ntn_` (older secrets start with `secret_`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the integration's capabilities">
    On the integration's **Configuration** tab, under **Capabilities**, keep only what your workflow needs. See [Required permissions](#required-permissions) for the read-only and read-write sets.

    If Resolve will publish postmortems, also enable **Insert content**. See [Use Notion for postmortems](#use-notion-for-postmortems).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share pages and databases with the integration">
    Open the integration's **Access** tab and select the pages and databases agents should reach. Child pages inherit access from their parent, so sharing a top-level page such as **Engineering** covers everything under it.

    You can also share one page from the page itself: open the **•••** menu, choose **Connections**, and add `cloudthinker`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the connection in CloudThinker">
    In the sidebar, click **More → Workspace settings → Connections**. On the **Builtin Connections** tab, search for **Notion** and click **Connect**.

    Paste the secret into **NOTION_TOKEN** and click **Connect**.

    **Success state:** the dialog closes and the Notion connection shows **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Notion checks the secret when an agent first calls it. Run the verify prompt below before you rely on the connection, so a mistyped or revoked secret shows up now instead of in the middle of an incident.
</Note>

## Connection details

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Alias** | A short name for this connection. Agents and [Rules](/guide/approvals/rules) use it to tell connections apart | `marketing-wiki` |
| **Description** | What this Notion workspace holds. Agents read it to pick the right connection, so make it specific | `Marketing team wiki and launch plans` |
| **NOTION_TOKEN** | Internal Integration Secret from your integration's **Configuration** tab in Notion | `ntn_<your-secret>` |

One secret covers one Notion workspace. To reach a second Notion workspace, create an integration there and add it as a separate connection.

## Required permissions

Two things in Notion set the ceiling on what agents can do: the integration's **Capabilities** and the pages on its **Access** tab. CloudThinker can never reach a page the integration isn't shared with.

| Goal | Capabilities to enable |
|------|------------------------|
| Search and summarize (read-only) | **Read content** |
| Read discussions on pages | Add **Read comments** |
| Edit pages, add blocks, create pages or database rows | Add **Update content** and **Insert content** |
| Leave comments for your team | Add **Insert comments** |

Leave user information at **No user information** unless a workflow needs to resolve people by name. Without it, agents see people in Person properties as IDs, not names.

<Tip>
Start read-only with **Read content**, and share only the teamspaces or top-level pages agents need, such as your runbooks and postmortems. Add write capabilities later if a workflow calls for them.
</Tip>

## Agent capabilities

CloudThinker runs Notion's official MCP server (Model Context Protocol, a standard way for agents to call a tool).

| Capability | What agents can do |
|------------|--------------------|
| **Search** | Find pages and databases by title and content across everything shared with the integration |
| **Read pages** | Read a page's properties and its full block content, including nested blocks |
| **Query databases** | Filter and sort database rows, such as postmortems from the last quarter or open action items |
| **Edit content** | Update page properties, append blocks, and create pages or database rows |
| **Comments** | Read comments on a page and add new ones |

Edits and comments are writes, so [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) decides whether each one runs, waits for a person, or is refused. Searches and reads run without asking.

### Use Notion for postmortems

[Resolve](/guide/incident/overview) can draft and publish a postmortem to Notion after an incident is resolved. In the sidebar, open **Resolve → Integrations**, select **Notion**, turn on **Publish postmortems** under **What Resolve can do**, and enter a **Notion parent page ID**, the page new postmortems go under. Share that parent page with the integration and enable **Insert content**. See [Resolve integrations](/guide/incident/integrations) for the full setup.

The page ID is the 32-character string at the end of the page's URL. Drop the title before it and any `?v=` or `?pvs=` part after it. For `https://www.notion.so/Postmortems-1a2b3c4d5e6f7a8b9c0d1e2f3a4b5c6d?pvs=4`, the page ID is `1a2b3c4d5e6f7a8b9c0d1e2f3a4b5c6d`.

### Verify the connection

```text
Search Notion for our on-call runbook and summarize the escalation steps
```

A working connection names the actual page it found and quotes its content. A reply that says no pages were found means the integration isn't shared with that page. An authorization error means the secret is wrong.

### Example prompts

```text
Summarize the postmortems in our Notion Incidents database from the last 90 days and list repeat root causes
Which Notion pages under Engineering → Runbooks haven't been edited in 6 months?
Add a step to the Database failover runbook in Notion: confirm replica lag is under 5 seconds before promoting
Compare the deploy steps in our Notion release checklist with the Production deploy runbook and list the differences
```

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Every request fails with 401 Unauthorized">
Notion rejected the secret. It may be mistyped, revoked, or from an integration that was deleted. Copy the current **Internal Integration Secret** from the integration's **Configuration** tab. In CloudThinker, open **Notion** in **More → Workspace settings → Connections**, click the pencil icon on the account's row, click **Replace…** in **Edit Instance**, paste the secret, and click **Save and test**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The agent can't find a page that exists">
The page isn't shared with the integration. Add it on the integration's **Access** tab, or from the page's **••• → Connections** menu. Sharing a parent page also shares its children.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Reads work but edits fail">
The integration lacks **Update content** or **Insert content**. Enable them under **Capabilities** in Notion. If you meant to keep the connection read-only, this failure is the expected result.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An edit is waiting and nothing happens">
The write is waiting for a person. Look for the approval card in the chat and click **Approve**. If the card says "Waiting for an approver," click **Notify approvers**. See [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) to change who approves and when.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Postmortems aren't published to Notion">
Check that the **Notion parent page ID** in Resolve matches a page shared with the integration, and that **Insert content** is enabled.
</Accordion>

## Security

<ConnectionSecurity />

- **The Access tab is your scope.** The integration sees only the pages shared with it. Review that list when teams reorganize content.
- **Keep the secret out of chat.** Paste it only into the connection dialog, never into a chat message or a repository.
- **Rotate by refreshing the secret.** Refreshing the secret in Notion stops the old one working at once, so replace it in CloudThinker straight away (pencil icon → **Replace…** → **Save and test**). See [Rotate a credential](/guide/connections/overview#rotate-a-credential).
- **Edits appear under the integration's name.** Notion shows changes made through the connection as the `cloudthinker` integration, so your team can tell them apart from human edits.
