A share link turns one [artifact](/guide/artifacts/overview) (a report, dashboard, or saved file) into a public web page. Anyone with the URL can open it without a CloudThinker account, and you can revoke it whenever you want.

## Prerequisites

- The **Developer** or **Admin** [workspace role](/guide/workspace-users). **Viewers** can't create or revoke share links.
- An artifact in the **Artifacts** gallery or in a conversation

## Create a share link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the artifact">
    Go to **Workspace Settings → Artifacts** and find the item, or open the artifact from the conversation that built it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Share">
    Use the **Share** icon on the artifact's card or at the top of the artifact page. The share dialog opens and confirms that anyone with the link can view the item without signing in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create share link">
    **Success state:** the dialog shows a **Share URL** with **Open link** and **Revoke** buttons. In the gallery, the card now shows a shared marker and its tooltip reads **Shared · Manage link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the URL and send it">
    Paste it into an email, a ticket, or a chat. Click **Open link** first if you want to see the page the way a recipient will.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What viewers see

| Viewers get | Viewers don't get |
|---|---|
| That one artifact, on its own page | Access to your workspace, conversations, or other artifacts |
| The artifact's current version | Older versions |
| The data as of when that version was published | Live data. A shared page never runs queries against your connections |
| Your logo and a "Shared from" footer with your company name, if [workspace branding](/guide/workspace-branding) is set up | A sign-in prompt |

Because the link always serves the current version, a new version CloudThinker publishes later replaces what viewers see at the same URL. Revoke the link first if a change shouldn't go out.

<Warning>
A share link is public until you revoke it, and anyone who receives the URL can forward it. Read the artifact once as an outsider would before you share: cost figures, account IDs, resource names, and architecture details are all visible.
</Warning>

## Review everything you've shared

In the gallery, open the filter and set **Sharing** to **Shared**. The list then shows only items with an active link, so you can audit them in one pass. Set it to **Private** to see what hasn't been shared.

## Revoke a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share dialog">
    Click the shared icon on the artifact's card (**Shared · Manage link**) or **Share** on the artifact page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Revoke">
    **Success state:** the dialog goes back to **Create share link**. The old URL stops working for everyone right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Sharing the same artifact again creates a new URL. The revoked one never comes back.

## Conversation replays

A public replay plays back a whole conversation, message by message. Creating replay links isn't available in customer workspaces yet, but you may receive one, for example from a CloudThinker demo.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Create share link fails">
    **Cause:** your workspace role is **Viewer**, which can open artifacts but not share them. **Fix:** ask a workspace Admin to share it, or to change your role in [Workspace users](/guide/workspace-users).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The recipient gets a not found page">
    **Cause:** the link was revoked, or the URL was cut off when it was pasted. **Check:** open the share dialog. If it shows **Create share link**, the item isn't shared. **Fix:** create a new link and send the full URL.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The shared page shows old numbers">
    **Cause:** shared pages show the snapshot from when the current version was published, never live data. **Fix:** ask CloudThinker to update the artifact. The new version appears at the same link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The footer doesn't show our company">
    **Cause:** workspace branding isn't set. **Fix:** add your logo and company name in [Workspace branding](/guide/workspace-branding).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
