Workspace members are the people from your [organization](/guide/organization) who can open a given [workspace](/guide/workspaces). Each one has a workspace role (Admin, Developer, or Viewer) that decides what they can do there.

## How workspace access works

A person must belong to the organization before you can add them to a workspace. Their organization role sets their default access:

| Organization role | Workspace access | Can you change it per workspace? |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | Admin in every workspace, automatically | No |
| **Admin** | Admin in every workspace, automatically | No |
| **Developer** | Only workspaces they're added to, as Developer by default | Yes |
| **Viewer** | Only workspaces they're added to, as Viewer by default | Yes |

A per-workspace role is called a role override. Use it to let a Viewer run work in one sandbox workspace, or to make a Developer the Admin of their team's workspace without giving them organization-wide control.

<Warning>
Workspace Admin can add, edit, and remove connections and credentials, and manage approvals. Grant it sparingly on workspaces connected to production.
</Warning>

## Prerequisites

- You're a workspace **Admin** (organization Owners and Admins always are).
- The person is already in the organization. To invite someone new, see [invite members](/guide/organization#invite-members).

## Add members from the workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Members tab">
    In the sidebar, click **More → Workspace settings** and select **Members**. The **Chat Settings** icon in the chat box opens the same dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start adding">
    Click **Add Member**. The **Add people to &lt;workspace&gt;** dialog lists organization Developers and Viewers who aren't in this workspace yet. Owners and Admins aren't listed because they already have access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick people and a role">
    Select one or more people. With one person selected, you can change **Joins as** to override their role. With two or more, everyone joins with their organization role. To give some of them a different role, add them, then change each **Workspace Role** in the members table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add">
    Click **Add** to confirm (the button shows how many people you're adding).

    **Success state:** each person appears in the members table with their **Organization Role** and **Workspace Role**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set access across workspaces at once

Use this when one person needs access to several workspaces.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the member">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Organization** and find them in **Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open their workspace access">
    Click **Edit Workspace Access** on their row. This option shows for Developers and Viewers only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose workspaces and roles">
    Check each workspace they should open. For each one, keep **Inherit from Org** or pick **Admin**, **Developer**, or **Viewer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.

    **Success state:** the workspaces appear in their account menu the next time they open it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Workspace roles

Viewers are strictly read-only: they can open conversations, findings, and dashboards, but can't send chat messages. Developers do day-to-day work. Admins also configure the workspace.

| Area | Viewer | Developer | Admin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chat | Read conversations | Chat, rename, and star conversations | Also delete conversations |
| Dashboards and artifacts | View | Create and edit | Create and edit |
| Optimize | View | Run scans, work findings, assign recommendations | Also edit, promote, and restore recommendations |
| Review | View | Resolve findings and merge | Also set up and remove repositories |
| Cyber | View | Create, edit, and run tests | Also delete |
| Resolve | View | View only* | Edit and delete incidents |
| Agents and automations | View | Create and edit custom agents. Can't create or edit automations | Also create, edit, and delete automations |
| Connections and credentials | View | View | Add, edit, and remove |
| Knowledge | View | View | Manage |
| Approvals, notifications, webhooks | View | View | Manage |
| Workspace settings, branding, members, BYOK | View every tab except BYOK | View every tab except BYOK | Manage |

\*Developers can't change incidents in Resolve. To let an on-call engineer work incidents in one workspace, make them workspace Admin there.

Who can approve an agent's action is set separately. See [approvers](/guide/approvals/approvers).

Some controls sit above workspace roles. Admin Settings, [usage](/guide/billing/usage), and [audit logs](/guide/security/audit-logs) are for organization Owners and Admins. [Billing](/guide/billing/subscription) and organization-wide [BYOK](/guide/byok) are Owner-only.

## Change a role or remove someone

Open **More → Workspace settings → Members**. The table shows **Member**, **Organization Role**, **Workspace Role**, and **Actions**.

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Change a role | Pick a new value in the **Workspace Role** dropdown |
| Remove someone | Click the remove icon in **Actions** and confirm **Remove Member** |

Organization Owners and Admins show a fixed role with no remove option, since their access comes from the organization. Removing someone from a workspace keeps them in the organization, so you can add them back later.

[Automations](/guide/automation/automations) they own in this workspace turn off. A workspace Admin clicks **Take ownership** on each one, then turns it back on. To remove someone from every workspace at once, remove them from the organization in **Admin Settings → Organization**.

## Add members automatically with SSO

If you use SSO with just-in-time provisioning or [SCIM](/guide/security/scim), new members can join workspaces as soon as they're created. Go to **Admin Settings → Identity and access** and set **Auto-assigned workspaces** on the **Provisioning & directory sync** card. New members join those workspaces with their **Auto-assigned organization role** (Viewer by default). See [SSO](/guide/security/sso#choose-how-users-are-provisioned) and [SCIM group mapping](/guide/security/scim#map-groups-to-workspaces).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The person I want isn't in the Add Member list">
    **Cause:** they're not in the organization yet, they're already in this workspace, or they're an organization Owner or Admin (who already has access).

    **Fix:** check the members table first. If they're missing from the organization, [invite them](/guide/organization#invite-members).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't change someone's workspace role">
    **Cause:** they're an organization Owner or Admin, or you aren't a workspace Admin.

    **Fix:** change their organization role in **Admin Settings → Organization**, or ask a workspace Admin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A Viewer can't type in the chat">
    **Cause:** Viewers are read-only and can't send chat messages.

    **Fix:** change their **Workspace Role** to **Developer** for this workspace, or change their organization role in **Admin Settings → Organization** to apply it everywhere.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add Member is missing">
    **Cause:** you aren't a workspace Admin.

    **Fix:** ask an Admin of this workspace, or an organization Owner or Admin, to add the person.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
