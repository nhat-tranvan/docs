Manage who has access to each workspace and what they can do. Workspace members are organization members who have been granted access to a specific workspace.

## What you can do

Workspace member management lets you:

- **Control access** — decide which organization members can see and use a workspace
- **Assign workspace roles** — set per-workspace roles that can differ from organization roles
- **Monitor membership** — view member count and quota usage per workspace

<Info>
Members must first belong to your organization before they can be added to a workspace. To invite someone to your organization, go to **Admin Settings → Organization**.
</Info>

## Adding members to a workspace

There are two ways to grant workspace access:

### From the workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace">
    Open the workspace you want to manage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Member">
    In the Members section, click **Add Member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an organization member">
    Pick a member from the dropdown — only Developers and Viewers are shown, because Owners and Admins already have implicit access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a workspace role">
    The role is pre-filled based on their organization role; change it if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### From admin settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open organization members">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Organization** and find the member in the Members section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open workspace access settings">
    Click the grid icon next to their name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set access and roles">
    Toggle access for each workspace and set a role per workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Organization Owners and Admins automatically have access to all workspaces — you don't need to add them manually.
</Info>

## Workspace roles

Each member can have a workspace-specific role that overrides their organization role for that workspace. This lets you give a Viewer organization-level member Developer access in a specific workspace, for example.

All three roles see every module in the sidebar — what differs is what each can change. Viewers are strictly read-only: they can open conversations, findings, and dashboards, but cannot send chat messages.

| Area | Viewer | Developer | Admin |
|------|--------|-----------|-------|
| Chat with agents | Read conversations | Chat, rename, and star conversations | Chat, plus delete conversations |
| Dashboards & artifacts | View | Create and edit | Create and edit |
| Optimize | View | Work findings — assign owners, move statuses | Everything, plus module settings |
| Review | View | Act on reviews — resolve findings | Everything, plus repository setup |
| Cyber | View | Create and run pentests | Everything, plus delete apps |
| Resolve | View | View | Manage and delete incidents |
| Connections & credentials | View | View | Add, edit, and remove |
| Agents & automations | View | Edit agents | Edit agents, plus schedules and automations |
| Knowledge bases | View | View | Manage |
| Webhooks & detection rules | View | View | Manage |
| Workspace settings & members | View | View | Manage |

Some surfaces sit above workspace roles entirely, at the organization level:

- **Admin Settings** — organization profile, members, identity and access, [audit logs](/guide/security/audit-logs) — is visible to organization Owners and Admins only.
- **[Usage](/guide/billing/usage) and [credit limits](/guide/billing/credit-limits)** are visible to organization Owners and Admins.
- **[Billing](/guide/billing/subscription) and [BYOK](/guide/byok)** are managed by the organization Owner only.

When managing workspace access from **Admin Settings → Organization** (via the grid icon), you can choose per workspace:
- **Inherit from Org** — uses their organization role
- **Admin / Developer / Viewer** — overrides with a workspace-specific role

## Managing workspace members

The workspace members table shows:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Member** | Avatar, display name, and email address |
| **Organization Role** | The member's role at the organization level |
| **Workspace Role** | The member's role in this specific workspace (editable) |
| **Actions** | Remove member from workspace |

### Changing a member's role

Click the workspace role dropdown next to a member to change their role. Only workspace admins and organization admins/owners can change roles. Implicit members (organization Owners and Admins) cannot have their workspace role changed.

### Removing a member

Remove a member from a workspace to revoke their access. This does not remove them from the organization — they can be re-added later.

<Warning>
Removing a member from the organization (via **Admin Settings → Organization**) automatically removes them from all workspaces.
</Warning>

## Provisioning and SSO

If your organization uses single sign-on (SSO) with just-in-time (JIT) provisioning — accounts created automatically at first SSO sign-in — or [SCIM](/guide/security/scim) directory sync, new members are automatically added to the **default workspaces** configured in your provisioning settings. See [SSO setup](/guide/security/sso) for details.
