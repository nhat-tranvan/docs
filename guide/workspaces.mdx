A workspace is a separate area inside your [organization](/guide/organization) with its own connections, agents, knowledge, conversations, and settings. Nothing in one workspace is visible from another, so a workspace is how you keep production apart from staging, or one team's tools apart from another's.

## What a workspace holds

Everything you set up while working lives in the current workspace:

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Access to your systems | [Connections](/guide/connections/overview) and [credentials](/guide/credentials) |
| How agents behave | [Custom agents](/guide/agents/customize), [skills](/guide/skills/overview), [knowledge](/guide/knowledge), [memory](/guide/memory), [commands](/guide/commands) |
| Safety controls | [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) mode and rules |
| Work and results | Conversations, [artifacts](/guide/artifacts/overview), [automations](/guide/automation/automations), and Optimize, Review, Cyber, and Resolve data |
| People | [Workspace members](/guide/workspace-users) and their workspace roles |

Billing, seats, SSO, and the audit log belong to the organization, not to a workspace.

## Choose a layout

Most teams start with one workspace and split when access or noise becomes a problem.

| Pattern | Split by | Pick it when |
|---|---|---|
| Environment | Production, Staging, Dev | Production needs stricter approvals and fewer people with write access |
| Team | Platform, Security, App team | Each team uses different connections, skills, and knowledge |
| Customer or business unit | One workspace per customer | You run operations for several clients and must keep their data apart |

Example: a platform team with 12 AWS accounts uses one workspace, adds one connection per account, and sets **Environment** (Production, Staging, or Development) on each connection. They split production into its own workspace only when it needs stricter Approvals or a separate credit limit.

Your plan sets how many workspaces you can create. See [plans and pricing](/guide/billing/pricing).

## Prerequisites

- You're an organization **Owner** or **Admin** to create or delete workspaces. Developers and Viewers can only work in workspaces they're assigned to.

## Create a workspace

Signup already created your first workspace. Create another only when you need separate access, Approvals, or data, as in [choose a layout](#choose-a-layout).

<Tip>
Trying CloudThinker in an organization that already has real work? Create an `Eval` workspace for the trial. When you're done, deleting it removes the trial's connections and conversations in one step.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new workspace">
    Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar, select the organization, and click **New workspace**. You can also go to **Admin Settings → Workspaces** and click **New workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    In the **Create Workspace in &lt;organization&gt;** dialog, enter a **Name** (required) and an optional **Description**. Use a name people will recognize in the switcher, such as `Production` or `Platform team`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**.

    **Success state:** the workspace appears as a card in **Admin Settings → Workspaces** and in the account menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your systems">
    A new workspace starts empty. Switch to it and add a [connection](/guide/connections/overview) so agents have something to work with. A card that shows **No cloud connected** still needs one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switch workspaces

Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar and pick a workspace from the list on the right. From **Admin Settings → Workspaces**, you can also click **Go to workspace** on a card and confirm the **Switch Workspace** prompt.

**Success state:** the page reloads and the sidebar shows the new workspace's name.

## Open workspace settings

Workspace settings open in a dialog with a menu of tabs. In the sidebar, click **More → Workspace settings**.

You can also open the same dialog from the **Chat Settings** icon in the chat box, or from the settings icon on the current workspace's card in **Admin Settings → Workspaces**.

| Group | Tabs |
|---|---|
| Workspace | **General**, **Members**, **Branding**, **BYOK** |
| Agent Configuration | **Connections**, **Skills**, **Memory**, **Agents**, **Knowledge**, **Credentials** |
| Workflow | **Artifacts**, **Commands**, **Approvals** |
| Integrations | **Notifications** |

You only see the tabs your role allows. For example, **BYOK** shows for workspace Admins only. Everyone can open **General**, but only workspace Admins can save changes. See [workspace roles](/guide/workspace-users#workspace-roles).

## Rename a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General">
    Open workspace settings and select **General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and save">
    Change **Name** or **Description**, then click **Save**.

    **Success state:** the new name shows in the sidebar and the account menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change the logo and colors used on reports, see [workspace branding](/guide/workspace-branding).

## Delete a workspace

<Warning>
Deleting a workspace removes it for every member, including its connections, conversations, and settings. Check with the workspace's members before you delete it.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch away from it">
    You can't delete the workspace you're in. Switch to another workspace first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    Go to **Admin Settings → Workspaces**, click **Delete** (the trash icon) on the workspace's card, and confirm in the **Delete Workspace** dialog.

    **Success state:** the card disappears from the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="New workspace is greyed out">
    **Cause:** you're a Developer or Viewer, or your organization has reached its plan's workspace limit. At the limit, **Admin Settings → Workspaces** shows **Workspace limit reached**.

    **Fix:** ask an Owner or Admin to create it, delete a workspace you no longer use, or [upgrade your plan](/guide/billing/subscription). Existing workspaces keep working at the limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The delete icon is locked">
    The tooltip gives the reason:

    - **Cannot delete the current workspace:** switch to another workspace first.
    - **This is the last workspace in the organization:** create another workspace before deleting this one.
    - **You need owner permission:** only organization Owners and Admins can delete workspaces.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A teammate can't see a workspace">
    **Cause:** they're a Developer or Viewer and haven't been added to it.

    **Fix:** add them from the workspace's **Members** tab. See [workspace members](/guide/workspace-users).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An agent can't find a connection I added">
    **Cause:** connections belong to one workspace. The connection was probably added in a different workspace.

    **Fix:** check the workspace name in the sidebar, then add the connection in the workspace where you're chatting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
