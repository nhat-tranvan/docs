A workspace is a separate area inside your [organization](/guide/organization) with its own connections, agents, memory, conversations, and settings. Agents and members in one workspace can't use another workspace's connections, data, or conversations, so a workspace is how you keep production apart from staging. Organization Owners and Admins can open every workspace, and usage and audit logs cover all of them.

## What a workspace holds

Everything you set up while working lives in the current workspace:

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Access to your systems | [Connections](/guide/connections/overview) and [credentials](/guide/credentials) |
| How agents behave | [Custom agents](/guide/agents/customize), [skills](/guide/skills/overview), [memory](/guide/memory) |
| Safety controls | [Approvals](/guide/approvals/overview) mode and rules |
| Work and results | Conversations, [artifacts](/guide/artifacts/overview), [automations](/guide/automation/automations), and Optimize, Review, Cyber, and Resolve data |
| People | [Workspace members](/guide/workspace-users) and their workspace roles |

Billing, membership, SSO, and the audit log belong to the organization, not to a workspace.

## Choose a layout

Most teams start with one workspace and split when access or noise becomes a problem.

| Pattern | Split by | Pick it when |
|---|---|---|
| Environment | Production, Staging, Dev | Production needs stricter approvals and fewer people with write access |
| Team | Platform, Security, App team | Each team uses different connections, skills, and memory |
| Customer or business unit | One workspace per customer | You run operations for several clients and must keep their data apart |

Example: a platform team with 12 AWS accounts uses one workspace, adds one connection per account, and sets **Environment** (Production, Staging, or Development) on each connection, so [approval rules](/guide/approvals/rules) can treat production changes more strictly. See [set the environment](/guide/connections/overview#set-the-environment). They split production into its own workspace only when it needs stricter Approvals or a separate credit limit.

Your plan sets how many workspaces you can create. See [plans and pricing](/guide/billing/pricing).

## Prerequisites

- You're an organization **Owner** or **Admin** to create or delete workspaces.
- You're a workspace **Admin** to rename a workspace or change its settings. Organization Owners and Admins always are. Workspace Admins can't delete a workspace from workspace settings.
- Developers and Viewers can only work in workspaces they're assigned to.

## Create a workspace

Signup already created your first workspace. Create another only when you need separate access, Approvals, or data, as in [choose a layout](#choose-a-layout).

<Tip>
Trying CloudThinker in an organization that already has real work? Create an `Eval` workspace for the trial. When you're done, deleting it removes the trial's connections and conversations in one step.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new workspace">
    Click the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar, then **New workspace** under your organization's workspaces. You can also go to **Settings → Organization → Workspaces** and click **New workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    In the **Create workspace in &lt;organization&gt;** dialog, enter a **Name** (required) and an optional **Description**. Use a name people will recognize in the switcher, such as `Production` or `Platform team`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**.

    **Success state:** the workspace appears in the list on **Settings → Organization → Workspaces** and in the workspace menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your systems">
    A new workspace starts empty. Switch to it and add a [connection](/guide/connections/overview) so agents have something to work with. A workspace row that shows **No cloud connected** still needs one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    Only organization Owners and Admins can see the new workspace at first. Add Developers and Viewers from its **Members** page. See [workspace members](/guide/workspace-users).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switch workspaces

Click the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar and pick a workspace from the list. Workspaces in your other organizations are under **Other organizations**. From **Settings → Organization → Workspaces**, you can also click **Go to workspace** on a row and confirm **Switch workspace**.

**Success state:** the page reloads and the sidebar shows the new workspace's name.

## Open workspace settings

Click the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar, then **Settings**. Settings opens on the **Workspace** tab. You can also press ⌘⇧, (Ctrl+Shift+, on Windows and Linux), or click **Settings** on the current workspace's row in **Settings → Organization → Workspaces**.

[Workspace settings](/guide/workspace-settings) lists every page, what you set there, and who sees it. See [workspace roles](/guide/workspace-users#workspace-roles) for what each role can change.

The **BYOK** page picks which LLM key this workspace runs on: the organization key, a workspace key, or none. This page is where you set a workspace key. The organization key itself is set by the Owner in **Settings → Organization → BYOK**, and the Owner can require it everywhere. See [organization and workspace keys](/guide/byok#organization-and-workspace-keys).

## Rename a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General">
    Go to **Settings → Workspace → General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and save">
    Click **Edit** next to the workspace name. In the **Edit workspace** dialog, change **Name** or **Description**, then click **Save**.

    **Success state:** the new name shows in the sidebar and the workspace menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change the logo and colors used on reports, see [workspace branding](/guide/workspace-branding).

## Turn capabilities on or off

**Settings → Workspace → Capabilities** lists Advisor and Blind Spot. When they're available for your organization, both start on in every workspace and use the workspace's credits. A workspace Admin turns each one off or back on with its switch, unless the organization Owner has set it for every workspace. See [Capabilities](/guide/capabilities).

## Delete a workspace

<Warning>
Deleting a workspace removes it for every member, including its connections, conversations, and settings. There's no restore button in the app. Check with the workspace's members before you delete it.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch away from it">
    You can't delete the workspace you're in. Switch to another workspace first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    Go to **Settings → Organization → Workspaces**, click **Delete** (the trash icon) on the workspace's row, and confirm in the **Delete Workspace** dialog. You don't type the name, so check the row first.

    **Success state:** the row disappears from the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What else happens:

| Item | What happens |
|---|---|
| Scheduled resource discovery | Turned off. The workspace's creator gets a notification |
| Workspace count | Drops by one, freeing room under your plan's limit |
| Audit log | The deletion is recorded with the workspace name in [audit logs](/guide/security/audit-logs) |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="New workspace is greyed out">
    **Cause:** you're a Developer or Viewer, or your organization has reached its plan's workspace limit. At the limit, **Settings → Organization → Workspaces** shows **Workspace limit reached**.

    **Fix:** ask an Owner or Admin to create it, delete a workspace you no longer use, or [upgrade your plan](/guide/billing/subscription). Existing workspaces keep working at the limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The delete icon is missing">
    The row hides the trash icon when the workspace can't be deleted:

    - It's the workspace you're in. Switch to another workspace first.
    - It's the last workspace in the organization. Create another workspace before deleting this one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A teammate can't see a workspace">
    **Cause:** they're a Developer or Viewer and haven't been added to it.

    **Fix:** add them from the workspace's **Members** page. See [workspace members](/guide/workspace-users).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An agent can't find a connection I added">
    **Cause:** connections belong to one workspace. The connection was probably added in a different workspace.

    **Fix:** check the workspace name in the sidebar, then add the connection in the workspace where you're chatting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
