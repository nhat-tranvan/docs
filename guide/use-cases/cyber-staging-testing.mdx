Before a release, you want to know whether one customer can read another customer's orders in staging. This walkthrough runs a Cyber pentest limited to the shop and order paths, with two test users, and follows one proven issue to a verified fix.

## The scenario

Your team ships a new order history page next week. Staging runs at `https://staging.shop.example.com`. The payments and admin areas belong to another team and must stay out of the test. You have two test accounts, one in each tenant, and you want proof for every issue, not a list of guesses.

## Prerequisites

- A staging deployment you're authorized to test, and access to its DNS records to verify the domain.
- Workspace admin rights, or an admin who can verify the domain for you.
- Two test accounts in the app, in different tenants.
- A [Git provider connection](/guide/connections/github) with the app's repository, if you want CloudThinker to draft the fix.

The [Cyber quickstart](/guide/security/cyber-quickstart) covers each setup screen in more detail.

## Walkthrough

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register the app and verify the domain">
    Click **Cyber** in the sidebar, open **Pentest apps**, and click **Register an app**. On **Target**, enter the **App URL or host**, an **App name**, and **Web application** under **What to test**. Under **Target ownership**, click **Create DNS proof**, add the TXT record at your DNS provider, and click **Check again**.

    **What you should see:** **Target ownership** shows **Verified**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add both test users">
    On **Sign-in**, type a name and click **Add authentication**, once for each account. Give each a clear **Identity label** and an **Identity description** that names its tenant, for example `customer-a` in Tenant A and `customer-b` in Tenant B. With two users, Cyber can test whether one can reach the other's data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Safe and save without running">
    On **Scope and intensity**, select **Safe** under **Default intensity**, which sends only read requests, and click **Continue**. On **Review**, click **Run later**, because setup sets no include or exclude paths and **Register app** would start a run against the whole target.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the scope">
    In the app header, click **Settings**, then **Edit** on the **Scope** row to open **Manage scope**:

    | List | Rules |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Include paths** | `/shop/**`, `/orders/**`, `/api/orders/**` |
    | **Exclude paths** | `/admin/**`, `/api/payments/**` |

    Click **Save scope**. A path that matches both lists is excluded.

    **What you should see:** **Scope saved**, and the **Scope** row lists your include paths instead of reading **Whole target**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the pentest">
    Click **Run pentest** in the app header, check that **Safe** is selected, and click **Run Safe pentest**. The run uses credits. You can leave the page: you get a notification when it ends, and the app's **Runs** tab lists it.

    **What you should see:** a run marked **Running**, with a live timeline of what Cyber is testing and a **Results** pane beside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the proof">
    When the run shows **Completed**, open the app's **Issues** tab and select **Confirmed**: verified issues that Cyber reproduced. Open one, for example `customer-b` reading `customer-a`'s order through `/api/orders/{id}`. Under **HTTP captures**, you see the exact request and response that prove it, with secrets redacted.

    To ask about a lead under **Needs verification**, open it and click **Ask Cyber**:

    ```text
    Which request and identity would prove this lead? Is the same check missing on the other /api/orders endpoints in scope?
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix and retest">
    On the confirmed issue, click **Open PR**. The issue moves to **In progress**, and a fix chat opens. Say what you want:

    ```text
    Add the tenant ownership check to every /api/orders handler, with a test that customer-b gets 404 for customer-a's order.
    ```

    <ApprovalDefault />

    Review and test the pull request, merge it, and deploy to staging. Then click **Awaiting retest** on the issue's stepper and run the pentest again.

    **What you should see:** if the proof no longer works, the issue moves to **Verified fixed**. If it still works, the issue stays open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What made this work

- **Ownership came first.** Cyber tests only a domain you've proved you control. See [Run a security test](/guide/security/security-testing).
- **Scope kept other teams safe.** Include and exclude paths kept payments and admin out of the run.
- **Two identities found the real bug.** A cross-tenant leak only shows when Cyber can sign in as two customers.
- **Every issue came with proof.** The request and response let you replay the request yourself. See [Triage issues](/guide/security/cyber-findings).
- **A retest closed the loop.** The next run, not a checkbox, moved the issue to **Verified fixed**.
